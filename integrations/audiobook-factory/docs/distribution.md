# Where an AI-narrated audiobook can actually go

Verified 2026-08-19. Re-verify before promising any platform in a contract.

This is the constraint that shapes the whole engagement, so settle it before
quoting: the narration engine is a solved problem, the distribution right is not.

## The short version

**ACX will not take an AI-narrated file you produced elsewhere.** ACX's
submission requirements call for human narration unless otherwise authorised,
and explicitly list unauthorised text-to-speech, AI, and automated recordings
as prohibited. Uploading a file made with an outside AI tool and presenting it
as a human recording violates those terms.

**That is not the same as "AI audiobooks cannot reach Audible."** Audible is the
storefront; ACX is one route into it. Audible sells titles labelled
"Narrator: Virtual Voice", produced through Amazon's own KDP conversion of an
eligible Kindle ebook. And several aggregators accept AI narration with
disclosure and distribute into retail catalogues, including Audible's, through
different commercial paths.

So the honest answer to a client is: yes, with a named route, and the route
changes what we build.

## Routes, ranked for this client

A course catalogue is not a trade book. For Global Leader Solutions the
retail-audiobook path is probably not even the best one.

| Route | Takes AI narration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Course platform / LMS companion audio** | Yes - it is their own property | No gatekeeper. Fastest. Raises course completion. Use the `lms` profile. |
| **Private podcast feed** (member-only RSS) | Yes - their own feed | Students listen in the app they already use. Use the `podcast` profile. Generated by `afx` as `feed.xml`. |
| **Direct sale from their own site** | Yes | Highest margin for the client. No platform terms to satisfy. |
| **Aggregators** (Spotify/Findaway, Author's Republic, Kobo Writing Life) | Generally yes, with disclosure | The realistic retail path. Disclosure of synthetic narration is normally required. |
| **KDP Virtual Voice** | Amazon's own voices only | Requires an eligible live Kindle ebook, English, no existing audiobook. Not our audio. |
| **ACX direct** | No, not for externally produced AI audio | Only with human narration, or separate written authorisation. |

## What this means for the build

1. **Default to `lms` and `podcast` profiles.** They carry no eligibility
   question and cover the client's own platform, their member area, and a
   private feed. That is where course audio earns its money anyway.
2. **The `acx` profile is a technical spec, not a permission.** It exists so a
   master meets the loudness, peak, and noise-floor requirements. Meeting the
   spec does not make an AI-narrated file eligible for ACX.
3. **Disclose synthetic narration.** Put it in the closing credits and in the
   platform metadata. The generated credits block already says so. Aggregators
   generally require it, and it costs nothing with a business audience.
4. **If the client wants Audible retail specifically**, the two clean options
   are a human narrator for those titles, or KDP Virtual Voice off an existing
   Kindle ebook. Both are decisions for the client, not for us to assume.

## Rights to settle in writing before production

- Who owns the synthetic voice model if the author's voice is cloned, and what
  happens to it when the engagement ends.
- Written consent from Dr. Campbell for voice cloning, naming the permitted
  uses.
- Whether the engine's licence permits commercial distribution at the plan the
  client is on. On ElevenLabs, commercial rights depend on the tier.
- Disclosure language the client is comfortable with.
