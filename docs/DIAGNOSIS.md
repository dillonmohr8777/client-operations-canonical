# Why the old workflow felt impossible

## Bottom line

Dillon was not failing at prompts. The prior setup made him serve as the missing operating system.

Every new task forced him to remember which client, project, account, artifact, commitment, approval, voice rule, design preference, and prior decision applied. Adding more agents increased output without creating one authoritative state or acceptance loop. The anxiety was a rational response to fragmented coordination.

The full evidence audit is preserved at `C:\Users\dillo\Documents\Codex\2026-07-15\dissect-my-entire-life-that-you\DILLON_MARKETING_OS_AUDIT.md`.

## Root causes

1. Dated conversations acted like projects, so business identity and current outcomes fragmented.
2. Several partial control centers existed, but none was made the sole authority.
3. Background automations created task clutter or prepared weakly routed work faster than Dillon could trust it.
4. Prediction was a manually written sentence, not a scored queue with feedback.
5. Voice was mainly adjectives and punctuation rules, not channel-specific examples and edit deltas.
6. Design feedback was trapped in individual tasks instead of client references, anti-references, and rendered QA.
7. Client context and access routes were incomplete, so each task rediscovered facts and credentials.

## What changed

- One canonical project: `client-operations`.
- One pinned user-facing Marketing Chief task.
- One exact client registry, versioned queue, deterministic control view, redacted correction ledger, and live system-health surface.
- Background Gmail and Slack mechanisms are prepare-only sensors feeding redacted, exact-routed, deduplicated intake. They do not create Codex tasks, notify Dillon, or write the queue.
- One next-action ranker separates automatic work, Dillon decisions, and blocked unblocks.
- One bounded worker-handoff contract with stale-write rejection, artifact-boundary checks, secret and PII rejection, WIP limits, rollback backups, and Chief-only reconciliation.
- Channel voice and rendered design standards now exist, with feedback ledgers designed to improve from accepted edits.
- Bitwarden and Access Broker provide the durable non-secret access route. Raw secrets never enter project state or memory.

## The daily interaction

Dillon opens the pinned Marketing Chief task and says what changed or simply says `continue`.

The Chief refreshes live health and intake, loads the exact client context and corrections, ranks the queue, executes the highest safe local action, verifies the result, reconciles canonical state, and returns the finished work plus at most one decision that only Dillon can make.

## What cannot be automated away

- MFA, one-time codes, CAPTCHA, passkeys, recovery, new consent, and master-password entry.
- A business decision whose answer is not evidenced.
- Explicit approval to send, publish, deploy, purchase, change spend, change an account, or take a destructive action.
- Taste calibration before enough accepted and rejected examples exist.

Those are intentional human gates. Repeating client routes, project locations, routine safe steps, and already stated operating preferences is not.
