## Reusable client production method

1. Resolve exact client, audience, offer, conversion action, approved assets and commercial claims.
2. Research competitor ads and pages for message hypotheses. Ad longevity and public engagement are proxies, not conversion evidence.
3. Write one pain, one promise, one proof point and one CTA. Draft short script and shot list before spending on generation.
4. Capture real product/service proof. Use generated imagery only where it accurately represents the offer; preserve official identity separately. Never synthesize testimonials.
5. Assemble one editable master; check captions, voice/music rights, pacing, mobile readability and every factual claim.
6. Test against a control with tagged destination and validated qualified-outcome reporting. Record results and reuse the winning message only when evidence supports it.
