04
Growth system

Five connected workstreams. One patient journey.

1

Establish the source of truth

Connect Search Console, GA4, Tag Manager, Business Profile, the form or Boulevard path, and privacy-safe outcome stages. Record exact owners and baselines.

2

Clarify page ownership

Map priority services and locations so each valuable query has one best page. Protect strong pages, merge overlap, rewrite weak titles and headings, and simplify internal links.

3

Publish physician-reviewed answers

Turn real consultation questions into concise answer sections, FAQs, comparisons, and new pages. Attach author, reviewer, revision date, source links, and valid schema where appropriate.

4

Earn independent corroboration

Align the practice and physician profiles across trusted directories and professional sources. Pursue fewer, stronger mentions that validate the same facts.

5

Convert and learn

Launch the first paid path only after measurement passes. Review search terms, pages, Maps, AI answers, and consultation quality together every week.

The monthly content rule

Two new pages should fill proven gaps, not feed a calendar. Each page needs a defined query set, patient question, physician reviewer, consultation path, internal-link role, and measurement plan before drafting begins.

05
Paid search

Build Google Ads after the measurement path is credible.

A clean paid launch is a controlled extension of the website strategy, not a shortcut around it.

Account structure

Use a GT Clinic-owned Google Ads account with GT Clinic billing. Momentum receives management access and documents each material change.

First campaign

Begin with one priority service and one local consultation intent. Keep brand protection, service intent, and exploratory queries separate.

Landing path

Send traffic to one focused page with physician authority, service fit, clear expectations, and one consultation action. Preserve GCLID and UTMs.

Tracking gate

Verify form or scheduling completion, deduplication, consent, referral exclusions, call handling, and source capture before enabling spend.

Optimization signal

Use privacy-safe consultation milestones and clinic feedback about fit. Do not send symptoms, treatment details, or patient information to advertising or AI tools.

Launch conditions

Priority service chosen · landing page approved · conversion path verified · monthly media budget confirmed in writing · account ownership and billing confirmed · campaign review completed.

90-day plan

A sequence that closes the foundation before it adds volume.

1
Days 1–14

Instrument and stabilize

  • Confirm owners, goals, top services, and approvals.
  • Connect GSC, GA4, GTM, GBP, Ads, and outcomes.
  • Back up site and document rollback.
  • Rerun crawl, mobile, schema, and index checks.
  • Audit contact form and Boulevard decision.
  • Freeze ranking, Maps, and AI prompt baselines.
2
Days 15–30

Correct high-intent paths

  • Resolve page ownership for priority services.
  • Protect and improve proven local pages.
  • Review high-risk medical and competitor claims.
  • Repair titles, headings, internal links, and schema.
  • Begin NAP and profile corrections.
  • Approve the first paid landing brief.
3
Days 31–60

Build authority and the first paid path

  • Publish two reviewed pages against proven gaps.
  • Strengthen physician and practice entities.
  • Complete first citation and profile wave.
  • Launch the focused landing page.
  • Validate conversions end to end.
  • Launch Google Ads only after the gate passes.
4
Days 61–90

Compound what earns trust

  • Expand pages gaining impressions or citations.
  • Improve queries sitting near page one.
  • Review search terms and consultation quality.
  • Retest Maps and AI answer visibility.
  • Reclaim mentions and strengthen trusted sources.
  • Set the next-quarter test portfolio.

Timing assumes timely access, clinic review of medical content, and approvals. Search movement is measured and reported; specific ranking or lead outcomes are not guaranteed.
