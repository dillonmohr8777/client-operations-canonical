What is already covered

Search results surface the homepage, FAQ, contact, Cherry Hill, Moorestown, and Voorhees location pages, plus pages for hyperpigmentation, scars, microneedling, laser vein removal, hair restoration, and the two GT Restore intimate wellness services. The homepage also presents aesthetic and functional medicine, with a physician-led consultation path.

Retrieval limitation

SiteGround powCaptcha returned HTTP 202 for every direct page, sitemap, robots, and WordPress API request. This blocks a fresh source-level crawl and means current H1s, canonicals, meta descriptions, schema, internal paths, duplicate content, page count, and performance are unverified. It does not prove Google or Bing access is blocked.

A dated 21 August six-template sample found canonicals, descriptions, and JSON-LD on each sampled template, with homepage H1 count zero and blog H1 count two. Revalidation is required before calling those current defects.

Priority findings

  1. Verify robots, sitemap, WAF policy, indexed URLs, and real crawler access in Search Console and host logs.
  2. Confirm contact-form completion, referral source capture, service interest, and confirmation behavior before Ads or conversion reporting.
  3. Choose one clinic-approved functional medicine priority for a dedicated physician-reviewed decision page.
  4. Confirm one canonical destination for Botox/Xeomin, fillers, laser hair removal, biostimulators, and GT Restore Lift.
  5. Replace the current Voorhees slug-style result title with a natural, supported local title.
  6. Make nearby location pages materially distinct, then remove any unsupported nearest or closest claim.
