# Design System: BigOrange Homepage Trio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Work, Made Visible"**

BigOrange looks seasoned, direct, and alive because the design exposes the thinking behind the work. Orange is the active signal, ink supplies authority, warm paper keeps the system human, and the exact approved logo anchors every expression. Unbounded carries decisive ideas; Manrope carries useful explanation. Copy stays practical, lightly playful, and human reviewed, with no invented proof or generic agency theater.

The shared system has three explicit expression modes. **Citrus Foundry** is cinematic and industrial: drawing paper meets dark worksite atmosphere, orange behaves like work light, rounded glass surfaces provide selective lift, and square particles assemble into the mark. **Blueprint Frequency** is technical and inspectable: warm drafting paper, fine rules, contour geometry, translucent orange structures, and scan or waveform motion show a connected system. **Orange Press** is tactile and editorial: press orange, black ink, warm stock, torn or angled crops, builder-specific editorial photography, square controls, pop-open press notes, and oversized spreads make the process feel published rather than polished away.

These are coordinated voices, not interchangeable costumes. New work selects one mode and follows its material and motion grammar while preserving the shared identity, accessibility behavior, evidence discipline, and useful non-sales action. Surface-specific hierarchy, proof sequence, and composition remain in the corresponding surface brief.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Orange, ink, and warm paper form the shared identity.
- Unbounded display type and Manrope body type stay exact across all modes.
- Each expression uses a distinct material, edge, imagery, and motion grammar.
- The approved BigOrange logo resolves clearly and never becomes decorative filler.
- Motion reveals structure, then yields to a complete static reduced-motion state.
- Voice is warm, direct, seasoned, practical, lightly playful, and human reviewed.

**The One Identity, Three Expressions Rule.** Preserve the shared primitives, then commit each surface to Citrus Foundry, Blueprint Frequency, or Orange Press without blending their signature treatments.

## Colors

The palette is a compact family of signal oranges, near-black inks, warm drawing papers, and restrained graphite text; expression comes from how those roles are combined, not from adding unrelated accent hues.

### Primary

- **BigOrange Signal:** The shared brand orange for identity, active markers, calls to action, and controlled emphasis in Blueprint Frequency and Orange Press.
- **Foundry Work Light:** The brighter orange used when Citrus Foundry needs emitted light, particles, or a hot industrial signal.

### Secondary

- **Blueprint Deep Orange:** A grounded action color that keeps orange buttons legible on warm drafting paper.
- **Foundry Burnt Orange:** The darker foundry state for hover, heat, and stronger contrast.
- **Press Flash Orange:** A vivid print accent reserved for the most saturated Orange Press moments.

### Neutral

- **Workshop Ink:** The shared charcoal foundation for text and cinematic dark surfaces.
- **Print Ink:** The denser black used by Orange Press for masthead actions, editorial sections, and halftone marks.
- **Drafting Ink:** The crisp technical black used by Blueprint Frequency for rules, diagrams, and selected states.
- **Drafting Paper:** The warm neutral ground of Blueprint Frequency.
- **Foundry Drawing Paper:** The slightly grayer paper that bridges Citrus Foundry's light and night halves.
- **Press Stock:** The warmer tactile paper used by Orange Press.
- **Warm White:** The cleanest paper and reversed-text tone.
- **Muted Graphite:** Supporting copy, captions, and low-priority annotation.

**The Orange Means Work Rule.** Use orange to identify action, active state, construction, measurement, or publication energy; do not scatter it as ambient decoration.

**The Paper Is Warm Rule.** Keep light surfaces visibly warmer than pure interface gray so technical and editorial structures still feel human.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Unbounded (with Raleway, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks)  
**Body Font:** Manrope (with Open Sans, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks)

**Character:** Unbounded gives BigOrange an unmistakable, forward-driving voice without borrowing a generic agency grotesk. Manrope keeps explanations, labels, and process language plainspoken and easy to scan.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** Heavy, tightly tracked, compact-line headlines for hero promises and major section turns; keep the measure near 9–12 characters where the implemented composition calls for it.
- **Headline:** Strong section and panel statements that remain visibly subordinate to the hero.
- **Title:** Process steps, card names, questions, and compact editorial declarations.
- **Body:** Comfortable explanatory copy with generous leading and a typical measure under 42rem.
- **Label:** Small, high-weight navigation, captions, system labels, and uppercase metadata; use uppercase only when the expression treats the text as annotation.

**The Exact Pairing Rule.** Unbounded owns proposition and structure; Manrope owns explanation and interaction. Do not substitute a decorative serif, condensed poster face, or generic system font.

**The Press Type Safety Rule.** Orange Press may loosen Unbounded leading to 0.96 through 1.0 and lower its compact scale before any glyph or whole word is clipped. Preserve the display face, balanced editorial breaks, and zero document overflow from 320px upward.

**The Press Type Ramp Rule.** Orange Press uses the documented microprint ladder and fluid display endpoints in the portable token layer. Reuse those exact steps for captions, press notes, cards, and responsive headings instead of introducing nearby one-off sizes.

## Layout

The system is full bleed and grid led. Desktop surfaces repeatedly pair unequal two-column compositions with wide media, then use ruled three-part or five-part bands to expose process. Horizontal gutters remain fluid from a compact mobile inset to generous desktop margins; major sections use a six-to-ten-rem vertical rhythm, with Citrus Foundry allowed to extend cinematic sections farther.

At the observed wide breakpoint, multi-column systems, process lists, and split image-copy sections collapse to a single column. At the compact breakpoint, display type tightens, image stages shorten, navigation becomes a keyboard-operable disclosure, and all content respects the 390px reading width. Sticky headings become static when the single-column reading order takes over.

**Citrus Foundry** favors paper-to-night seams, conveyor-like bands, immersive photography, and selectively rounded glass. **Blueprint Frequency** favors drawing grids, measured rules, contour fields, inspectable tabs, and balanced technical splits. **Orange Press** favors editorial spreads, hard gutters, numbered columns, asymmetric crops, and torn or angled image edges. The surface briefs own the exact sequence and composition.

**The Reading Order Rule.** Responsive collapse must preserve the meaning and action order in the HTML; visual spectacle never reorders the decision path.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat by default and uses imagery, tonal changes, borders, grids, clipping, and overlap to create depth. Citrus Foundry alone permits a concentrated atmospheric lift in its frosted navigation and primary action. Blueprint Frequency remains diagrammatic, while Orange Press uses paper layering and crop geometry rather than interface shadows.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Foundry Navigation Lift:** A broad dark ambient shadow used only beneath the translucent fixed header.
- **Foundry Action Glow:** A warm, diffused orange shadow used only to make the primary action feel like a work light.

**The Flat Foundation Rule.** Keep resting content surfaces flat; depth should explain material or hierarchy, not imitate a generic card stack.

## Shapes

Shape changes with the expression while the system stays disciplined. Ten-pixel controls and fourteen-pixel media or glass surfaces belong to Foundry and Blueprint. Eight-pixel caption plates stay compact and functional. Orange Press uses square buttons, ruled columns, clipped paper, and hard editorial edges. Circles appear as process points, scan contours, print dots, or measurement rings, never as a default container shape.

**The Edge Declares the Mode Rule.** Rounded glass signals Foundry, measured radii signal Blueprint, and square or torn edges signal Press; do not mix these silhouettes inside one surface without a functional reason.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Foundry and Blueprint actions use compact rounded controls; Orange Press uses square editorial controls.
- **Primary:** Orange or deep orange carries the practical call to action, with ink or warm white selected for contrast.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover may darken and lift by three pixels in Foundry or Blueprint. Every action uses a visible three-pixel focus outline with generous offset and cannot depend on motion for recognition.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Text links use a restrained underline motion. Blueprint navigation actions may use a one-pixel outline. Press secondary actions remain hard-edged and high contrast.

### Tabs

- **Style:** The Blueprint Frequency control is a ruled five-part band with Unbounded labels.
- **State:** Unselected tabs remain paper or warm white; the selected tab reverses to drafting ink with a small orange status point.
- **Keyboard:** Arrow keys, Home, and End move selection; focus remains visible.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** Industry and process items are normally flat ruled columns. Foundry media and Blueprint atlas frames may use the shared surface radius.
- **Background:** Alternate paper, warm white, ink, and orange in large semantic fields.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Refer to the flat foundation rule; content cards do not float.
- **Border:** One-pixel graphite or translucent white rules make structure inspectable.
- **Internal Padding:** Compact rows use the small-to-large spacing scale; major cards may extend to section rhythm.

### Navigation

- **Style:** The exact logo stays left anchored and the practical call stays visually distinct. Foundry uses a fixed translucent dark bar, Blueprint a sticky warm-white drafting bar, and Press a thin editorial masthead.
- **States:** Text links use underline, inversion, or ink fill according to mode. Mobile navigation opens beneath the header, closes after selection, and returns focus when Escape dismisses it.

### Media Captions

- **Style:** Small Manrope labels sit on an ink-backed or warm-paper plate at a media edge.
- **Purpose:** Captions explain the working decision, proof role, or builder context visible in the image. Orange Press uses only unmistakably home-building imagery and never exposes internal design-review labels on the rendered page.

### Press Notes

- **Style:** Hard-edged orange boxes sit directly on the Industry Solutions photography and carry the exact buyer-fit questions.
- **Behavior:** The connected-system and Industry Solutions rails center one complete card at a time. The note for the active scene clips and snaps open from its top edge, its label, headline, and explanation resolve in sequence, and hover adds a distinct lifted press state. Mouse drag, touch swipe, arrows, Home, End, and keyboard focus update the same active state. Without JavaScript or with reduced motion, every note remains fully visible and static.

### FAQ Disclosures

- **Style:** Full-width ruled rows use Unbounded questions, Manrope answers, and a plus-to-minus marker.
- **Behavior:** Only one answer remains open at a time, and the summary is keyboard operable with a visible focus ring.

### Logo Reveal

The exact approved logo is the resolved endpoint. Foundry assembles it from orange square particles and Blueprint scans it through contours and points. Orange Press reserves particle motion for one dedicated bottom stage: the proven `bigorange-logo-particle-8777.png` mask supplies the exact target geometry, three clean elliptical paths establish a recognizable atom, orange square particles orbit and collapse inward to assemble the full mark, then the canvas disappears completely as the exact static logo resolves. The hero and content sections never carry particle marks. When reduced motion is requested or canvas is unavailable, show the complete static logo immediately.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the exact approved BigOrange logo, orange-and-ink identity, Unbounded and Manrope pairing, and the primary action wording "Schedule a Non-Sales Call."
- **Do** choose one expression mode and carry its material, edge, image, and motion grammar through the surface.
- **Do** keep factual claims grounded in approved source material; Orange Press uses the canonical builder-page copy and unmistakably home-building imagery without internal review labels.
- **Do** preserve semantic reading order, keyboard operation, visible focus, 390px overflow safety, and a complete static reduced-motion fallback.
- **Do** write in a warm, direct, seasoned, practical voice with light playfulness and human-reviewed judgment.

### Don't:

- **Don't** blend Foundry glow, Blueprint contours, and Press halftone or torn-paper treatments into one undisciplined costume.
- **Don't** invent client results, testimonials, pricing, named expertise, or proof to fill a visual slot.
- **Don't** substitute generic service-card grids, stock agency gradients, decorative icon clouds, or unexplained accent colors for the chosen expression.
- **Don't** make particles, scans, waves, or print effects the only way to recognize the logo, state, action, or content.
- **Don't** place the Orange Press particle logo over hero copy, photography, calls to action, or any other content section.
- **Don't** let oversized display type create clipped words, horizontal overflow, or a broken mobile reading order.
