# BigOrange Homepage Trio

## Platform

web

## Users

- Owners and marketing leaders at custom home builders who need clearer positioning, credible proof, useful answers, and a low-pressure next step.
- BigOrange marketers reviewing how the authority-hub system can extend into stronger homepage, content-marketing, and industry-solution expressions.

## Product Purpose

Create three materially different, noindex review builds from the existing BigOrange custom-home-builder authority-hub truth. Each iteration must preserve the exact BigOrange identity, the non-sales call, source-safe copy, and editable web semantics while testing a different visual and motion thesis.

## Positioning

BigOrange combines practical positioning, content and inbound marketing, WordPress execution, technical search work, AI-search readiness, and a human-reviewed expert workflow. Tools accelerate the work. People own facts, judgment, voice, and publication.

## Constraints

- Preserve the intended production canonical: `https://bigorange.marketing/marketing-agency-for-builders/`.
- Keep all review deployments noindex and nofollow.
- Do not invent results, client proof, testimonials, pricing, or Janice-attributed expertise.
- Generated visuals are builder-specific editorial imagery and are never presented as client proof.
- Orange Press uses seven unique content images: one hero, five Industry Solutions scenes, and one finished-home handoff. No content-image hash may repeat within Orange Press or across the trio.
- The exact BigOrange logo is the only intentional repeated image family.
- Every site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, mobile reading order, and 390px overflow safety.
