# BigOrange Custom Home Builder Authority Hub

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: owners and marketing leaders at custom home builders who need a credible, measurable way to attract and qualify right-fit homeowners.
- Secondary: BigOrange marketers who must edit, review, approve, and reuse the authority-hub system in WordPress without depending on a developer for routine changes.
- Internal evaluator: BigOrange leadership, reviewing whether one human expert interview can become a repeatable, evidence-led content engine.

## Product Purpose

Evolve BigOrange's existing `/marketing-agency-for-builders/` page into the central custom-home-builder authority hub. The hub must preserve the current page's service and conversion role while adding decision-grade guidance, proof, topic-cluster navigation, FAQs, structured data, and clear paths into supporting content and a non-sales call.

Success means the page helps the right builder understand BigOrange's expertise, find a useful next step, and move into a qualified conversation while giving BigOrange a repeatable production system for future industries.

## Positioning

BigOrange combines StoryBrand clarity, WordPress execution, technical SEO, AI-search readiness, and a human-reviewed subject-matter-expert workflow. AI organizes and accelerates the work; named experts and marketers remain responsible for facts, voice, approval, and publication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing URL and its current search equity; do not create a competing pillar URL.
- Deliver one pillar page, two complete supporting articles, FAQs, internal-link architecture, metadata, schema, technical SEO guidance, AI-search guidance, a 90-day roadmap, a 12-month roadmap, workflow documentation, and a final presentation.
- The current WordPress page already ranks strongly for several commercial home-builder-agency queries. Expansion must protect those mappings while improving coverage for the approved custom-home-builder topic.
- The August 17 Moz export supplements the August 4 Semrush decision map; the two sources measure different things and must not be conflated.
- Janice-dependent stories, quotes, examples, and claims remain clearly marked for interview and SME approval. They must not be invented or published as final.
- Publishing, production configuration changes, and external delivery remain approval-gated. Local and draft-stage production can proceed.
- The surface mode is Persuade: explain the offer quickly, establish trust with real proof, demonstrate the authority-hub mechanism, and make the non-sales call the primary action.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the verified BigOrange name and logo assets.
- Preserve BigOrange's incumbent orange, charcoal, white, home photography, Raleway display type, and Open Sans body type unless a future approved brand-system change supersedes them.
- Voice: warm, direct, seasoned, practical, lightly playful, customer-first, and specific. Use citrus language sparingly.
- Calls to action should feel helpful and low pressure, especially `Schedule a Non-Sales Call`.

## Product Principles

1. Protect existing authority before adding new surface area.
2. Make the buyer's decision easier before explaining the system behind it.
3. Show proof and process; do not substitute generic agency claims.
4. Let AI accelerate structure and reuse while humans own truth and approval.
5. Keep daily editing simple, reversible, and documented for BigOrange marketers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, semantics, reduced motion, responsive reading order, descriptive links, and meaningful image alternatives. Builder audiences may review the page on job sites or phones, so mobile legibility and performance are first-class requirements.
