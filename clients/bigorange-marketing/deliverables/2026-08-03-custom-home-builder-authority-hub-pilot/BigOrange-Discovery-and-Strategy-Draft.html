Technical and AI-search work order

Fix the critical path before adding more weight.

26mobile Lighthouse performance
28.6smobile LCP lab snapshot
2,650msmobile TBT lab proxy
67desktop performance
  1. Find the real mobile LCP element and request chain. Do not guess from the score alone.
  2. Remove hero video, carousel, or heavy background media from the critical path. Use a lightweight poster first.
  3. Cut unused page-builder CSS and long JavaScript tasks. Audit sliders, animation, chat, tracking duplication, and third parties.
  4. Improve accessibility and information structure. Preserve visible focus, semantic headings, descriptive alt text, contrast, keyboard access, and reduced motion.
  5. Make the page easy to retrieve and cite. Use visible direct answers, stable entity details, named authorship, review dates, internal links, and structured data that matches the page.
  6. Re-test before release. Validate mobile, desktop, forms, metadata, schema, crawl access, and rollback evidence in staging.
AI-search boundaryThere is no magic tag or guaranteed answer placement. Crawl access, indexability, clear answers, reputable citations, consistent entities, and real proof are controllable inputs.

Editorial workflow

One expert hour becomes a source, not a content vending machine.

AI can organize, compare, outline, and repurpose. It cannot supply BigOrange's experience, client proof, or final judgment.

1. FrameDefine the reader, decision, source boundary, and claim exclusions.
2. InterviewCapture decisions, mistakes, objections, language, examples, and caveats from Janice or the assigned SME.
3. ExtractBuild a claim table, FAQ candidates, article angles, proof gaps, and internal-link opportunities.
4. VerifySeparate firsthand expertise, approved client evidence, official guidance, and statements that still need a source.
5. DraftCreate answer-first copy with explicit placeholders instead of invented expertise.
6. ReviewSME truth check, editorial quality check, accessibility and technical QA, approval, then publication.
Quality floorDirect answer, original expertise, source quality, audience specificity, internal-link value, scanability, accessibility, conversion clarity, fact-check completion, and human approval.
