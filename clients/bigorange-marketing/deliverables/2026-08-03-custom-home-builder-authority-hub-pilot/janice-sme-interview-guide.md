# Janice SME Interview Guide

Purpose: capture BigOrange's firsthand builder-marketing expertise and replace provisional language in the pillar and supporting content with reviewed, attributable insight.

Duration: 60 minutes. Record and transcribe only with consent.

## Preparation

- Send the pillar outline and two article summaries at least one business day ahead.
- Ask Janice to bring one anonymizable builder example, one common failure pattern, and one sales objection.
- Confirm which comments may be quoted, paraphrased, anonymized, or kept internal.
- Do not request or record confidential client data that is unnecessary for the content.

## Interview agenda

### 0–5 minutes: audience and stakes

1. Which kind of custom home builder is the best fit for this guidance?
2. What is usually happening in the business when they start looking for marketing help?
3. What do builders misunderstand about the time between marketing activity and a qualified project?

### 5–15 minutes: buyer decision journey

4. What makes a prospective homeowner trust one builder enough to make contact?
5. What proof do buyers look for before a consultation?
6. Which questions do strong prospects ask, and which questions reveal a poor fit?
7. Where do buyers hesitate or disappear?

### 15–27 minutes: positioning and referrals

8. What makes builder positioning sound interchangeable?
9. How do you uncover a builder's real difference without forcing a slogan?
10. When do referrals stop being a sufficient growth system?
11. What is one mistake builders make when they try to broaden their audience?

### 27–39 minutes: website and content

12. What must a builder website prove above the fold?
13. Which project details or photography are most persuasive?
14. What pages or answers are almost always missing?
15. What makes a builder blog useful rather than filler?
16. Which homeowner questions deserve a direct, public answer?

### 39–49 minutes: search, local, and AI discovery

17. How do builders actually describe their markets and project types?
18. What local proof is both honest and useful?
19. What would you want an AI answer engine to understand about a BigOrange builder client?
20. Which category claims should we avoid because the reality is more nuanced?

### 49–56 minutes: measurement and handoff

21. What should count as a qualified builder lead?
22. What can marketing own, and what still depends on sales follow-up?
23. Which leading indicators matter before closed-project data is available?
24. What does a healthy 90-day start look like?

### 56–60 minutes: distinctive point of view

25. What advice do you give builders that other agencies often miss?
26. Finish this sentence: “A builder does not need more marketing noise. They need…”
27. What should a skeptical builder ask BigOrange before deciding to work together?

## Extraction template

For every useful segment capture:

- transcript timestamp;
- exact language;
- approved use: quote, paraphrase, internal only, or exclude;
- topic and target asset;
- claim type: opinion, process, client example, metric, or prediction;
- source or verification needed;
- confidentiality and anonymization notes.

## Required post-interview decisions

1. Confirm or revise the pillar's promise and five operating stages.
2. Replace every `[JANICE SME VALIDATION]` marker.
3. Select at least one publishable firsthand example without unsupported outcome claims.
4. Approve five to eight FAQ answers.
5. Identify one story suitable for a future case study with separate client approval.
