# Design System: BigOrange Custom Home Builder Authority Hub

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Builder Signal Studio"**

The system feels like an experienced builder and marketer opening the work together: dark editing rooms, warm drawing paper, real project photography, orange signal light, and glass controls that stay close without crowding the story. It is architectural, kinetic, direct, and low-pressure.

The world now supports two long-form expressions. One behaves like an industry field guide with a sticky proof corridor. The other behaves like a portfolio proof room with a staged opening, flagship chamber, image rail, and connected-system workspace. Both share the exact BigOrange mark, the incumbent palette, the approved Unbounded and Manrope experience type, the same six verified builder images, and the same straight-talking voice.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Exact BigOrange logo particles that assemble and settle beneath a pixel-sharp source asset
- Fixed liquid-glass navigation over ink, photography, paper, and orange fields
- Dimensional Unbounded statements with Manrope explanation and deliberately varied copy rhythm
- Long editorial sequences that alternate dark experience chambers with bright proof and answer sections
- Sticky image storytelling, horizontal swipe rails, and native disclosure or tab controls
- Bespoke BigOrange line icons in dimensional glass tiles instead of numeric markers
- Orange reserved for action, signal, selection, and particle identity rather than decorative saturation
- Verified home photography and supportable proof with no invented commercial outcomes

## Colors

The palette combines construction-plan neutrals with a disciplined orange signal and two dark experience fields.

### Primary

- **BigOrange Orange:** Action fill, selected statements, progress, branded icons, and exact-logo particles.
- **Deep Orange:** Darker action state and legible accent copy on Drawing Paper or White.

### Neutral

- **Experience Ink:** Primary cinematic field, glass source, and portfolio module background.
- **Builder Ink:** The incumbent BigOrange dark and supporting high-contrast text field.
- **Signal Black:** The deepest rail and closing field.
- **Charcoal:** Supporting dark material and fallback photography surface.
- **Drawing Paper:** Warm editorial canvas for builder realities, proof, process, and answers.
- **White:** High-contrast display copy and clean proof fields.
- **Plan Line:** Quiet dividers, browser chrome, and drafting structure.
- **Muted Copy / Warm Muted:** Supporting copy on light and dark surfaces respectively.

**The Useful Orange Rule.** Orange identifies action, signal, selected proof, and the exact mark. A section may commit to orange as a material, but routine cards do not all receive an orange accent.

**The Material Contrast Rule.** Alternate Experience Ink, Signal Black, Drawing Paper, White, photography, and occasional full Orange to create depth. Do not add unrelated accent colors to manufacture variety.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Self-hosted Unbounded Variable with Raleway, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks  
**Body Font:** Self-hosted Manrope Variable with Open Sans, Arial, and sans-serif fallbacks  
**Control Font:** Manrope Variable with the body fallback stack

**Character:** Unbounded gives the experience the unmistakable dimensional signal language approved from the Primary Portfolio reference. Manrope keeps dense builder guidance warm, modern, and readable on a phone or at a desk. Both are self-hosted to avoid a fragile third-party font dependency.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (730, clamp(3.45rem, 7vw, 6rem), 0.9): First-view promises and portfolio-scale statements with a restrained dimensional shadow.
- **Headline** (720, clamp(2.9rem, 5.9vw, 6rem), 0.94): Major section arguments with short, authored line breaks.
- **Title** (720, clamp(2rem, 3.4vw, 3.75rem), 0.98): Modules, proof stages, steps, and workspace panels.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.72): Explanation, answers, evidence, and qualification guidance. Readable measures stay near 35 to 55rem depending on composition.
- **Control** (800, 0.75rem, 0.08em tracking): Navigation, tabs, compact buttons, and genuine interactive labels.

**The Two-Voice Rule.** Unbounded makes promises and names decisions; Manrope explains the work and carries evidence.

**The Headline-First Rule.** Do not place a kicker or eyebrow above a heading. The heading owns the hierarchy; controls and in-module taxonomy may use compact labels only when they perform a real interface job.

## Layout

The layout uses fluid edge gutters and intentionally different section grammars rather than repeating one card grid. Long pages move through full-viewport hero fields, two-column editorial arguments, asymmetric proof modules, sticky image stages, horizontal rails, image-led galleries, workspace tabs, process ledgers, and native answer disclosures.

The industry expression uses a split hero, a six-card swipe rail, a 390vh sticky proof corridor, and alternating paper, ink, and orange sections. The portfolio expression uses a concise 230vh opening sequence that tightens to 185svh on phones, a cinematic hero, a flagship split chamber, a 12-column module grid, a swipeable project rail, a tabbed workspace, and a sticky 90-day path.

At 900px, major two-column compositions stack, the navigation becomes a glass menu, and sticky layouts become readable sequences. At 620px, gutters tighten to 1.15rem, headlines scale with the viewport, rails retain horizontal swipe behavior, and workspace tabs become a two-by-two control grid. Every surface must stay free of horizontal overflow at 390 CSS pixels and preserve the proof order.

**The Chapter Change Rule.** Each major chapter must change composition or material, not merely swap copy inside the same card pattern.

**The Mobile Sequence Rule.** Responsive adaptation preserves the argument and every proof image. It never hides the next step or forces a desktop canvas into a phone.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is a hybrid of real photography, tonal layering, liquid glass, dimensional display shadows, scroll-sticky staging, and perspective on the flagship browser frame. Glass is used intentionally for navigation, decision panels, proof captions, system cards, and the dedicated logo chamber while long editorial fields preserve breathing room.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Glass Rail:** `0 1.5rem 4rem rgba(0,0,0,.28)` plus a one-pixel inset highlight for the persistent navigation.
- **Flagship Frame:** `0 3.5rem 7rem rgba(17,17,17,.3)` for the single portfolio browser chamber.
- **Proof Image:** `0 2rem 5rem rgba(21,21,21,.2)` for the sticky industry image stage.
- **Orange Action:** `0 1rem 2.5rem rgba(244,119,33,.2)` for the main next step.
- **Deep Glass Panel:** `0 3rem 8rem rgba(0,0,0,.45)` plus inset highlights for major decision and finale modules.

**The Glass Hierarchy Rule.** Major decision panels receive deep glass and ambient shadow; supporting modules use lighter glass or material contrast so depth remains legible instead of becoming uniform.

## Shapes

The form language is architectural with controlled softness. Half-rem corners contain annotations, three-quarter-rem corners shape controls, one-rem corners define substantial modules, and 1.15 to 1.2rem corners contain glass rails and browser frames. Large orbit circles, ellipses, and detached drafting lines are excluded. Structure comes from spacing, crop, glass depth, and alignment.

**The Radius Hierarchy Rule.** Annotation, control, module, and glass/frame radii stay distinct. Pill geometry is reserved for tags that behave like compact filters, never applied to every button by default.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Three-quarter-rem controls with a minimum 3.2rem height; the primary page action rises to 3.6rem.
- **Primary:** BigOrange Orange fill, Experience Ink copy, and compact Manrope control type.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover lifts three pixels and may resolve to Deep Orange. Keyboard focus uses a three-pixel orange outline with a five-pixel offset.
- **Ghost:** Transparent over dark material with a quiet white border and a solid surface hover.

### Liquid-Glass Navigation

The navigation is fixed inside the viewport with a translucent inset edge, 30px backdrop blur, mild saturation, ambient shadow, and an inset highlight. Desktop keeps the brand, section routes, and non-sales action in one rail. Mobile keeps only the logo and menu control in the collapsed header, places the action inside the bounded menu, locks page scroll while open, and clears the reading area while the user scrolls down.

### Exact-Logo Particles

Particles are sampled from the alpha and original pixel colors of the verified BigOrange orange logo. They scatter, assemble, and then settle under the original pixel-sharp logo asset. The full wordmark and tagline remain readable, and the final logo owns a dedicated full-viewport chamber at the absolute bottom. Reduced motion resolves directly into the complete asset.

### Sticky Proof Stage

The industry proof stage keeps the argument on one side while four verified builder images crossfade inside a clean image field. Copy, semantic label, caption, and image change together without leading-zero numbering or reticle clutter. On mobile the stage becomes a stable vertical reading sequence without horizontal overflow.

### Swipe Rails

System and project rails support buttons, pointer dragging, touch swipe, and left or right arrow keys. Cards use different widths or heights to create editorial range. Scroll position receives a visible progress or current-item readout where the interface needs one.

### Connected-System Workspace

Four keyboard-operable tabs switch between Position, Presence, Demand, and Proof. Each panel pairs an explanation with one verified image and keeps the active tab visually unmistakable.

### Cards / Containers

Paper, White, Orange, and Ink modules may sit in the same asymmetric grid. Cards use deliberate glass depth, dimensional one-rem SVG icon tiles, a small citrus-leaf glint, scale, crop, and material change for emphasis. The icon drawings use layered fill and line geometry so Position, Website, Search, Content, Campaigns, Measurement, Proof, and Fit remain recognizable rather than collapsing into generic symbols. Image cards always carry useful alt text and contextual copy.

### FAQ Disclosures

Native `details` and `summary` elements provide keyboard-operable disclosure. Plan-line dividers separate questions; an authored two-line plus becomes a minus when open; only one answer remains open at a time in the portfolio expression.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the verified BigOrange logo, incumbent builder photography, orange, charcoal, and white while using the approved Unbounded and Manrope experience type.
- **Do** sample particle geometry from the exact logo asset and preserve its wordmark and tagline.
- **Do** hand particles off to the original logo asset and reserve a dedicated bottom chamber for the resolved mark.
- **Do** use bespoke BigOrange SVG icons rather than leading-zero or Unicode markers.
- **Do** let chapters change material, scale, and interaction across the long page.
- **Do** keep proof contextual, supportable, and visually connected to the claim it supports.
- **Do** preserve visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, 390px overflow safety, and mobile proof order.
- **Do** keep important answers in static HTML with matching FAQ schema.

### Don't:

- **Don't** place kickers or eyebrows above headings.
- **Don't** use gradient text, Unicode glyphs as icons, thick side accents, or repeating generic cards.
- **Don't** replace the incumbent brand with an architecture-portfolio palette or generic SaaS system.
- **Don't** reintroduce leading-zero labels, giant orbit rings, satellite motifs, detached drafting lines, or fuzzy particle-only logos.
- **Don't** turn every surface orange or give every glass panel the same depth.
- **Don't** use detached stock promises, invented outcomes, or unsupported statistics.
- **Don't** make the non-sales action urgent, manipulative, or high pressure.
