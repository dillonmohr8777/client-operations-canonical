# Custom Home Builder Marketing Readiness Checklist

Use this worksheet to find the next right fix before adding another campaign, page, or tool.

## Positioning

- [ ] The first screen names the builder type, market, project fit, and next step.
- [ ] The message explains a visible operating difference rather than relying on quality, integrity, craftsmanship, or dreams alone.
- [ ] The site helps a wrong-fit prospect self-select respectfully.
- [ ] The sales conversation and website make the same promise.

## Website and proof

- [ ] Selected projects explain the homeowner goal, meaningful constraint, decision, and builder role.
- [ ] Every public review, award, result, and project detail has an approved source.
- [ ] The process shows major decisions and communication expectations.
- [ ] Team roles, service areas, project types, and qualification guidance are current.
- [ ] The primary CTA tells the visitor what happens next.

## Search, local, and AI visibility

- [ ] Each distinct audience need has one primary URL.
- [ ] Titles, headings, links, and image alternatives describe the page clearly.
- [ ] Canonicals, sitemaps, breadcrumbs, and visible structured data agree.
- [ ] Market content reflects real local expertise rather than copied city-page language.
- [ ] Important answers are direct, current, attributable, and independently supportable.
- [ ] Search and AI crawler access is an intentional policy decision.

## Content system

- [ ] The next 90 days of content map to real buyer and sales questions.
- [ ] Every asset has a reader job, source, owner, reviewer, CTA, and internal-link destination.
- [ ] Expert interviews separate publishable facts from confidential material.
- [ ] AI-assisted drafts retain source notes and never bypass human fact checking.
- [ ] Useful existing content is updated before overlapping pages are created.

## Mobile, accessibility, and performance

- [ ] A real mobile viewport is usable without pinching, horizontal scrolling, or a prolonged blank state.
- [ ] The hero image is correctly sized and below-the-fold images lazy-load.
- [ ] Keyboard focus is visible and all controls work without a mouse.
- [ ] Contrast, headings, form labels, links, image alternatives, and iframe titles are verified.
- [ ] Console errors, unused scripts, render-blocking resources, and third-party dependencies are reviewed.

## Measurement and lead quality

- [ ] Analytics, booking, forms, calls, consent, CRM, and sales use agreed definitions.
- [ ] Primary CTAs have consistent event names.
- [ ] Reporting separates test activity from real prospects.
- [ ] The scorecard includes consultation fit and sales acceptance, not only visits and form counts.
- [ ] Conversion reporting is described as pending validation until the complete path is verified.
