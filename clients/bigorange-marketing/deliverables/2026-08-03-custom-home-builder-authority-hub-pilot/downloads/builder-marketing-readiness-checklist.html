Custom Home Builder Marketing Readiness Checklist

Find the next right fix before adding another campaign, page, or tool.

Positioning

  • The first screen names the builder type, market, project fit, and next step.
  • The message explains a visible operating difference.
  • The site helps a wrong-fit prospect self-select respectfully.
  • The sales conversation and website make the same promise.

Website and proof

  • Selected projects explain the goal, constraint, decision, and builder role.
  • Every public review, result, and project detail has an approved source.
  • The process shows major decisions and communication expectations.
  • Team, market, project-fit, and CTA information is current.

Search, local, and AI visibility

  • Each audience need has one primary URL.
  • Titles, headings, links, image alternatives, canonicals, sitemaps, and schema agree.
  • Market content reflects real local expertise.
  • Important answers are direct, current, attributable, and supportable.

Content system

  • The next 90 days map to real buyer and sales questions.
  • Every asset has a job, source, owner, reviewer, CTA, and link destination.
  • Interviews separate publishable facts from confidential material.
  • AI-assisted drafts retain source notes and human fact checking.

Mobile, accessibility, and performance

  • The page is usable on a phone without horizontal scrolling or a long blank state.
  • Images are correctly sized and below-the-fold media lazy-loads.
  • Keyboard focus, contrast, headings, labels, links, alternatives, and iframe titles are verified.
  • Console errors, unused scripts, render-blocking resources, and third parties are reviewed.

Measurement and lead quality

  • Analytics, booking, forms, calls, consent, CRM, and sales use agreed definitions.
  • Primary CTAs use consistent events and test activity is excluded.
  • The scorecard includes consultation fit and sales acceptance.
  • Conversion reporting stays pending until the complete path is verified.
