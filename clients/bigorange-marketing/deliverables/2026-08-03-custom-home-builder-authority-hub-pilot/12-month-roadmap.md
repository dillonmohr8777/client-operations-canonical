# 12-Month Builder Marketing Authority Roadmap

## North star

Become the most useful, most transparent marketing resource for growth-minded custom home builders in the United States—measured by qualified demand, market recognition, and repeat use, not by an unprovable “number one” claim.

## Quarter 1: establish the authority hub

- Launch the evolved pillar, two production articles, three subpillars, core FAQs, and two practical downloads.
- Correct performance, accessibility, schema, measurement, and publishing workflow gaps.
- Establish a monthly SME interview and update cadence.
- Define qualified lead and sales feedback loops.

## Quarter 2: build evidence and local depth

- Publish market-page and local-proof guidance without doorway-page duplication.
- Launch a builder case-study program with consistent before/work/timeframe/result documentation.
- Publish budget, KPI, review, portfolio, and project-story guidance.
- Create a quarterly “What We Are Seeing in Builder Marketing” briefing using approved aggregated evidence.

## Quarter 3: expand the buyer-decision franchise

- Produce a research-backed buyer trust report or survey with transparent methodology.
- Launch the “From Referral-Dependent to Search-Visible” webinar and companion toolkit.
- Add video transcripts, expert roundups, and builder sales-objection content.
- Form selective partnerships with builder associations, vendors, designers, architects, or finance specialists where editorial independence is clear.

## Quarter 4: compound and prune

- Update winning assets using real query, sales, and customer questions.
- Consolidate or retire thin and overlapping content.
- Publish an annual benchmark with methodology, limits, and date.
- Convert the authority system into sales enablement, nurture, and onboarding materials.
- Set the next year's market or service expansion based on qualified-pipeline evidence.

## Sustainable publication rhythm

- Monthly: one SME session, one substantial article, one existing-page refresh, and one short FAQ batch.
- Quarterly: one proof asset, webinar or briefing; one technical and accessibility audit; one executive performance review.
- Annually: one original benchmark or research asset and a full cluster consolidation review.

## Primary opportunities

- Builder-specific buyer-journey expertise.
- Honest AI-search education grounded in official platform guidance.
- Original evidence from BigOrange's work, with client approval.
- Warm, practical tools builders can use before they are ready to hire.
- Stronger connections between website, visibility, proof, lead qualification, and sales feedback.

## Principal risks

- Publishing AI-generated sameness without firsthand expertise.
- Creating multiple pages that compete for the same topic.
- Treating volatile AI answers as stable rankings.
- Claiming performance before CRM, analytics, and attribution definitions are reconciled.
- Letting Elementor, media, and third-party scripts erase the benefit of stronger content.
- Publishing case claims without source, timeframe, or approval.

## When to expect qualified inbound leads

No responsible roadmap can guarantee a date. Initial leading indicators can change within weeks after crawl and indexation. Qualified organic demand commonly requires months of consistent technical, content, proof, and distribution work, with timing affected by current authority, competition, market reach, sales-cycle length, and implementation quality. BigOrange should evaluate the first 90 days as a validated foundation, months 4–6 as an evidence-building period, and months 7–12 as the first meaningful compounding window—not as a promise of a particular lead count.
