Good questions deserve straight answers.

What makes marketing for a custom home builder different?

The sale is personal, expensive, and built on trust. Buyers need more than a gallery. They need a clear sense of fit, process, judgment, communication, and what it will feel like to work together.

Should a builder still rely on referrals?

Referrals are valuable. Your marketing should make them stronger by giving referred buyers the proof and context they need to move forward with confidence.

What should a builder website include?

A clear market position, strong project stories, an understandable process, real people, useful answers, credible proof, and a next step that feels helpful.

How do you know if the marketing is working?

Start with the quality of the conversations. Then connect visibility and website behavior to consultation fit, sales acceptance, pipeline, and closed work.
