# BigOrange Builder Keyword Strategy

Prepared August 20, 2026

## Recommendation

Evolve `https://bigorange.marketing/marketing-agency-for-builders/` as the one commercial authority hub. Do not launch a second custom-home-builder-agency pillar.

Moz shows that the existing URL already ranks strongly for the commercial cluster, while the Semrush map shows meaningful demand for broader informational and consideration-stage builder topics. Splitting the pillar would risk cannibalizing an established page.

These terms should remain semantically visible in the title, opening copy, proof, service framing, and metadata without forcing repetitions.

## Improve on the existing URL

- `custom home builder marketing agency`: 13 desktop and 13 mobile. Use as a natural niche variation in the title, eyebrow, description, one body section, internal anchors, and relevant inbound links.
- `home builder marketing firm`: 15 desktop and 14 mobile. Cover as a secondary agency-category variation, not a separate page.
- `builder advertising and marketing`: 14 desktop and 17 mobile after a material decline. Diagnose the older ranking URL and intent before deciding whether this belongs on the hub or an advertising support page.
- `home builder marketing solutions`: tracked but unranked. Support with a specific solution/system section and future guide.
- `home builder marketing automation`: tracked but unranked. Build as a distinct operational guide in the first 90 days; do not overload the commercial hub.

## Build as distinct supporting intent

Use the August 4 Semrush decision map for demand, difficulty, CPC, and build priority:

- `SEO for home builders` — subpillar
- `home builder website not generating leads` — supporting article
- `custom home builder target market segments` — guide
- `home builder marketing plan` — guide and worksheet
- `local SEO for home builders` — supporting article
- `home builder website optimization` — technical article
- `recommended phrases for home builder website SEO` — checklist
- `home builder blog` — supporting article already drafted

Each support URL must link back to the authority hub, and the hub must link to the support URL once it exists. Do not publish empty links.

## Reject or defer

- Reject generic `digital marketing agency` and `SEO agency` terms for this pilot because the intent and difficulty are too broad.
- Reject consumer queries such as `custom home builders near me`; the searcher wants a builder, not a builder-marketing partner.
- Defer paid-media, consulting, and ambiguous website-builder terms until the core authority and proof system is live.
