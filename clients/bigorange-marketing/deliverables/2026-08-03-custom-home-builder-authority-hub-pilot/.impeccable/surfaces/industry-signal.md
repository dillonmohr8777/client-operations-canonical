# BigOrange Industry Signal

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Builder marketing should make judgment visible, not decorate generic agency claims.

**OWN-WORLD:** BigOrange orange, Experience Ink, Drawing Paper, exact-mark particles, verified architectural photography, and liquid-glass navigation.

**STORY:** Recognize the builder reality, inspect the connected system, watch the work become proof, understand the authority path, get straight answers, and choose a useful next step.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** The verified white logo, one builder-specific promise, one direct mechanism, the non-sales action, real builder photography, and the exact orange mark resolving from particles.

**FORM:** Long-form industry field guide. Split hero, five builder realities, six-part swipe system, 390vh sticky proof corridor, answer authority path, image range, verified case links, measurement, FAQ, and particle close.

**FINISH:** The page must feel closer to an Align industry solution and Need Momentum depth experience than to a conventional agency landing page, while preserving BigOrange's own palette and voice.

## Product job

Help custom home builder owners and marketing leaders see how position, website, search, useful answers, project proof, advertising, and measurement work together. Earn a low-pressure non-sales conversation without unsupported urgency.

## Quality bar

- Exact BigOrange identity is unmistakable in the first viewport and close.
- All six builder images appear with useful alt text and non-invented context.
- No kicker or eyebrow sits above a heading.
- Swipe, sticky, disclosure, focus, mobile, and reduced-motion behavior work live.
- The page remains noindex on the private Netlify preview and canonicalizes to the intended BigOrange service URL.
