# BigOrange Custom Home Builder Authority Hub

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Turn BigOrange's builder expertise into a design-studio review wall: clear decisions, pinned proof, and one useful next move.

**OWN-WORLD:** Use BigOrange orange, charcoal, white, Raleway, Open Sans, incumbent home photography, low-pressure language, and verified client proof. Do not invent a second visual identity.

**STORY:** Name the builder problem, show the five-part blueprint, prove BigOrange can execute it, explain visibility and measurement, answer objections, then invite a non-sales call.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Show the existing builder identity, exact category promise, one-sentence mechanism, and primary non-sales CTA over verified home photography.

**FORM:** Design studio wall. Structured panels, pinned proof, blueprint index, and material contrast give the content a builder-native review rhythm without copying a residential architecture portfolio.

**FINISH:** Take the chosen concept all the way to a polished, coherent end state rather than stopping at a safe or generic first pass.

## Product job

Help an owner or marketing leader at a custom home builder understand BigOrange's niche expertise, inspect a practical marketing system, and choose a low-pressure next step. Preserve the current service-page conversion job while adding topic-cluster authority.

## Quality bar

- Established BigOrange identity is unmistakable in the first viewport.
- One H1, direct reader job, and one dominant conversion action.
- Responsive layout has no horizontal overflow at 320 CSS pixels.
- All controls have keyboard focus and reduced-motion behavior.
- Below-the-fold content uses a repeatable but non-generic studio-wall grammar.
- No Janice-attributed claim or unsupported result appears before review.

## Approved direction rationale

Seven grounded structural candidates were considered: blueprint walkthrough, showhome tour, trust timeline, design studio wall, sales shortlist navigator, build ledger, and authority-hub atlas. The design studio wall best supports a professional builder audience while keeping BigOrange's established visual authority intact. Challenger visual worlds were rejected because they would reduce brand recognition without improving audience identification or product clarity.
