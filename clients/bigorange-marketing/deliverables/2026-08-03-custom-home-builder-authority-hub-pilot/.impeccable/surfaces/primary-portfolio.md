# BigOrange Primary Portfolio Experience

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The builder's judgment is the product; the experience turns that judgment into a signal buyers can feel.

**OWN-WORLD:** BigOrange Ink, White, Orange, exact-logo particles, cinematic builder imagery, glass rails, and editorial proof chambers.

**STORY:** Recognize the work, reveal the exact mark, name the difference, open the proof, inspect the system, understand the first 90 days, get direct answers, and choose the next move.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A live statement appears immediately, then scroll resolves the exact orange BigOrange logo before opening the cinematic builder hero.

**FORM:** Primary Portfolio proof room. 355vh staged opening, oversized image hero, capability band, manifesto, flagship browser chamber, four proof modules, six-image swipe rail, tabbed workspace, sticky 90-day path, case links, answers, and particle close.

**FINISH:** Carry the Primary Portfolio's exact-logo particle behavior, glass rail, proof chambers, and swipeable work grammar into a much longer BigOrange-native experience without copying the portfolio's colors or claims.

## Product job

Make a custom builder feel the depth of BigOrange's thinking before reading every detail. Use the experience to pull the reader toward proof and a low-pressure non-sales conversation, not to create motion for its own sake.

## Visual ingredient inventory

| Ingredient | Implementation medium | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Staged opening | Sticky scroll scene + exact-logo canvas | Statement visible at load, exact mark at mid-scroll, second statement at exit |
| Cinematic hero | Verified blue-hour home image + oversized type | Builder category, mechanism, and action remain HTML |
| Flagship chamber | Perspective browser frame + verified walkthrough image | Show the thinking behind the finish |
| Proof modules | Asymmetric 12-column grid | Paper, Orange, image, and Ink each carry a different job |
| Project rail | Six verified images + semantic figures | Mixed dimensions, buttons, drag, swipe, and arrow-key access |
| Connected workspace | WAI-style tabs + four image panels | Position, Presence, Demand, and Proof switch together |
| 90-day path | Sticky orange argument + three long steps | Honest sequence with no mystery-month language |
| Direct answers | Six native disclosures + FAQ schema | Static HTML remains extractable to crawlers and answer engines |

## Quality bar

- The opening never presents a blank first view.
- The glass rail remains fully inside desktop and mobile viewports.
- Exact-logo particles preserve the verified wordmark and tagline.
- No kicker or eyebrow sits above a heading.
- All imagery, copy, tabs, disclosures, and actions remain useful without motion.
- The route stays noindex on the private Netlify preview and canonicalizes to the intended BigOrange service URL.
