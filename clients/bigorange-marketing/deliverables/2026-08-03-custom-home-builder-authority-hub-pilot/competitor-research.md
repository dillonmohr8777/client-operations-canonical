# Competitor Research

Research date: August 3, 2026  
Market: United States custom home builder marketing  
Purpose: identify category conventions, proof patterns, content gaps, and opportunities for a more useful BigOrange authority hub.

## Executive finding

The category is crowded with agencies that say they understand builders. The clearest opportunities are not a longer service list or a louder promise. BigOrange can stand out by combining three things competitors rarely present together: a builder-specific growth blueprint, source-backed AI-search guidance, and a warm, low-pressure explanation of how marketing work becomes measurable qualified demand.

## What the market already expects

1. Builder specialization stated above the fold.
2. Proof from real construction or home-service work.
3. Clear service coverage across website, SEO, content, paid media, and lead follow-up.
4. A point of view on referrals, long sales cycles, local competition, and lead quality.
5. A direct CTA for a review, strategy session, or plan.

## White-space opportunity

### 1. Explain the whole decision system

Most pages list tactics. BigOrange should show how a prospective homeowner moves from first exposure to shortlist to consultation, and what marketing evidence they need at each stage.

### 2. Make AI visibility concrete

Avoid vague claims about “ranking in ChatGPT.” Explain the controllable inputs: crawl access, indexability, visible answer-ready copy, consistent entity information, reputable citations, structured data that matches the page, strong internal linking, and independent proof.

### 3. Use a real editorial operating model

The proposal's one-hour SME interview should become a visible production advantage. Show how one recorded conversation becomes a validated pillar update, articles, FAQs, case-study prompts, video clips, emails, and sales-enablement answers—without allowing AI to invent expertise.

### 4. Own the quality-of-lead conversation

The category overuses traffic and lead volume. BigOrange can define a better scorecard: qualified consultations, service-area fit, project-type fit, budget readiness, source-assist, sales acceptance, and movement through the real buying process.

## Page-level recommendation

Evolve the existing `/marketing-agency-for-builders/` page rather than creating a competing core URL. Preserve useful proof, award badges, case examples, package context, and established internal links. Replace the current broad sequence with a custom-home-builder authority structure, anchored table of contents, question-led sections, topic-cluster cards, cited FAQ answers, and a low-pressure strategy CTA.
