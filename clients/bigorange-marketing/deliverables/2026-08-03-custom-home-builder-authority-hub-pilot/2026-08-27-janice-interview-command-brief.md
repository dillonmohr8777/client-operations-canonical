## Permission codes

- `Q` — attributable quote approved, subject to final copy review.
- `P` — paraphrase approved, subject to final copy review.
- `A` — anonymized example approved; record the required redactions.
- `I` — internal context only.
- `X` — exclude from all working and published content.
- `?` — permission not settled; do not use publicly.

For any named client, metric, result, award, timeframe, or sensitive project detail, permission alone is not enough. Record the original source and verification needed.

## The complete 60-minute interview

### 0–5 minutes — audience and stakes

Use this section to define the right reader, the buying context, and realistic expectations.

1. **Which kind of custom home builder is the best fit for this guidance?**
   - Capture: project type, geography, size or maturity, sales model, values, and disqualifiers.
   - Feeds: pillar audience statement, qualification language, hero copy, FAQs.

2. **What is usually happening in the business when they start looking for marketing help?**
   - Probe: What happened right before the call? What do they say in their own words?
   - Capture: trigger moments, pain language, urgency, and one real but anonymizable example.
   - Feeds: pillar opening, CTA, buyer-state language, sales objection sheet.

3. **What do builders misunderstand about the time between marketing activity and a qualified project?**
   - Capture: timing misconceptions, sales-cycle caveats, leading indicators, and claims to avoid.
   - Feeds: 90-day section, SEO timeline answer, measurement FAQ, final presentation.

### 5–15 minutes — buyer decision journey

Use this section to explain how homeowners build trust and where qualified opportunities are won or lost.

4. **What makes a prospective homeowner trust one builder enough to make contact?**
   - Probe: Can you walk me through a real situation where trust increased or broke down?
   - Capture: trust signals, language, process evidence, and story permission.
   - Feeds: pillar trust journey, article 1, FAQ, CTA.

5. **What proof do buyers look for before a consultation?**
   - Capture: project context, photography, reviews, people, process, credentials, local proof, and what is overrated.
   - Feeds: proof section, imagery plan, article 1, case-study requirements.

6. **Which questions do strong prospects ask, and which questions reveal a poor fit?**
   - Probe: What exact objection or concern do you hear, and how do you answer it?
   - Capture: at least two strong-prospect questions, one poor-fit signal, one objection, and Janice's response language.
   - Feeds: qualification FAQ, sales objection sheet, consultation CTA, article 1.

7. **Where do buyers hesitate or disappear?**
   - Capture: journey stage, cause, missing information, sales follow-up dependency, and a common failure pattern.
   - Feeds: website content gaps, CTA, buyer journey, measurement handoff.
