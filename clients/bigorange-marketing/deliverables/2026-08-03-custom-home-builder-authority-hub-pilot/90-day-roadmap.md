# 90-Day Authority Hub Roadmap

## Objective

Turn BigOrange's existing builder page into the center of a measurable, maintainable custom-home-builder topic cluster without creating duplicate core pages or publishing unsupported claims.

## Days 1–30: foundation and proof of concept

### Strategy and content

- Complete Janice's SME interview and apply approved insights.
- Apply the completed August 4 Semrush priority map to page briefs and on-page targeting.
- Resolve final primary query, related query set, and cannibalization risks.
- Edit the existing builder URL into the authority pillar.
- Finalize and publish the two supporting articles after human review.
- Add the Builder Website Trust Checklist as the first downloadable asset.

### Technical

- Create a staging revision and rollback snapshot.
- Fix the mobile LCP request chain and highest-impact JavaScript long tasks.
- Resolve the two missing image-alt cases and accessibility findings.
- Implement the coherent schema graph and breadcrumbs.
- Verify analytics, consent, forms, thank-you behavior, and source capture.

### Baseline

- Record Search Console and GA4 landing-page baseline when access is available.
- Define qualified consultation, sales-accepted lead, and assisted outcome with the CRM owner.
- Create the first versioned AI-answer observation set.

### Exit criteria

- Pillar and two articles approved and live on the intended URLs.
- No competing page targeting the same primary query.
- Mobile lab LCP materially improved and field measurement plan active.
- Tracking definitions documented; conversion reporting remains pending until validated.

## Days 31–60: cluster expansion

- Publish the three subpillar guides.
- Publish articles on local SEO, AI search visibility, and the builder marketing plan.
- Add reciprocal internal links and breadcrumbs across the cluster.
- Launch the Local and AI Visibility Baseline Worksheet.
- Create an evidence-backed case-study intake template.
- Refresh GBP, directory, profile, and organization-entity consistency where access and approval exist.
- Review query emergence, indexation, crawl patterns, referral sources, and reader behavior.
- Run an editorial retrospective: which SME inputs created the strongest, most distinct answers?

## Days 61–90: proof and optimization

- Publish buyer-choice, referral-dependence, and lead-qualification articles.
- Publish the first approved builder case study or clearly label the dependency if client proof is not approved.
- Test one CTA variation on the pillar with a defined hypothesis and primary metric.
- Update weak or ambiguous sections using real Search Console queries and sales questions.
- Review content decay, broken links, schema consistency, Core Web Vitals field data, and accessibility.
- Present the first quarterly authority review and decide the next content wedge.

## Scorecard

### Leading indicators

- indexed cluster URLs;
- impressions and clicks from relevant builder-marketing queries;
- non-brand query breadth;
- engaged sessions and meaningful scroll or navigation behavior;
- downloads and consultation starts;
- citations and referrals from discoverable AI/search sources where observable;
- sales-team use of the pillar and supporting assets.

### Business indicators

- qualified consultations;
- sales acceptance rate;
- service-area and project-type fit;
- assisted pipeline;
- closed-won outcomes after attribution and CRM definitions are validated.

## Owners and cadence

- Strategy and SEO owner: weekly build review, monthly performance synthesis.
- Janice or assigned SME: source interview and factual review.
- Editor: voice, clarity, evidence, and originality.
- WordPress owner: staging, technical QA, publish, and rollback.
- Sales/CRM owner: lead-quality and downstream-outcome validation.
