The authority hub and the content engine

A hub is not a page.It is a rule about who owns which question.

The architecture, cloned per vertical

Industry hub: one commercial URL, updated in place
 ├── Supporting posts: definitions, checklists, examples
 ├── Topic pages: visibility, website, local, lead-gen
 ├── FAQ companion inside the hub, never a second FAQ URL
 └── Links: hub ↔ cluster ↔ book-appointment

Six rules travel with it. A keyword variation is not a page. Commercial queries sit on the hub; definitions sit on supporting URLs. Proof pages are not second hubs. Unranked high‑volume terms get an owner, not a post‑mortem. Generic head terms stay off the must‑own list. Undefined service lines stay blocked.
