## Placement rules

- Keep spelling, proportions, colors, and geometry exactly as captured.
- Do not redraw, morph, recolor, crop, or place the mark on people, vehicles, tools, uniforms, buildings, or generated scenes.
- The tagline is white. Put the logo only on a dark AMI field (`#0B1620`, `#0F2230`, or the green-blue cover gradient). Never put it on cream or paper.
- If a light page needs a brand presence, use a dark header strip and sit the exact file on that strip.
