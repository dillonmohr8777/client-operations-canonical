All material claims in the blog series are limited to the following authoritative sources or to clearly labeled practical guidance from AMI's existing service model.

## Editorial guardrails

- The articles do not claim that cleaning prevents all illness, guarantees compliance, or replaces an infection-prevention professional.
- Product instructions, Safety Data Sheets, facility policies, manufacturer requirements, and applicable regulations control when they are more specific than general educational guidance.
- Healthcare articles distinguish environmental cleaning from clinical-device reprocessing and do not present AMI as a clinical infection-control authority.
- Images are illustrative AI-generated editorial artwork, not photographs of an actual AMI client site, employee, or completed project.
