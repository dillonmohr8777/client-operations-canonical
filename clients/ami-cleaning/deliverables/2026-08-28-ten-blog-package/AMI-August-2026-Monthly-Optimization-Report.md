Each article includes a distinct primary query, concise answer-first introduction, clear heading structure, scannable lists, contextual calls to action, three useful FAQs, authoritative citations and natural links to relevant AMI service or industry pages. The topics support existing commercial pages instead of duplicating them.

### Original imagery and accessibility

Ten unique editorial images were produced and compressed to WebP, each with:

- descriptive alternative text based on the image’s purpose;
- a concise caption;
- the attribution “AI-generated editorial image by OpenAI for AMI Commercial Cleaning Services, 2026”;
- no embedded marketing text, logo or watermark; and
- an explicit note that the image is illustrative, not documentation of an actual AMI employee or client site.

Article structure was reviewed for one clear H1, sequential headings, descriptive link text, readable paragraphs, labeled lists and non-color-dependent meaning. Final production QA will include keyboard behavior, visible focus, image-alt persistence and heading order in the live WordPress theme.

### SEO and GEO foundations

- Unique SEO title, meta description, slug, excerpt and primary keyword for every article
- Internal links woven into relevant paragraphs rather than placed in a detached link list
- Primary-source external references from CDC, EPA and OSHA
- Direct answers and self-contained FAQ responses for AI retrieval and featured-snippet eligibility
- Article and FAQ structured-data implementation plan, subject to the active WordPress SEO-plugin output to avoid duplicate schema
- Canonical-URL and sitemap verification included in the release checklist

## Why the August scope increased

The launch established a working website and initial indexing. August data showed the correct next investment: Google is exposing AMI to more searches, but many relevant queries still sit outside high-click positions and the site has limited informational coverage to earn those clicks.

The added hours funded a complete content production cycle—not ten unedited drafts. That cycle included query mapping, authoritative research, writing, editing, conversion paths, accessibility, unique image production, image compression, attribution, metadata, internal linking, external citations, analytics review, lead-quality checks, publication preparation and reporting.

At the established $20 hourly rate, 70.5 hours equals the approved **$1,410** August total.
