# Onsite Google Ads course adapter

Status: active local operating guidance
Client: `onsite-concrete-landscape`
Shared source: `../../../docs/GOOGLE_ADS_BOOTCAMP_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`

## Business outcome

Apply the course to generate qualified estimate opportunities for Onsite's verified concrete and landscaping services in Vacaville and the approved service area. Traffic, form activity, phone clicks, and platform conversions are diagnostic until reconciled to a connected relevant call, qualified estimate, or booked job.

## Campaign structure

- Keep concrete and landscaping intent separately inspectable in campaigns, ad groups, search terms, landing-page routing, and reporting.
- Organize high-intent Search around service, intent, and market only after current conversion validation and authority support the build.
- Treat Performance Max, Smart campaigns, or any other observed campaign type as live-state evidence, not a permanent architecture rule.
- Re-read the exact live account before stating which campaigns are enabled, paused, limited, or spending.

## Measurement contract

- Proposed primary outcome: `qualified_estimate`.
- Secondary diagnostics: `contact_form`, `phone_call`, and `estimate_start`.
- Required campaign-source convention: `onsite_google_{service}_{market}_{campaign_type}`.
- Deduplicate forms, calls, CRM or estimate records, and imported outcomes. A phone click is not a connected call, and a connected call is not a qualified estimate without disposition evidence.
- Exclude tests from lead, estimate, booking, and primary-conversion totals.

## Course adaptations

- Business intake must confirm the exact service, market, price or margin assumptions, capacity, seasonality, estimate path, and landing page before keyword or budget planning.
- Unit economics should use qualified-estimate and booked-job outcomes when available, not raw contact-action CPA.
- Search-term review should use irrelevant, supplier, DIY, employment, and wrong-service patterns as evidence for negatives without over-blocking valid local demand.
- Ads and pages must match the exact service and market and use only verified claims, project proof, coverage, pricing, schedule, and warranty language.
- Do not scale from click efficiency or low reported CPA while conversion setup, call quality, deduplication, or estimate disposition remains unresolved.

## Approval boundary

Local research, audits, drafts, QA plans, and paused proposals are allowed. Live account, site, tag, CRM, budget, bidding, campaign-state, or conversion changes remain governed by `launch-authority.json` and current explicit approval.
