## Closest quantified organic baseline

Across the three preserved query rows for Apr 19–Jul 17, Google Search showed **632 impressions, 0 recorded clicks, and a simple unweighted average position of 17.82**. This is not a site total and should not be presented as one. It does show that local commercial queries were appearing in Google before the July content plan, with room to improve rankings and clicks.

The July package uses those live Search Console signals to select three non-duplicative topics. It was explicitly marked review-ready and not published, so none of the later July drafts can be credited with traffic or leads.

## GBP and website-action evidence

- A June GBP PDF report referenced in the parent evidence set reportedly showed **1 call and 6 website clicks**. The underlying PDF/export was not present in the exact Drive folder or local client records reviewed here. Treat this as a historical report claim pending source-file recovery.
- An August 31 email reportedly stated **6+ phone and 10+ website-click organic actions**. No raw GBP export, date range, or action definitions were found in the allowed sources. Do not promote it beyond an unverified client-reported signal.
- A GBP website click is a referral action. It does not demonstrate a phone connection, form acceptance, qualified estimate, or booked job.

## Evidence gaps

1. No Onsite GA4 property appeared in the currently connected account inventory, so sessions, source/medium, landing-page behavior, and form events could not be reconstructed.
2. No Onsite Search Console property appeared in the current connected inventory. The local query figures above are therefore historical snapshots, not a fresh performance pull.
3. The exact Drive folder `https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1hLqg9TJqXztwdZyFScCEQBEh79cevoiH` contains project-photo folders and a social-login document, but no GA4, Search Console, GBP, or Semrush traffic export.
4. Semrush was unavailable for this review because the connected service had no usable API units/traffic plan.
5. No source in this audit ties organic visibility or GBP actions to named jobs, calls, forms, or revenue.

## Safe conclusion

The record supports saying: “Onsite had measurable organic Google visibility from April through July, existing content live by May, and a material Fairfield local-content build in August. The retained data does not support a claim about total organic traffic or qualified organic calls. To prove that result, recover the GBP Performance export and connect the exact GA4 and Search Console properties.”
