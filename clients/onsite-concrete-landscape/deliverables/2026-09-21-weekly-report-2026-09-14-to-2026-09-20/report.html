A week spent establishing what is actually wrong with the measurement on this account, rather than assuming it matched someone else's.

Read your report

Organic is moving. The paid measurement is broken, and it is a different fault to the one we named.

Named, not glossed.

Two things pulling in opposite directions. Organically the account is progressing: two more local pages went live and the city picture is separating, with Vacaville strongest, Fairfield improving and Davis only just started. On the paid side a correction is owed. Earlier in the month the counting problem here was described as the same defect fixed on another account. It is not. This account has two separate issues: nine conversion actions are all set as primary, so very different events are summed into one number, and the advertising landing page carries no measurement tag at all, so form submissions made there never reach the ad account. Neither is fixed.

This is a work and delivery update. Current platform metrics are not established for this report, so they are not estimated.

This report deliberately leads with what is wrong with the measurement, because no delivery figure from this account means much until it is settled.

Sep 14Verified work / decision

Earlier description of the counting defect withdrawn

This account's conversion counting problem had been attributed to the same defect corrected on another account. A direct check showed that is wrong. The defect here is different and larger, and the earlier description should not be relied on.

Carrying a wrong diagnosis forward would have meant applying a fix that does nothing here and reporting the problem as solved.

Direct account review, Sep 14
Sep 14Verified work / decision

Nine conversion actions are all marked primary

Nine separate conversion actions are set as primary on this account. Events of very different value are therefore summed into a single reported number, and the bidding treats them as equivalent.

A single conversion total built from nine unlike events cannot be read as a count of enquiries, and it teaches the bidding to chase whichever is easiest to trigger.

Conversion action list read directly, Sep 14
Sep 14Verified work / decision

The advertising landing page carries no measurement tag

The page paid traffic is sent to has no measurement tag installed, so submissions made on it are never reported back to the ad account. This has been the case throughout the period covered by recent reporting.

This is the most likely explanation for the gap between what the website receives and what the advertising account shows. It is a plumbing problem, not a performance one.

Landing page source inspected directly, Sep 14

Both findings remain open at the end of this period. The tag installation needs access to the landing page, and the conversion action rework needs a decision from you about which single event counts as a real enquiry.

Stating this plainly is better than reporting the diagnosis as though it were the repair.
