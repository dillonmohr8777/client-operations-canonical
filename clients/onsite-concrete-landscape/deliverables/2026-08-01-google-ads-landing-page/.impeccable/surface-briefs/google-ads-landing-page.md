## Mode

## Visitor and intent

The visitor arrives from Google Ads with Vacaville concrete or landscape intent. Keep those two service paths separately inspectable while presenting Onsite as one coordinated team for the connected site.

## Primary action

Move a qualified visitor into the consultation request or phone path. The primary measurement action is `qualified_estimate`; supporting interactions are `estimate_start`, `contact_form`, and `phone_call`.

## Surface expression

Follow the datum-line site sequence established in the first body comment: confirm Vacaville fit, choose concrete or landscape, show verified real Onsite project imagery, explain the one-team sequence, then request a consultation. On mobile, preserve the red index and vertical sequence while the sticky call and consultation bar keeps both actions reachable and clears when the form enters view.
