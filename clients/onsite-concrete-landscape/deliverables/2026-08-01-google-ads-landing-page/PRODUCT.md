# Onsite Concrete & Landscape Ad Landing Page

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: dependency-light static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript published to the exact existing Netlify landing-page site recorded in `RELEASE.md`.

## Users

Vacaville area property owners arriving from a service-specific paid search ad who need to determine whether Onsite handles their concrete or landscape project and request a consultation.

## Product Purpose

Turn qualified local search intent into estimate conversations while keeping concrete and landscaping services separately inspectable. Success means a visitor can confirm fit, review real project imagery, understand Onsite's one-team approach, and start a consultation request or phone call.

## Positioning

Onsite coordinates concrete and landscape work through one local team rather than splitting the project across disconnected subcontractors.

## Operating Context

The page is a focused Google Ads destination led by Vacaville relevance. Campaign parameters and Google click identifiers must remain intact through the conversion path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Separate concrete and landscaping intent.
- Provide phone and consultation-request paths.
- Preserve `utm_*`, `gclid`, `wbraid`, and `gbraid` values in form fields and outbound links.
- Primary measurement goal: `qualified_estimate`.
- Secondary interaction names: `estimate_start`, `contact_form`, and `phone_call`.
- Do not claim tracking is live until the deployed destination is validated.
- Do not invent prices, project timing, ratings, testimonials, warranties, awards, or outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

Use the verified Onsite wordmark and the existing red, orange-red, black, white, and metallic gray identity. The voice is direct, practical, local, and built around disciplined coordination.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified transparent Onsite logo in `assets/logo.png`.
- Public service facts: driveways, stamped and decorative concrete, patios, walkways, foundations, flatwork, landscape design and installation, drought-tolerant landscaping, irrigation, drainage, lighting, turf, retaining walls, and pavers.
- Public trust facts: serving the market since 2004, licensed and insured, one team, no subcontractors, and free consultation.
- Public contact detail: 707-628-5049.
- No approved testimonials, ratings, pricing, timelines, or performance outcomes are available for this surface.

## Product Principles

- Put Vacaville and service fit ahead of broad brand language.
- Show how one coordinated team reduces project fragmentation.
- Earn attention with real work, not unsupported superlatives.
- Preserve a short path to a phone call or consultation request.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must remain understandable without motion or JavaScript, support keyboard navigation, expose clear focus states, use descriptive image alternatives, and keep mobile actions reachable without obscuring content.
