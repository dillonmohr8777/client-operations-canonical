07THE PILOT

A focused 90-day
Phase Two pilot

Start with a small set of priorities, build the complete path, and scale only what the evidence supports.

01MONTH 1

Prioritize and build

  • Choose two or three priority programs or growth objectives.
  • Confirm audiences, offers, owners, and conversion paths.
  • Build landing pages, tracking, and follow-up workflows.
  • Prepare initial campaign assets and outreach lists.
02MONTH 2

Launch and activate

  • Launch the first campaigns across selected channels.
  • Start Ad Grants work if NKCDC is eligible and approved.
  • Activate outreach, social, email, SMS, and partners.
  • Produce the first workshop, webinar, or expert content.
03MONTH 3

Optimize and scale

  • Compare lead quality, registrations, attendance, and outcomes.
  • Improve offer, audience, creative, and follow-up.
  • Expand the strongest campaign or program.
  • Agree on the next quarterly roadmap.
THE DECISION

Choose the first two or three Phase Two priorities and define the 90-day pilot together.
