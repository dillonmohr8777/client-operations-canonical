# Align HCM weekend attribution knowledge session

Status: completed locally, read only

Freshness: 2026-07-18T01:00:59Z

## Outcome

The live HubSpot refresh completed through the authorized Align HCM route. The work remains private and no deployment, publishing, messaging, account change, or CRM write occurred.

## Current evidence

- 105 form submissions were audited across 79 unique identities.
- 22 submissions were classified as qualified buyer leads.
- 13 qualified buyer leads have logged HubSpot follow-up evidence.
- 9 qualified buyer leads have no logged HubSpot follow-up evidence; this does not prove that no contact occurred outside HubSpot.
- The qualified-lead attribution split is 1 direct AI/AEO, 2 website or content assisted, 8 temporal-only, and 11 unattributed.
- Directly attributed won revenue remains $0 in the refreshed evidence-weighted view.
- The broader CRM dashboard currently shows $10,702,720 in open pipeline and $3,675,091 in year-to-date won revenue. These are CRM totals, not marketing-sourced totals.

## Knowledge synthesis

Three approaches were compared:

1. First-touch or last-touch attribution is useful as a diagnostic, but it is not reliable here because source fields, session-level traffic, and campaign tracking are incomplete.
2. A journey evidence model separates direct, assisted, temporal, and unattributed evidence. This is the selected operating foundation because it preserves what the CRM can actually prove.
3. A conservative weighted-influence model can quantify directional influence, but its output is modeled and must remain separate from sourced pipeline or revenue.

The recommended operating model is the evidence-tiered journey model with conservative weighting shown only as a labeled secondary view. A form submission proves that the website converted the visitor; it does not by itself prove that SEO, social, content, or a specific campaign created the demand.

## Measurement gaps and controls

- The session-level traffic endpoint remains unavailable to the current private app. No value was estimated from that missing source.
- Deal and contact linkage remains incomplete, so associated-deal totals cannot be generalized to all pipeline.
- GA4 and Search Console are not part of this refresh path, leaving page-level behavior and search-demand evidence incomplete.
- Lead-level source reports remain private. This canonical summary contains aggregates only.

## Recommended next local step

Prepare a tracking-field and CRM-association remediation plan covering first touch, last touch, landing page, referrer, CTA location, content ID, campaign UTMs, and deal-to-contact linkage. Reverify the unavailable traffic scope before treating it as a usable source. Keep all provider changes approval-gated.
