It should not be reported externally as sourced pipeline because it includes a 25% temporal-correlation weight. The difference between it and the full $10,326,920 is primarily a measurement gap, not proof of zero impact.

## The production attribution model

1. **First touch:** channel that created the first known session. Use for demand creation.
2. **Lead-creation touch:** session and page that produced the first qualified form submission. Use for conversion effectiveness.
3. **Assisted touches:** documented social, organic, referral, email, and content interactions before opportunity creation. Use for influence.
4. **Opportunity-source touch:** campaign and channel active immediately before lifecycle stage changed to Opportunity. Use for pipeline sourcing.
5. **Revenue touch:** distribute won revenue with a 40/20/40 position model across first touch, assists, and opportunity-creation touch.
6. **Incrementality layer:** compare pre/post cohorts and controlled campaign periods. Attribution allocates credit; incrementality tests whether the work caused lift.

## Required tracking changes

- Add standardized UTMs to every LinkedIn/company/personal-profile post and preserve them into HubSpot contact properties.
- Fire form_viewed, form_started, form_submitted, meeting_booked, qualified_lead, and opportunity_created with page, CTA location, campaign, and content ID.
- Add hidden fields to all HubSpot forms for first-touch UTM, last-touch UTM, landing page, referrer, CTA location, and content ID.
- Require every deal to have a primary associated contact and immutable Original Marketing Source plus current Opportunity Source.
- Connect GA4 and Search Console, and grant the HubSpot private app the analytics scope required for source reports.
- Maintain the owned-work ledger with publish time, URL/post ID, author/profile, campaign, cost, and UTM.
- Report qualified pipeline, not raw contacts: qualified buyers, opportunities, open amount, won amount, sales velocity, and source-quality rate.

## Interpretation guardrail

A form submitted on the website proves the website converted the visitor. It does not prove the website or social media originally created the demand. A lead arriving after an optimization is correlation until the source, touchpoint, and timing are captured or an incrementality test shows lift.
