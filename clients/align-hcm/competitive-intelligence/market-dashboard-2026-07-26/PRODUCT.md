# Product Context

## Product

Align HCM Competitive Intelligence Dashboard

## Audience

Align HCM leadership, marketing, sales, and delivery leaders who need a fast, evidence-backed view of the HCM consulting market.

## Primary job

Show where Align is differentiated, where competitors are strongest, which market opportunities deserve attention, and what changed in the monitored public sources.

## Success criteria

• A leader can understand the competitive picture in under five minutes.

• Facts from reviewed official pages are visually separated from operator inference.

• Every competitor has a source-backed profile, a sales landmine, and a useful discovery question.

• The dashboard remains readable with JavaScript disabled.

• The weekly workflow stays local and inactive until n8n ownership and Firecrawl credentials are configured.

## Scope

This first version compares Align HCM with HRchitect, PayTech, PredictiveHR, OnActuate, and Silver Cloud HR. It uses public official pages reviewed on July 26, 2026, plus the existing local press-watchdog health state.

## Assumptions

• The dashboard is an internal decision-support artifact, not client-facing collateral.

• A capability is marked confirmed only when it appeared on one of the reviewed official pages. Absence from the matrix does not prove that a firm lacks the capability.

• Strategic priority scores are operator inference on a 0 to 100 scale, not market measurements.
