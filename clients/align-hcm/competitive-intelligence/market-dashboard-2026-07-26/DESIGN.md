# Design Context

## Visual source of truth

The dashboard extends Align HCM's existing dark intelligence-dashboard system and the observed production brand tokens.

• Page: `#071321`

• Card: `#0B1B2E`

• Primary text: `#F7F0E2`

• Align orange: `#F05A28`

• Align teal: `#27A591`

• Warning amber: `#F7C873`

• Positive green: `#4CC38A`

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and DM Sans for body copy when installed, with system fallbacks. Keep numeric KPIs in the heading family.

## Layout

Use a restrained editorial dashboard with a narrow reading column for narrative and full-width chart cards for comparison. Cards use 22 pixel radii, thin translucent borders, and generous internal space. Tables scroll inside their card on narrow screens.

## Interaction

Platform filters update competitor rows and the comparison count. Navigation anchors move between Overview, Competitors, Guidance, and Sources. All controls expose visible focus and pressed states.

## Accessibility

Maintain high contrast on the dark background, use semantic headings and tables, provide same-data chart fallbacks, preserve keyboard access, respect reduced motion, and never rely on color alone to communicate status.

## Writing

Professional and consultative. No em dashes. Clearly label confirmed facts, inference, caveats, and source dates.
