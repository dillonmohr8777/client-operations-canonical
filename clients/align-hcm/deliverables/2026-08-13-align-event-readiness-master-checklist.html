  • Current contract, sponsor kit, portal, and event manual are stored and versioned.
  • Dates, venue, footprint, included items, restrictions, and vendor contacts are confirmed.
  • Owners, approver, budget, objectives, and numeric targets are named.

  • Every production asset has factual, brand, technical, and owner approval.
