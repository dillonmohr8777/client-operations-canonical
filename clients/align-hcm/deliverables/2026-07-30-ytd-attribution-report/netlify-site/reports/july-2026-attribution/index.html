Why this report exists, and why it could not have been written last month

On July 17 deterministic attribution capture went live on alignhcm.com. Every form conversion now stores its first and last touch channel, the actual referrer, the landing page, the conversion page, and the CTA placement.

It paid for itself in under two weeks. Of the ten conversions captured since it switched on, three had been filed by HubSpot as Direct Traffic when they were nothing of the kind. Two came from search engines and one came straight from ChatGPT. That ChatGPT lead is now an open deal. Before July 17 all three would have been invisible.

Why the rep-sourced rows are separated out

Sixty nine of July's records were created by the team rather than by the website: a rep saving a prospect with the browser extension, a contact typed into the CRM, or the call transcript integration. Those carry no traffic source at all by design.

Keeping them separate is what makes the rest of this report trustworthy. Marketing is credited with exactly 9 contacts in July, and not one of them is borrowed from the sales engine. Every figure that follows is a marketing number.

Organic is the highest quality channel we have

One in four organic search contacts has gone on to produce a deal, against roughly one in eighteen for direct traffic. Organic is also the only channel that has ever produced a Customer.

So four to six organic leads a month is worth considerably more than the raw count suggests, and it is the number most worth growing.

What organic closes is SmartCare

The one organic-sourced win in company history was a SmartCare engagement at $54,000, and the organic deal open on the board today is SmartCare as well.

Organic buyers arrive already owning a platform. Leading with support and optimization is what converts, and July's nine inquiries say exactly the same thing.

Speed is the win condition

That organic win closed in 47 days, comfortably inside a normal cycle. Buyers who arrive with a live operational problem move fast when we move fast.

July's best example: an organic lead arrived at 14:46 and was assigned and contacted twice by 18:40 the same afternoon.

AI is a real channel now

Three of July's nine came from ChatGPT, confirmed by referrer rather than inferred. That is more AI referred contacts than the previous six months combined.

One of them landed straight on the Dayforce implementation page and another on the fractional assistance page. The assistant is surfacing deep service pages, not just the brand, and the fractional one became a deal.

The July milestone: an AI sourced deal, the first of its kind

The fractional payroll inquiry that arrived from ChatGPT on July 20 became an open deal in the UKG pipeline on July 28. It is the first deal in company history traceable to an AI assistant, and it only exists in the record as AI sourced because the capture went live nine days before it arrived.

Two honest notes on it: no deal value has been entered yet, so there is no dollar figure to quote, and it currently sits at Expressing Interest, a stage this portal flags as closed, which is why it does not appear in an open pipeline total. Worth entering an amount and confirming the stage.

What “Offline Sources” means — and how we fix it

The Offline bucket is not one broken marketing channel. It is the CRM’s record-entry taxonomy: integrations, imports, prospecting extensions, manual creation, meetings, and connected inboxes. GA4 improves the future journey; it cannot manufacture historical browser evidence.

The right correction is additive, not destructive

Keep HubSpot’s native Original Traffic Source untouched for auditability. Add a normalized reporting field beside it: Attributed Discovery Channel, plus Attribution Confidence and Attribution Evidence. A workflow may populate those fields when referrer, UTM, click ID, social platform, or self-reported source provides deterministic proof.

This preserves the truth of how a record entered HubSpot while still giving marketing an accurate channel view.

STEP 1 · LIVE

Capture every forward touch

First and last UTM, referrer, landing page, social platform, click IDs, conversion type, content and CTA are now written at conversion.

STEP 2 · HUBSPOT

Normalize beside the native source

Create the reporting channel, confidence and evidence properties. Never overwrite Original Traffic Source.

STEP 3 · WORKFLOW

Recover only what can be proved

Classify Google/Bing, LinkedIn and AI assistants from deterministic values. Leave every unsupported record unresolved.

STEP 4 · REVENUE

Carry evidence into the deal

Require a primary contact and copy the opportunity-source fields at deal creation so attribution survives through Closed Won.

What changes now that GA4 is live

From July 30 forward, GA4 supplies the behavioral layer: landing pages, engaged sessions, content depth, CTA clicks, meeting starts, form success, and assisted paths. HubSpot remains the revenue system of record. The join is the campaign/referrer/click evidence stored on the contact — not a rewrite of historical Offline values.
