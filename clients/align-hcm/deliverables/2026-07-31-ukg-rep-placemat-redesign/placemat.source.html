Objection handling, quick reference

“Why is the assessment free?”
Align invests in the QOA because it makes them a better implementation partner. They go in with their eyes open instead of discovering surprises mid-project. That saves everyone time and money.
“What's the catch?”
No catch. If the assessment says stay put, they'll shake hands and walk away. But every QOA they've run has surfaced 3 to 5 high-impact opportunities the customer didn't know they were leaving on the table.
“We already have support.”
SmartCare isn't generic support. It's a dedicated team that knows the customer's configuration because they built it. The 60-day inclusive period lets them experience the difference before committing.
“Why only 60 days?”
60 days covers the hyper-care window where 80% of post-go-live issues surface. Most customers convert to a paid subscription because they see the ROI by Day 30.
“We're not ready for a full project.”
That's exactly the right time for a QOA. It's a zero-risk way to build the business case and get clarity on scope, timeline, and budget before any commitment.
