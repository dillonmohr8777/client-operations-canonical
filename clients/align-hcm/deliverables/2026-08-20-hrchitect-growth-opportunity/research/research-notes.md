## Technical AI search audit

Verified 2026-08-20:

- `robots.txt` returns 200 and does not disallow crawling.
- Yoast sitemap index returns 200 and includes post, page, careers, event, leadership, category, tag, industry, platform, service, and author sitemaps.
- Homepage, UKG, Paylocity, and Dayforce pages return 200, include canonical URLs, one H1, and usable title and description tags.
- Commercial platform pages expose baseline WebPage, WebSite, Organization, BreadcrumbList, and ImageObject schema.
- No `llms.txt` was found at the conventional root path. This is optional and not a ranking requirement.
- The sampled commercial pages did not expose Service or FAQPage schema. This is an opportunity to improve explicit service entity definition and answer extraction where page content supports it.
- The platform pages contain strong proof and service breadth, but their answer architecture can be made more extractable with direct definitions, comparison tables, process steps, FAQ modules, named expert authorship, and source-backed statistics.

## Recommended operating model

1. Preserve the commercial pages that already rank and expand them with direct answer blocks, proof, and internal links.
2. Build one authoritative content hub per qualified cluster rather than publishing thin variations.
3. Pair commercial service pages with decision content, checklists, comparison pages, and implementation risk content.
4. Add supported Service and FAQPage schema, expert authorship, reviewed dates, and transparent citations.
5. Track classic rankings, AI Overview citations, Semrush AI visibility, Search Console query movement, and qualified CRM outcomes together.
6. Use HubSpot and terminal agents to enforce metadata, links, schema, refresh cadence, and reporting without removing human editorial control.
