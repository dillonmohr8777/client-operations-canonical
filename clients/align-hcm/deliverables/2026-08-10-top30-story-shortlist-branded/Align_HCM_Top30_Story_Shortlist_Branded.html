How this was built

Verbatim from the shortlist build notes

Source & scope

Built from Client_Story_Database_v8.xlsx, Stories tab, rows 1-459 (453 of 483 engagements = 94%). The final ~30 rows and 4 of the 5 worksheets were not readable through the Microsoft 365 connector, so this shortlist may shift once those rows are included.

Deal values cross-verified live against HubSpot portal 242825734 on 10 Aug 2026. Read-only: nothing was created, changed or sent.

One correction you should apply to the source workbook

The Stories tab Deal Size ($) column sums raw HubSpot "amount", not "amount_in_home_currency". Because Align books in USD and CAD, every Canadian engagement is overstated — by 36.7% relative to true USD. Portfolio-wide that is $56,512,040 raw vs $54,266,143 USD, a $2,245,897 overstatement, 99.7% of it in the 104 CAD deals.

Values in this file are restated. Canadian rows are discounted at the verified blended rate 0.7316. Re-derive the source column from amount_in_home_currency to fix it permanently.

Also check deal 320839688918 (Burnco SmartCare 2026) by hand: it is coded USD but converted at a CAD rate, so a currency-code-based fix will miss it.

Selection method

Score = 45% deal value (log-scaled, true USD) + 30% recency + 25% narrative readiness (has narrative / has quote).

Constraints: one story per client; max 4 per industry; max 7 per size band; $50k minimum; at least 4 non-UKG-Pro stories so Dayforce and UKG Ready are represented.

Client names were de-duplicated first — the source splits some clients across spellings (KCC / Kimberly-Clark / Kimberly Clark UTA; REI / Recreational Equipment Inc; EOS Hospitality across three names). 10 merges applied.

What the shortlist needs from you

20 of 30 already have a drafted narrative and 16 have a Raven-sourced quote — so the net new writing is 10 narratives and 14 quotes, not 30.

Reference Status is blank on all 453 source rows. The Vocab tab already defines the right ladder (None / Logo OK / Quote OK / Reference Call OK / Logo+Quote OK / Logo+Quote+Reference Call OK) — it just has never been filled in. Until it is, none of these are cleared to name publicly.

The Validator column is blank on purpose. Mike's objective names Maher, Tammi, Rich, Moe, Sneh, Sarah and Allison — but only Maher and Allison map to CRM deal owners, and the Engagement Owner column that would connect stories to the delivery team is empty on every row. That mapping needs a human.

Unblocking the other 421 engagements

The How to Use tab has the fix: in OneDrive, right-click the sales SOW folders, choose "Always keep on this device", then re-run the narrative pass. 12 of the 62 drafts are low-confidence purely because their SOWs were cloud-only stubs.
