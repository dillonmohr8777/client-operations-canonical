# Align HCM addressable competitor map

Date checked: 2026-08-20

## Scope

This is the major addressable set for a fast, serious first campaign. It includes direct firms that sell overlapping implementation, optimization, managed support, integration, and post launch HCM services, plus major platform companies whose in house growth teams and partner ecosystems are strategically adjacent. It is intentionally not described as every HR technology vendor in existence.

## Evidence standard

Decision makers were accepted only where a current official leadership page, official company post, or visible LinkedIn profile supported the company and role. A personal email was not inferred from a domain pattern. Official general inboxes are included only when the company publicly posted them. Records without a verified inbox are marked LinkedIn first.

The installed LinkedIn outreach companion is designed for one confirmed job application at a time. It is not a bulk scraping or mass outreach bot. Public LinkedIn URLs were therefore researched and verified individually, and no connection request or message was sent.
