What does SmartCare support after HCM go-live?

SmartCare gives HR, payroll, and workforce teams ongoing HCM support after implementation. Align HCM specialists stabilize issues, maintain configuration, improve reporting and integrations, transfer knowledge, and operate payroll, HRIS, or workforce-management processes on the platform the organization already uses every day.

→ Since go-live, your team has taken ownership of the system but would benefit from expert partnership to drive efficiency and best practices.
→ Many of the features you've invested in haven't been fully leveraged yet, creating an opportunity for optimization.
→ Today, key activities like payroll processing and reconciliation are happening outside the system using multiple spreadsheets, while available BI capabilities remain underutilized.
→ There is a level of dependency on individual team members that could affect consistency and continuity over time.
→ Planning and executing for critical deadlines like open enrollment, ACA, and year-end processing is an annual challenge.
→ You're seeking guidance on where to start within a specific module and the right questions to ask.
