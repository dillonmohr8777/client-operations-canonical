## What we found

OpenAI's self-serve Ads Manager is live at ads.openai.com (beta rollout expanded May 2026). Ads appear to logged-in adult users on Free and Go tiers, clearly labeled and visually separated from ChatGPT's answer. Ads do not change what ChatGPT says. Advertisers get aggregate performance data only.

For a realtor, the channel fits because people research agents and markets in conversation before they call. Intent signals that matter:

- Neighborhood and town research (buyer intent)
- Home valuation questions (seller intent)
- Mortgage and financing questions (early buyer)
- Agent selection and vetting questions (near-move intent)

There are no classic keywords and no demographic targeting. You win with context hints (plain-language descriptions of conversations where the ad belongs), relevance, and a landing page that answers the question first.

Early market reports put CPCs roughly in the $2.50-$8 range (some realtor roundups cite about $3-$5). No minimum spend. Treat those numbers as directional and re-verify inside Ads Manager at setup. The auction and formats are still changing.

## Launch considerations

1. **WordPress admin first.** Nothing launches without editable pages and working capture.
2. **Four pages live** with tappable phone, two-field form, and mobile-tested capture before spend.
3. **Staging hygiene.** Title currently reads "Home - WordPress Blog." Fix or noindex until ready.
4. **Ads Manager account** under Deborah's or the brokerage's business identity. Complete account info, logo, billing, and business verification. Campaigns will not deliver until those steps are done.
5. **UTMs on every URL** (e.g. utm_source=chatgpt, utm_medium=cpc, utm_campaign=ad_group). Prefer dedicated landing paths so traffic stays separable.
6. **Attribution.** Real estate cycles are long. Track cost per genuine conversation, not vanity clicks. Use a "How did you hear about us?" field. Plan for a long attribution window (industry guidance often points to ~12 months).
7. **Budget pacing.** Start about $20-$30/day, US only, one ad group for ~14 days. Improve relevance before raising bids. Do not turn on all four groups at once.
8. **Sequence:** Selling, then 55+ / downsizing, then Choosing an agent, then Buying.
9. **Creative.** Information-dense, helpful, never pushy. Local listing photography, not mismatched stock. Hard-sell and misleading creative get rejected or underperform.
10. **Broker review.** RE/MAX Homeland REALTORS should sign off on copy and Fair Housing before launch.

## Best practices especially for realtors

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Match ads to conversational intent (sell, value, 55+, choose agent) | Bid like Google keyword campaigns |
| Send clicks to the matching answer page | Send ChatGPT traffic to a generic homepage |
| Lead with value ("what homes are going for in central NJ") | Lead with "#1 team" hard sell |
| Localize by county / town context in hints and copy | Use national fluff that could be anyone |
| Keep CTAs low-friction (valuation, checklist, consult) | Ask for an exclusive agreement from a cold click |
| Use verified proof (homes.com 5.0/111, closed volume) | Invent ratings or unverified view counts |
| Reference genuine age-restricted communities for 55+ | Steer or describe "who a neighborhood is for" |

Suggested ad-group context themes (write full conversation descriptions at setup):

- **Selling:** timing, list prep, what it costs to sell in NJ, Monmouth / Ocean / Mercer sellers
- **Home value:** online estimate vs real CMA, what my house is worth
- **55+ / downsizing:** active adult communities in central NJ (age-restricted only)
- **Choosing an agent:** questions to ask before signing; comparing agents in central NJ
- **Buying:** towns and process in Monmouth, Ocean, Mercer (after seller groups prove out)

## Fair Housing reminder

US real estate advertising falls under the Fair Housing Act. Copy must never suggest a preference or steer by race, color, religion, sex, familial status, national origin, or disability. ChatGPT Ads' lack of demographic targeting removes one class of targeting risk, but copy still carries the duty.

The 55+ material is lawful only when it refers to genuinely age-restricted communities under the Housing for Older Persons exception. Do not extend that language to general neighborhoods. Broker review before live.
