Your current staging site cannot convert that click. It has no form, no tappable phone, no email link, and 449 words. Phase 2 — context hints, not keywords

Four ad groups, in this order: Selling, then 55+ / downsizing, then Choosing an agent, then Buying. Write the conversations where the ad belongs. Purchase-intent language (best, buy, compare, price) raises the odds a card is shown.

Phase 3 — chat cards

Information-dense, helpful, never pushy. Use your listing photography or genuine local photos. Not stock that does not look like central New Jersey.

Distinct UTM on every ChatGPT ad. Dedicated landing paths. GA4 generate_lead on every form submit. A “How did you hear about us?” field. Track cost per genuine conversation, not cost per click.
