## Stack

Delegated by Dillon: dependency-light static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so the proof of concept can be reviewed locally, hosted anywhere, and later connected to a warehouse without committing the client to a framework.

## Users

- Confirmed pilot user: the Puttery NYC operator or account owner who needs to validate marketing, reservation, visit, event, and realized-revenue measurement before approving production integration.
- Inferred secondary users: Momentum Digital and DM Marketing Specialist team members diagnosing attribution and deciding where to allocate paid media.

## Product Purpose

Create a reviewable Puttery NYC attribution pilot workspace spanning the owned website, Tock, Toast, Tripleseat, GA4, Google Ads, and Meta, with Resy and Eventbrite retained as portfolio expansion lanes. Success means an operator can inspect the dated operational state, understand the evidence route, answer every integration blocker, export the discovery record, distinguish modeled from verified outcomes, and leave with an explicit go or blocked decision.

## Positioning

The product treats source integrity as part of the interface. Every metric carries its source, freshness, and confidence, while the identity spine makes incomplete cross-platform joins visible instead of presenting false precision.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show portfolio and venue-level performance without combining unknown brands or unsupported totals.
- Show marketing spend, booking value, realized revenue, event pipeline, match rate, and data freshness only when their definitions and source state are explicit.
- Support a staged integration path: first-party click capture and UTMs, platform exports, partner APIs and webhooks where authorized, normalized warehouse tables, and offline conversion feedback.
- Never imply that modeled demonstration data is live client performance.
- No scraping, credential bypass, consent bypass, or platform-policy evasion. API gaps are handled through authorized exports, partner access, first-party instrumentation, and reconciliation.

## Product Principles

1. Truth before totals: provenance, freshness, and confidence travel with every KPI.
2. One venue at a time: preserve brand and location boundaries before rolling up the portfolio.
3. Revenue closes the loop: separate booked value, collected revenue, refunds, and event pipeline.
4. Progressive certainty: improve from directional click evidence to deterministic joins without hiding the gap.
5. The dashboard ends in action: surface the next instrumentation or budget decision, not only charts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web proof of concept must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive layouts, readable contrast, reduced motion, and non-color-only status cues.
