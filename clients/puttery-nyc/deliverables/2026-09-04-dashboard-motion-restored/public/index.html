The pilot must answer three business questions

Every chart, integration, and data request exists to support one of these decisions.

BOOKING

Which campaigns create real Tock reservations?

Separate clicks and checkout starts from completed bookings, then account for edits, transfers, cancellations, and refunds.

VISIT

Which reservations become completed Puttery visits?

Keep reservation creation and service completion as separate conversion events so paid media is not optimized toward future cancellations.

REVENUE

What collected Toast revenue can be joined exactly?

Report exact matches, platform-level outcomes, and unmatched value separately. Never turn a timing guess into a person-level match.

Truth rule

Tock booked value, Tripleseat event value, deposits, and Toast sales describe different financial facts. They are never added into one revenue total.

Match confidence ladder

  1. Exact platform IDReservation, transaction, event, order, or check identifier survives the handoff.
  2. Approved deterministic identityConsented, normalized identity is transformed and joined under an approved privacy design.
  3. Platform sourceThe vendor confirms source or campaign at aggregate grain, without a person-level join.
  4. UnmatchedThe outcome remains visible but is excluded from exact attribution and media optimization.
