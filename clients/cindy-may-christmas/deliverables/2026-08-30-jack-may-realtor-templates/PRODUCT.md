# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so the review concept remains portable, fast to inspect, and independent of the current IDX platform.

## Users

The primary visitor is a Louisville-area home buyer, seller, or rental-property owner evaluating Jack May and May Team Realtors. The visitor needs a clear route to search listings, estimate a home's value, understand property-management support, or contact Jack.

## Product Purpose

Create a private cinematic homepage concept that demonstrates how May Team's existing services and real Jack May identity could be presented with stronger hierarchy, motion, and trust. Success means a first-time visitor understands who Jack is, what May Team helps with, and the next action within seconds.

## Positioning

Jack May is the broker-owner directly accountable for a connected buy, sell, and property-management journey. The page makes that guidance visible as one continuous sequence instead of a crowded IDX dashboard.

## Operating Context

This is a noindex public review concept referred through Cindy May. Jack May is not registered as a canonical client. The current May Team Realtors website remains the source of truth for service names, contact information, live listing search, forms, and legal or IDX functionality.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Link to the current May Team listing search, home-value estimator, property-management page, contact page, and Jack May profile.
- Use the existing cinematic concept-property film as an interaction demonstration.
- Preserve a usable static poster and reduced-motion experience.
- Do not imply that the generated château is a real May Team listing.
- Do not invent listings, testimonials, sales figures, awards, certifications beyond current source wording, or performance claims.
- Do not reproduce or simulate IDX inventory locally.
- Keep all links and contact details traceable to the current May Team website.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the exact May Team Realtors logo artwork, its black and red identity, and the Jack May name.
- Present the dark source logo on a deliberate light brand plate when contrast requires it; never redraw, pixelate, recolor, or place it as an accidental white-box paste.
- Use a dark, restrained property-film world with May Team red as the action accent.
- Use the Signal reference's self-hosted Unbounded, Archivo, and Manrope families.
- Use authored CSS or SVG geometry instead of emoji or Unicode glyphs for interface icons.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current brokerage site: https://www.mayteamrealtors.com/.
- Current Jack profile: https://www.mayteamrealtors.com/jackmay.
- Current service routes for listings, home value, buyers, property management, and contact.
- Exact locally captured May Team logo and Jack May portrait under the cinematic site's assets/jack folder.
- Prior authenticated source review and three dated concept builds under this project.
- No verified Jack biography beyond his broker-owner title and current contact/profile copy.

## Product Principles

- Put one real person and one clear next action ahead of interface volume.
- Let the property film demonstrate attention and sequence without pretending it is inventory.
- Reuse current May Team language and destinations rather than inventing a parallel brokerage.
- Keep buying, selling, valuation, and property management as distinct routes inside one coherent journey.
- Treat public review, current production, and future client approval as separate states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast intent, touch layouts from 320px upward, reduced motion, semantic section structure, and descriptive alternative text.
