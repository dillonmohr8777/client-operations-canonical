## What all the expensive ones share

1. One photograph or film. Not a collage. Not a generated night plate.
2. Almost no UI on the first screen. Four links or a hamburger. Search is secondary or hidden.
3. The boast is off-market, 100M+, exclusive, or inquire. Not Buy / Sell / Manage cards.
4. Serif wordmark. Thin white type. Gold or cream if there is metal.
5. The house, the view, or the street is the brand. The agent is a signature, not a target graphic.

## What this means for Jack

If the next draft is supposed to feel rich, stop mixing newspaper chrome, orbit portraits, and glass cards.

Steal the 111 West 57th / Kirman / Beverly Hills Estates first screen: one real Louisville estate or Highlands street, a serif May Team mark, four links, and one Inquire or View listings button. Keep May Team red as a single accent, not a civic theme.

Do not copy Beverly Hills costume onto the copy. Copy the emptiness.
