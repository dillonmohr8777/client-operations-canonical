## What to steal

The winning realtor pattern in 2026 is almost always the same:

1. One full-bleed photograph or quiet video of a real place.
2. Thin transparent nav. Five links or fewer.
3. One job in the first screen: Search homes, plus a secondary Home value or Call.
4. The agent is present as a person, not as a graphic device.
5. Buy, sell, and manage are routes, not a newspaper or a tech dashboard.

Do not copy Beverly Hills costume onto Louisville. Copy the structure.

## Recommended next build

One site, not three concepts.

- Direction: Coughtry + Hatef + Kumara. Kentucky-team usefulness with Luxury Presence calm.
- Hero: a real Louisville house or Highlands street, not a generated night plate and not a newspaper.
- First actions: Search homes, What's my home worth, Call Jack.
- Secondary: Buyers, Sellers, Property management, Jack, Answers.
- Jack appears once as an uncropped portrait. Logo is an end-card or header mark, never a particle stunt on the first screen.
- Keep May Team red. Drop civic-newspaper chrome, target orbits, and stacked glass lenses.
