## The pattern

1. The first thing is a house. Lawn, porch, pool, driveway, or rooms people use.
2. The house can be 6,000 or 11,000 feet. It still looks like a home.
3. Search, home value, or View listings sits on the photo. Not Buy / Sell / Manage cards.
4. No newspaper. No orbit headshot. No glass widget stack. No empty black field.
