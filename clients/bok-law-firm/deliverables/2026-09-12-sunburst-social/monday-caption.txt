Monday Reset: one small step is still a step. ☀️

Some weeks arrive with a very long list. You do not have to sort it all out today.

Choose one thing you can realistically finish: put an appointment on the calendar, gather one document you need, or write down a question for your next conversation.

Make it specific. Give yourself a little time. Then notice what you did get done.

A fresh start can be small.
