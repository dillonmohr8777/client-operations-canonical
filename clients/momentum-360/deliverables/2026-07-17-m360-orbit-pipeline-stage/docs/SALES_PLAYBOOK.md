# M360 Orbit Sales Playbook

## The 30-second pitch

> M360 Orbit is Momentum 360's AI marketing command center. A business owner asks one real question, Orbit routes it to the right one of 19 specialists, and returns a prioritized plan built around Momentum's operating playbooks. If the work needs a shoot, campaign build, CRM workflow, or human review, the same Momentum team can pick it up. It is the speed of software with a real agency behind it.

## The one-line version

> Nineteen AI marketing specialists, one command center, and a human team when it is time to execute.

## Discovery questions

1. What marketing work keeps getting pushed to nights, weekends, or “next week”?
2. Where does your team wait the longest for an answer or first draft?
3. Which channel creates the most revenue today?
4. Which channel creates the most confusion?
5. What happens after a lead comes in?
6. What company context does your team repeat in every brief?
7. What would a useful result from Orbit look like in the first 30 days?
8. Who will own reviewing and implementing the output?

## Qualification score

Score each 0-2:

- Recurring marketing workload
- Clear internal owner
- Existing data/context
- Measurable growth goal
- Need for cross-channel coordination
- Interest in Momentum execution

9-12: Managed/Growth candidate. 6-8: Core candidate. Below 6: keep in Preview nurture.

## Demo flow

### 1. Frame the job — 3 minutes

Use the prospect's words. Do not start by touring features.

> “You said the team has traffic but cannot tell why qualified calls are flat. Let us put that exact job into Orbit.”

### 2. Route live — 3 minutes

Submit the question with “Let Orbit route it” selected. Confirm the assigned agent makes sense.

### 3. Inspect the output — 7 minutes

Ask:

- Is the priority order right?
- Which action is new?
- Which action could the team complete this week?
- Where would a human handoff help?

### 4. Add company context — 5 minutes

Explain how paid onboarding adds offers, audiences, voice, proof, systems, and constraints so the answer stops being generic.

### 5. Show the workflow — 5 minutes

Map the output into the prospect's real process: HubSpot, content calendar, ad review, reporting, or project handoff.

### 6. Recommend one package — 5 minutes

Do not present every tier as equally valid.

- Core: one owner, one brand, mostly self-serve.
- Growth: team collaboration and workflow handoff.
- Managed: company wants accountability and Momentum execution.

### 7. Close — 2 minutes

> “The right next step is a 30-day paid pilot around one workflow. If Orbit cannot create useful work fast enough to justify the plan, we stop. If it does, we keep the founding price for 12 months.”

## Objection handling

### “We already use ChatGPT.”

> Keep it. Orbit is not asking you to replace a general model. It adds the missing operating layer: routing, specialist scopes, saved company context, repeatable workflows, and a direct handoff to people who can execute.

### “Why is this $149 if other tools are under $20?”

> Those products sell broad model access. Orbit is priced against the time and coordination it removes, plus the value of Momentum's playbooks and execution path. If it does not consistently recover more than $149 of capacity, the pilot should not continue.

### “Will the AI make things up?”

> Any model can be wrong. Orbit narrows the job, states assumptions, prohibits invented claims, and keeps a human handoff visible. We also test routing and output quality against a fixed evaluation set.

### “I do not want my client data training a model.”

> The public preview tells users not to submit confidential data and is configured not to store the model response. A paid deployment should not ingest private material until the data policy, access rules, and approved knowledge sources are documented with you.

### “Will this replace our marketing person?”

> Orbit replaces blank pages, repeated briefs, and waiting. Your marketing person still owns judgment, approvals, relationships, and execution. It gives them a faster first pass and a coordinated roster.
