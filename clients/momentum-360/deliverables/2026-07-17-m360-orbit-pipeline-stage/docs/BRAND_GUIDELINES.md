# M360 Orbit Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea

M360 Orbit turns the “360” in Momentum into a product behavior: it sees the whole field, routes the signal, and identifies the next move.

The brand must feel like a calm command center, not a science-fiction toy and not a generic chatbot.

## Name and tagline

- Master product name: **M360 Orbit**
- Short name after first reference: **Orbit**
- Parent brand: **Momentum 360**
- Primary tagline: **Every angle. One next move.**
- Product descriptor: **19 specialist AI marketing agents. One command center.**

Do not rename the company or alter the corporate Momentum 360 logo.

## Logo system

### Corporate mark

Use `site/assets/momentum-360-logo.png` exactly as supplied.

- Display it inside a circle so the white image corners do not become a square tile on dark surfaces.
- Minimum digital size: 40 px.
- Clear space: at least 25% of the mark's diameter on all sides.
- Do not redraw the “m,” recolor the watercolor fill, remove the gold arrow, stretch the mark, add shadows inside it, or place type over it.

### Product lockup

The product lockup combines the unmodified corporate mark with:

```
M360 ORBIT
by Momentum 360
```

“M360” is white or deep navy. “ORBIT” is Signal Gold. The parent-brand line uses wide uppercase tracking at a smaller size.

### Orbit motif

Orbit rings, trajectory lines, nodes, and routing pulses may surround the corporate mark. They are product graphics, not changes to the logo.

Use the motif to show:

- A question entering the system
- Routing to one specialist
- Multiple coordinated disciplines
- Movement toward a measurable next action

Avoid random starfields, robot mascots, floating chat bubbles, neon overload, and “AI brain” imagery.

## Typography

### Display

**Space Grotesk**, weights 500-700.

Use for headlines, callsigns, navigation, prices, numbers, and system labels.

### Body

**DM Sans**, weights 400-700.

Use for paragraphs, forms, explanations, support text, and UI detail.

## Agent naming system

Callsigns make the roster memorable while service roles keep it understandable.

| Squad | Callsigns |
|---|---|
| Capture & Showcase | VERA, LENS, AERO, FRAME, WAVE |
| Get Found | ATLAS, MAPS, SIGNAL, GRID |
| Win Customers | PULSE, HOOK, PIN, REVIVE, ECHO, STUDIO |
| Content & Operations | FORGE, PRISM, SCOPE, PATCH |

Always pair a callsign with a plain-language role on first view: **ATLAS — AI Search & SEO Strategist**.

Do not present the callsigns as people. They are product modules with a defined job.

## Layout and UI language

- Use deep navy for command surfaces and Field Paper for explanation surfaces.
- Use generous negative space and large type instead of dense dashboards.
- Use thin orbit lines, nodes, and small uppercase labels to create system character.
- Cards use 14-18 px corner radii. Buttons use 8 px corner radii.
- Primary buttons are gold on navy or gold with navy text.
- Every AI response must show the assigned callsign, role, squad, and live/starter mode.
- Every response must offer two next steps: ask again or hand off to a human.

## Imagery

Preferred imagery:

- Real locations, spaces, teams, shoots, screens, and work in progress
- Aerial context, immersive views, and before/after proof
- Cropped details that show craft
- System diagrams derived from routes, fields, and orbits

Avoid:

- Humanoid robots
- Generic glowing brains
- Stock-photo handshakes
- Random blue-purple gradients unrelated to Momentum colors
- Fake dashboards with unreadable data

## Voice

### Attributes

- Clear
- Capable
- Grounded
- Energetic
- Direct

### Formula

**Signal → specialist → reason → action → success measure**

### Examples

On-brand:

> Your Google Business Profile is not a profile problem. It is a proof-and-response problem. MAPS would fix these three signals first.

> One question entered Orbit. ATLAS found the search job. Here is the seven-day move.

Off-brand:

> Unleash the limitless power of our revolutionary AI army!

> Our cutting-edge proprietary algorithms guarantee explosive growth.

## Core copy

- Hero: **Every angle. One next move.**
- Subhead: **19 specialist agents, one command center, and a human team when it is time to execute.**
- CTA: **Ask Orbit free**
- Roster heading: **Nineteen specialists. Four coordinated squads.**
- Pricing heading: **Start with a question. Grow into a system.**
- Handoff CTA: **Hand this to a human**

## Accessibility

- Minimum body text size: 15 px.
- Minimum contrast target: WCAG AA.
- All motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Do not rely on color alone for agent state.
- Every form control needs a visible label.
- Focus states use Signal Cyan with a 3 px outline.
- Orbit graphics are decorative unless they communicate state; decorative graphics must be hidden from assistive technology.

## Governance

The product owner approves changes to name, tagline, tier names, and agent callsigns. Design may evolve layout and motion without changing the corporate mark. Any new claim about customer results must be traceable to measured evidence and approval.
