06 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

What owners ask first.

Orbit should earn trust before it asks for a subscription.

Is this just ChatGPT with a Momentum logo?+

No. Orbit first routes your problem to a specialist with a defined scope and Momentum operating playbook. The model provides the reasoning power; the routing, constraints, brand context, and human handoff make it a product.

Which specialist should I choose?+

You do not have to. Ask in plain English and Orbit will route the job. If you already know the exact discipline, choose a callsign to send the request directly.

Will it use my conversations to expose client information?+

The public preview tells you not to submit confidential data, and the backend is configured not to store model responses. Paid onboarding should include a written data-handling policy and access controls before private client context is added.

What happens when the work needs a person?+

That is the point of the Momentum 360 handoff. Strategy and first drafts move instantly; shoots, campaign builds, CRM work, and specialized execution can move to the human team.

Why pay when inexpensive AI tools exist?+

Low-cost tools sell model access. Orbit sells a faster path from a business problem to a specialist plan, saved company context, a connected workflow, and accountable human execution.
