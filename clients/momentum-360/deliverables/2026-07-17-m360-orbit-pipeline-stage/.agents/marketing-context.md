# M360 Orbit Marketing Context

Status: V1 auto-draft from the live app, Momentum 360 public site, and Momentum Slack evidence on July 9, 2026.

## 1. Product overview

- **One-line description:** M360 Orbit is a 19-specialist AI marketing command center trained on Momentum 360 operating playbooks and backed by a real human agency.
- **What it does:** A business owner describes a marketing problem in plain English. Orbit routes the job to the right specialist, applies a focused playbook, and returns an actionable plan with a measurable next move. Paid versions add saved business context, team access, workflow integrations, and human support.
- **Category:** Service-enabled AI marketing software.
- **Product type:** Lead-generating public app evolving into a subscription SaaS plus managed service.
- **Business model:** Free preview → $149/month Core → $399/month Growth → $1,250/month Managed plus onboarding.

## 2. Target audience

- **Primary ICP:** U.S. location-based service businesses with $1M-$20M in annual revenue, 5-100 employees, and meaningful marketing activity but no complete in-house marketing department.
- **Beachhead verticals:** Home services, real estate and property-adjacent businesses, venues, restaurants, retail, professional services, and multi-location local brands.
- **Decision-makers:** Founder/owner, president, VP of marketing, marketing manager, and sales leader.
- **Primary use case:** Turn a live marketing problem into a credible, prioritized plan without waiting days for an agency or learning prompt engineering.
- **Jobs to be done:** Decide what to do next; create the first usable draft; diagnose a marketing leak; coordinate work across channels; hand execution to a human specialist.

## 4. Problems and pain points

- Marketing is postponed until nights and weekends.
- Generic AI produces plausible but unfocused output.
- Different channels create conflicting priorities.
- Agencies can be slow for small, iterative work.
- The owner cannot tell which activity will move revenue first.
- A plan often dies because there is no clear path from advice to execution.

## 5. Competitive landscape

- **Direct:** Hostinger Agents, AI marketing-team products, and vertical multi-agent workspaces.
- **Secondary:** HighLevel AI Employee, HubSpot Breeze Agents, Vendasta AI Workforce, Jasper, and general AI subscriptions.
- **Indirect:** Traditional agency retainers, freelancers, DIY templates, disconnected point tools, and doing nothing.
- **Core gap:** Most low-cost tools sell access to AI. Most agencies sell human time. Orbit connects specialist software, proprietary agency context, and human execution.

## 6. Differentiation

1. Nineteen narrow specialists rather than one general assistant.
2. Deterministic intent routing before model generation.
3. Momentum 360 playbooks from a working agency serving location-based businesses.
4. Native strengths in immersive media, local visibility, and demand generation.
5. A direct handoff to the team that can perform the shoot, build, campaign, or CRM work.
6. Output designed around an owner, action, deadline, and success measure.

## 7. Objections and anti-personas

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “I already have ChatGPT.” | ChatGPT is a model interface. Orbit adds routing, specialist scopes, business context, playbooks, saved work, integrations, and human execution. |
| “AI tools are cheap.” | Core model access is cheap; reliable context, workflow, and accountable execution are the paid value. |
| “I do not trust AI with client data.” | The public preview prohibits confidential data and disables model-response storage. Paid onboarding must include role-based access and a written data policy. |
| “Will this replace my team?” | Orbit removes blank-page and coordination work so the team can spend more time deciding, producing, and selling. |

Anti-personas: companies looking for fully autonomous marketing with no human owner; buyers unwilling to provide business context; heavily regulated deployments before security and compliance controls are completed; price-only shoppers comparing raw model subscriptions.

## 8. Switching dynamics

- **Push:** Slow agency queues, scattered tools, repeated briefs, inconsistent output, unclear priorities.
- **Pull:** One command center, fast specialist plans, saved context, and human execution.
- **Habit:** Opening a general chatbot, sending another email to the agency, or delaying the work.
- **Anxiety:** Accuracy, privacy, brand voice, integration effort, and fear that the product is only a branded wrapper.

## 10. Brand voice

- **Attributes:** Clear, capable, energetic, grounded, direct.
- **Style:** Short sentences, strong verbs, visible assumptions, ranked actions, minimal jargon.
- **Do:** Name the problem, show the route, explain the reason, end with a next move.
- **Do not:** Hype the model, invent proof, overuse futurist language, or bury the action in long explanation.

## 11. Style guide
