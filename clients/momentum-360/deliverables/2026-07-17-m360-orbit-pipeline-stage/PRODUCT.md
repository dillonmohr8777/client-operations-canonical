# M360 Orbit Deep

## Product

M360 Orbit Deep is a web command center that turns one local-business marketing job into a decision-grade plan owned by the right specialist.

## Users

- Primary: local-business owners and marketing teams who need a concrete next move.
- Secondary: Momentum 360 operators who need a structured intake, evidence state, agent handoffs, approval gate, and execution-ready brief.

## Product promise

One job is routed to a primary specialist from a fixed 19-agent roster. When the work spans disciplines, the primary specialist can coordinate up to three additional specialists. The result must show evidence state, diagnosis, prioritized actions, handoffs, approval boundaries, and a measurable next move.

## Specialist roster

VERA, LENS, AERO, FRAME, WAVE, ATLAS, MAPS, SIGNAL, GRID, PULSE, HOOK, PIN, REVIVE, ECHO, STUDIO, FORGE, PRISM, SCOPE, and PATCH.

## Platform contract

- OpenAI Responses API with GPT-5.6.
- Live web search only when the configured model path is available.
- Multi-agent orchestration is bounded to three concurrent collaborators.
- Public-preview fallback never presents unverified web results as evidence.
- Sending, publishing, spend, account changes, and other consequential actions remain behind human approval.

## Experience commitments

- Momentum 360 navy, gold, cyan, and white visual language.
- Clear specialist ownership and visible collaborator attribution.
- No invented customer facts, performance metrics, prices, or case-study results.
- Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, semantic headings, and reduced-motion support.
