# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vite, React, and TypeScript with a WebGL-capable motion stack. Dillon explicitly prioritized the strongest elaborate effects over the quickest implementation. Each concept builds to an independent static Netlify deploy.

## Users

The primary visitor is a local or multi-location business owner evaluating one partner for search visibility, advertising, websites, social media, photography, video, virtual tours, and 3D property media. The visitor needs to understand the combined offer quickly and confidently request a free audit.

## Product Purpose

The two review concepts test how Need Momentum can become the primary agency brand while Momentum 360 becomes its named spatial-media service. Success means visitors understand the unified company, see the range of services without a copy-heavy experience, trust the real founders and proof, and reach the current free-audit path.

## Positioning

Need Momentum combines demand generation and digital presence with the ability to capture and present a business or property in immersive 360-degree media. The website must show those capabilities as one connected system instead of two disconnected companies.

## Operating Context

These are public, noindex Netlify review sites. The current Need Momentum and Momentum Virtual Tours WordPress properties remain untouched. The concepts use current public website copy, exact official assets, Jenny's Slack feedback, and the prior Put It On the Map concept as evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two distinct long-form homepage concepts are required.
- Need Momentum is the master brand; Momentum 360 appears as a service.
- The primary action is Request a Free Audit and links to the existing Need Momentum audit path.
- No new form backend, CRM routing, custom domain, redirect, canonical, or production WordPress mutation is in scope.
- Previews must be noindex and remain usable without WebGL or advanced motion.
- Copy must not invent customers, testimonials, performance metrics, pricing, or conversion results.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the exact Need Momentum logo and established Momentum blue, deep navy, cyan, white, and signal yellow/gold.
- Concept one is a faithful brand-adapted evolution of the existing Earth and orbital-globe composition.
- Concept two is the selected Kinetic Signal Field: particles, swipe chapters, meaningful liquid glass, disappearing real imagery, and cinematic founders.
- Use real, source-verified founder and company imagery. Do not synthesize or alter facial identity.
- Identity lock: Mac Frederick is the blond founder and appears on the left in the 2026 award photo. Sean Boyle is the dark-haired founder and appears on the right. Any asset or generated reference that reverses this mapping is rejected.

## Evidence on Hand

- Exact Need Momentum logo sourced from the current website.
- Existing Put It On the Map Netlify reference and local screenshot.
- Current Need Momentum and Momentum Virtual Tours public service pages.
- Real 2026 Inc. award image featuring Mac Frederick and Sean Boyle.
- Current canonical dark-haired Sean portrait and a real 2026 blond Mac image source from the official Momentum YouTube channel.
- User-supplied 1200 × 628 founder film featuring Mac left, Sean right, and the Need Momentum logo end card.
- Existing Momentum award badges and visual-system documentation.
- No approved customer metrics or testimonial quotes for this homepage; none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make the unified offer intelligible before asking visitors to explore details.
- Demonstrate range through composition and motion instead of dense copy.
- Keep Momentum 360 visible and valuable without letting it compete with the master brand.
- Make every elaborate effect support the brand story and degrade cleanly.
- Preserve SEO boundaries until a separately approved production migration exists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Both concepts must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, 200 percent zoom, touch input from 320px upward, reduced motion, low-power fallbacks, and readable content when JavaScript or WebGL enhancements are unavailable.
