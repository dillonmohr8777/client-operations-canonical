# July 2026 Momentum 360 Client Reports

## Purpose

Give each Momentum 360 client a four-page-or-less monthly read of the work completed during July 1–31, 2026, the verified platform and operating signals, measurement limits, and the next decision.

## Audience

Client stakeholders and Momentum 360 account owners reading a PDF on desktop or mobile.

## Surface mode

Read / Operate: the report should be understood in seconds, while keeping platform metrics, business outcomes, and pending validation visibly distinct.

## Hard requirements

- One report per exact July client roster entry; keep Fresh Blends and Replenish separate.
- Use exact verified client marks where available and preserve them without recoloring or distortion.
- Google Ads and Meta Ads are separate channels.
- Platform events are not presented as qualified outcomes unless reconciled.
- Counts are whole integers; unavailable fields are pending, not inferred.
- Every report is no longer than four US Letter pages.
- No send, publish, deploy, spend, or account mutation is part of this deliverable.
