# Monthly Report Design System

## Direction

An editorial command-center report: the dark Momentum 360 shell is the stable canvas, while each client mark and accent becomes a restrained signal layer. The page reads like a precise field brief, not a generic dashboard.

## Visual tokens

- Orbit Night `#041027` and Momentum Navy `#071632` for the canvas.
- Command Blue `#143564` for panels and data rails.
- Orbit Blue `#2F82FF` for verified activity and links.
- Signal Gold `#F2B63D` for the primary signal line and decisions.
- Field Paper `#F4F7FB`, Ink `#09152B`, and Slate `#53627A` for light reading surfaces.
- Client accent colors are used for the report masthead, one signal bar, and small status markers only.

## Typography

Embedded Space Grotesk for headings and metrics; Segoe UI for body copy and tables. Numeric values use tabular numerals, and small labels are kept secondary to readable body copy.

## Composition

1. Page 1: exact Momentum and client identity, reporting period, verified signal, four KPI tiles, completed-work summary, primary goal, and executive analysis.
2. Page 2: channel-specific evidence panels, named platform actions, campaign or delivery tables, and source freshness.
3. Page 3: outcome reconciliation plus detailed July work with explicit completion states and client-specific scope callouts.
4. Page 4: wins, watchouts, and a client-facing August action plan. Internal provenance remains in the report bundle rather than the client PDF.

## Accessibility and QA

Use bright text on dark surfaces, avoid color-only states, preserve 25% clear space around the exact circular M, include status text, keep tables selectable, and check every rendered page for clipping, overflow, and the four-page maximum.
