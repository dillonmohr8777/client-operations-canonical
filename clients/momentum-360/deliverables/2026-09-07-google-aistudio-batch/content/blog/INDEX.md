## Voice checks applied to every post

- Short declaratives, periods, plain words. Hyphens, never em dashes. Contractions on.
- Zero instances of the jargon list (elevate, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changing, revolutionary, transform, empower, robust, innovative, synergy, holistic, ecosystem, stakeholders, deliverables, solutions, and the rest of the profile's list).
- Number first, then the comparison, then the next action. Gaps named rather than papered over.
- "Momentum built the machine. Now we build yours." appears at most once per post (posts 01, 02, 05, 07, 08, 09).
- No client named. Descriptors from the evidence bank's anonymization map only. No team member named.
- No Momentum prices (awaiting sign-off). Offer scopes described without dollar figures.
