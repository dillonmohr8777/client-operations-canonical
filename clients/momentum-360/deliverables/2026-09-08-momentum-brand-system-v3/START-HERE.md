# Momentum: interactive brand and five full books

This is the blue and gold v3 replacement built around Dillon's supplied M360 AI Search Playbook, new original ceramic and engraved artwork, and Momo's Momentum m superhero chest emblem.

## Open the HTML
Extract the ZIP, then open `index.html` in your browser for the five-book library or `brand.html` for the interactive brand guide. Keep `assets`, `source`, CSS and JavaScript beside the HTML. These are real HTML pages, with responsive layouts, chapter search, source disclosures and locally saved exercises. No installation or account is needed for the downloaded pages. Browser storage rules may differ when opening local files; the page reports storage failures rather than claiming persistence.

On Dillon's current machine, the running preview is http://127.0.0.1:63260/ . This loopback address is local to that machine, not a public website. The same work is also transferred to the existing private Claude Design artifact; its delivery receipt records the actual verified save and any native-runtime limitations.

## Reuse the artwork
The brand guide exposes three galleries: six Momo scenes, six service icons and three original engravings. Download individual PNGs there or use the files in `assets`. Their exact generation prompts, dimensions and hashes are in `assets/generation-manifest.json`, with prompts also embedded in each generated image. Official Momentum Digital logo and mark files are preserved separately, byte-for-byte; rendered Momo insignia is illustration, not a replacement master logo.

## Review status
The full original manuscript files and their original publication conditions remain in `source`, with corresponding disclosures inside each book. This design replacement does not certify their business claims or authorize public publication. Exercises store user-entered practice notes locally; they do not search AI engines, place calls, modify a CRM, launch campaigns or grant release approval.

## Editable source
Shared HTML/CSS/JavaScript and the repeatable Python book builder are included. DESIGN.md records the shipped system. The Claude Design/Code handoff lives in the project separately because its native document packaging is specific to that runtime.
