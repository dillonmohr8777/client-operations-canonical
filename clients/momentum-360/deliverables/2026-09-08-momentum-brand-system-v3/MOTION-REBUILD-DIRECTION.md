## Diagnosis and common rules
Build each scene so its movement leads into the next. Mix expressive source imagery with short typographic pauses. Use controlled masking, scale, depth, and overlapping timing; eliminate long empty title-card holds. Preserve Momentum's verified logo and identity; adapt the reference's motion construction, not its exact people, room, text, or branding. Avoid fabricated search rankings, chat recommendations, numerical results, or readable generated interface copy. Composite exact text in the editor.
