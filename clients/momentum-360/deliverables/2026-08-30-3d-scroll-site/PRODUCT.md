# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for a fast, portable review build, with Blender-rendered media and progressive enhancement for scroll-linked motion. No build step or new backend is required.

## Users

The primary visitor is a local or multi-location business owner evaluating Momentum 360 as a partner for immersive property media, virtual tours, photography, video, websites, and demand generation. The visitor should understand the offer quickly and be able to request a free audit.

## Product Purpose

Create a Momentum 360-branded cinematic real-estate web experience that closely reproduces the supplied seven-shot luxury-home reference while changing the primary motion axis: scrolling down the page should drive a downward camera journey through the property story.

## Positioning

Momentum 360 connects cinematic property capture with the marketing systems that turn a space into attention and action. The experience should demonstrate spatial storytelling rather than merely claim it.

## Operating Context

This is a local review surface built inside Momentum 360's canonical client folder. It must not alter the existing Need Momentum or Momentum Virtual Tours websites, create a new public site, or imply that synthetic demonstration scenes are real client properties.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Match the reference's full-bleed luxury-home sequence, restrained overlay copy, slow camera drift, and cinematic crossfades.
- Replace lateral or autonomous movement with scroll-linked vertical travel down the page.
- Use the existing Momentum 360 Blender home scene and verified Momentum brand assets.
- Remain readable and navigable when JavaScript, advanced motion, or large imagery is unavailable.
- Do not invent customers, testimonials, performance metrics, pricing, property addresses, or conversion results.
- Treat the property imagery as a clearly labeled demonstration.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the exact Momentum 360 logo artwork and established deep navy, Momentum blue, cyan, white, and signal yellow/gold palette.
- Use square, instrument-grade controls and restrained glass only where it overlays moving imagery.
- Keep Momentum 360 as the visible brand for this spatial-media experience.
- Use the supplied TikTok only as a motion and composition reference; do not copy its creator overlays, captions, identity, or property claims.

- Let scroll position give the visitor direct control over the camera journey.
