# Momentum 360 concept frame prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation. These images are concept property imagery, not photographs of a listed property. Brand copy and the official Momentum 360 logo are applied in HTML rather than generated into the images.

## Common continuity lock

Every frame depicts the same contemporary Pennsylvania luxury estate at blue hour: pale limestone and warm cedar exterior, black steel window frames, warm oak interiors, travertine floors, soft amber architectural lighting, restrained neutral furnishings, rolling wooded hills, realistic editorial architectural photography, cinematic wide-angle composition, 16:10 landscape framing, no people, no text, no watermark, no logo, no surreal geometry, and no fisheye distortion.

## Shot prompts

1. `01-aerial-arrival`: high oblique drone establishing view. Show the complete estate, arrival drive, pool terrace, and wooded hills with a clear visual path toward the entry.
2. `02-entrance-approach`: lower forward-moving approach toward the illuminated front entry. Preserve the exterior materials and let the glass reveal the warm interior beyond.
3. `03-living-kitchen`: interior hero view from the great room toward the open kitchen and glass wall. Preserve the same architecture and show strong indoor-outdoor continuity.
4. `04-mezzanine-descent`: viewpoint from the upper mezzanine looking down through the double-height living space, with the stair and lower floor forming a convincing downward path.
5. `05-primary-suite`: calm primary bedroom with oak, limestone, neutral textiles, and a framed view back to the same hills and pool terrace.
6. `06-spa-bathroom`: limestone spa bathroom with a freestanding tub, walk-in shower, warm indirect lighting, and the same quiet architectural language.
7. `07-pool-finale`: low twilight view from the infinity-pool edge back toward the fully illuminated estate. Leave clean lower-left negative space for the final Momentum 360 call to action.
