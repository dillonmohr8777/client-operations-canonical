# July 17 AI review: M360 Orbit pilot decision sheet

## Decision requested

Approve a private, read-only shadow pilot for the existing 19-agent M360 Orbit operating model. Do not change the public site and do not enable outreach, CRM writes, publishing, ads, spend, or account changes.

## Recommended pilot

Use Melissa's Maps-first idea as a controlled test:

- one geographic market;
- one local-business category;
- one Momentum service offer;
- ten to twenty public businesses;
- Google Maps or an authorized Places route as identity source;
- Instagram only as supporting public evidence;
- MAPS and GRID for discovery and normalization;
- PATCH, ATLAS, PRISM, and SCOPE for opportunity diagnosis;
- HOOK plus the appropriate capture specialist for a review-only concept;
- human review before any prospect is promoted;
- no outreach.

## Why this pilot

- It exercises several Orbit specialists in one real Momentum workflow.
- Evidence is public and reproducible.
- Quality can be measured before connecting live systems.
- It directly supports pipeline development without pretending raw discovery equals a qualified lead.
- Failure is reversible because the pilot produces internal artifacts only.

## Success criteria

- 95 percent or more of pilot records have a reproducible Maps identity.
- Zero duplicates or cross-client facts remain in the accepted set.
- 90 percent or more of accepted claims have direct public evidence.
- Human reviewers accept at least 80 percent of qualified opportunity briefs without a factual correction.
- Median review time is materially lower than the current manual process.
- Zero messages, CRM records, publications, spend changes, or account changes occur.

## Stop conditions

Stop the pilot if:

- business identity or client workspace is ambiguous;
- a source route violates platform terms or approved access;
- private contact information or decision-maker identity would need to be inferred;
- a model invents a claim, approval, recipient, testimonial, or metric;
- client data leaks across workspaces;
- anyone treats a research record as consent to contact;
- the pilot cannot produce reproducible evidence for its conclusions.
