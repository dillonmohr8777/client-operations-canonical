> Here's a quick breakdown:
> *DR, Domain Rating*
> Domain Rating is an Ahrefs metric that measures the strength of a website’s backlink profile on a scale from 0 to 100.
> *What it means for you:* A higher DR generally indicates that the website has earned links from more authoritative websites. This can make it easier to compete for valuable search terms, although DR itself does not directly determine Google rankings.
> *CF, Citation Flow*
> Citation Flow is a Majestic (another SEO platform) metric that estimates the quantity and influence of links pointing to a website on a scale from 0 to 100.
> *What it means for you:* A higher CF means the website has a stronger volume of backlinks. However, CF measures link quantity more than link quality, so it should be reviewed alongside Trust Flow.
> *TF, Trust Flow*
> Trust Flow is a Majestic metric that estimates the quality and trustworthiness of the websites linking to a domain on a scale from 0 to 100.
> *What it means for you:* A higher TF suggests the website is receiving links from reputable and relevant sources. Strong Trust Flow is generally a better signal of backlink quality than link volume alone.
> *Keywords*
> Keywords represents the estimated number of search terms for which the website appears in Google’s top 100 organic search results.
> *What it means for you:* More ranking keywords can increase the number of ways potential customers discover the website. Quality matters as much as quantity. Rankings for relevant, purchase-intent keywords are more valuable than rankings for unrelated or low-value searches.
> *Overall Traffic*
> Overall Traffic is an estimate of the website’s monthly organic search traffic across all tracked countries.
> *What it means for you:* This shows the estimated visibility and reach the website receives from search engines. Growth usually indicates that more people are finding the business through unpaid search results.
> *US Traffic*
> US Traffic is the estimated monthly organic search traffic coming specifically from users in the United States.
> *What it means for you:* If the United States is a target market, this metric provides a more relevant view of whether SEO visibility is reaching the right geographic audience.
> *Important context*
> These are third-party estimates, not measurements taken directly from Google Analytics or Google Search Console.
> They are most useful for comparing websites and tracking trends over time. No single metric proves SEO performance by itself.
