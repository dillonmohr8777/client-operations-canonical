## The business in one page

Momentum Digital already has the brand, the book of business, and the four AI menu labels. The division's job is to turn those labels into four sellable machines, one free opener, and a sales path Jesse can run without Dillon sitting in every first call.

**Charter, proposed.** Momentum AI sits under Momentum Digital. Four lanes Mac put on the site. One flagship and one agent per lane. One free Snapshot. Dillon owns solution design, delivery, and quality. Mac owns strategy, price, and commercial yes. Jesse owns agreed prospecting and close follow-through. Codex remains the orchestrator. Nothing ships as Dillon's hours wearing an AI badge.

**Why now.** Mac said so, twice, on September 4. Inc 5000 press already named an AI division on August 21. ChatGPT Ads is self-serve in the US. AEO retainers in the SMB band sit about $1,500–$3,000 to enter and $3,000–$8,000 for active work; WebFX is the Pennsylvania shop publishing $3,000/month. No Philadelphia SMB agency has a named AI division on the market in the scan we ran. That is context, not a promise of demand.

**The finding that changes the go-to-market.** 241 cold emails, zero verified human replies. Two hand-built audits, two quotes. The funnel is Snapshot → discovery → one flagship. Not list-blast → voicemail → hope.
