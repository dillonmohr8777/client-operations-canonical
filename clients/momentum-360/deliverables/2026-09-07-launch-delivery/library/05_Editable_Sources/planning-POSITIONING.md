# Momentum AI — one-page positioning brief

Brand: Momentum Digital, Philadelphia. Division lockup: existing Momentum mark plus the word AI.
Core offer: four named lanes that already sit on needmomentum.com — AEO/GEO, AI Design, AI Marketing, AI Automation — each with one launch flagship and one agent that does the recurring work.
Ideal client: established local service businesses already tied to Momentum, with a named buyer, a working site, an existing lead source, and one repeatable operational problem.
Problem solved: they are invisible to AI search, slow to respond, and stuck operating the agency by hand.
Tone: warm, direct, measured. Momentum already did the work. Now it packages the machine.
Focus: Philadelphia-region small and midsize service businesses. Not healthcare or legal case records in the founding cohort.

Internal. Prices and claims below are proposed. Nothing here is a contract or an outbound authorization.

---

## Positioning statement

Momentum built the machine. Now we build yours.

For established local service businesses that already have demand but lose it to unread sites, slow follow-up, and disconnected tools, Momentum AI is the in-house division that makes the business easier to discover, faster to respond, and simpler to operate — with evidence of what changed. Model access is an input. The fee is for selecting a valuable process, connecting the right systems, verifying the result, and keeping it running.

## Target ICP

**Primary.** Local service owners in the Philadelphia region who already know Momentum, have a live website, a working lead source, and a CRM or booking process, and can name one repeated costly handoff. A relationship owner must approve the conversation. An employee must own adoption and exceptions.

**Secondary.** Existing Momentum accounts that already bought an adjacent lane (AEO add-on, ChatGPT Ads management, a reporting build) and can expand into a second flagship without a new relationship.

**Not first.** Cold Erie and Pittsburgh lists. Healthcare or legal case records. Buyers who want a guaranteed AI ranking. Accounts with no operator and no system access.

**Why this ICP.** Two hand-built AI search audits sent August 23 both reached quote. Two hundred forty-one cold emails sent September 2–3 produced zero verified human replies. The opener that works is a branded audit on a known relationship, not a cold send.

## Five-value map

1. **Get found by the systems that now write the answer.** AI crawlers can read the site, the business is typed as what it is, and twenty buyer questions are measured on two named engines. No ranking promise.
2. **See the thing in five working days.** A working prototype — site, dashboard, or film — from the client's verified logo and first-party facts. A prototype is a prototype.
3. **Content that moves, with the logo never model-drawn.** A monthly film and stills set in the client's identity, rights ledger attached, credits at cost.
4. **Every accepted lead has a receipt.** One intake to one destination, dedupe, exception queue, reply draft, destination readback, thirty named acceptance cases.
5. **Hours per client is an acceptance metric.** Every offer ships with its agent. An AI division whose product is Dillon's hours is not an AI division.

## Three elevator pitches

**Short.** We make a service business easier to find, faster to answer, and simpler to run. Then we show you what changed.

**Medium.** Momentum has served over a thousand Philadelphia-region businesses since 2015. The AI division does not sell a chatbot. It sells four finished machines already on the site: get found by AI, prototype in a week, content that moves, and an attribution spine. You pick one problem. We implement it, test it, and leave an operator who can run it.

**Long.** Most agencies will sell you a tool subscription and a slide. Momentum AI sells a verified process. Mac already put the four lanes on needmomentum.com: AEO/GEO, AI Design, AI Marketing, and AI Automation. Dillon's job is to put one flagship and one agent behind each lane, start with a free branded Snapshot on a relationship-approved account, and refuse any promise we cannot measure. The first cohort is three paid pilots. Setup fees fund setup. Recurring fees fund the agent plus a capped human review. If hours per client blow the budget, the offer failed, not the client.

## What we do not say

No guaranteed citations, rankings, leads, or revenue. No site-count claim. No founder likeness in generated film. No invented testimonials. No 40/40/20 split presented as agreed — Mac proposed it August 4; Friday still has to write it down.
