# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS, selected from the existing client-operations report conventions for a portable browser and PDF deliverable.

## Users

Primary reader: Jeffrey M. Petersen and the Tuxedo Den decision team evaluating Momentum before a full engagement. Secondary readers: the Momentum team responsible for explaining and implementing the plan.

## Product Purpose

Turn a current public SEO, AI visibility, authority, local entity, and technical snapshot into an evidence-led decision document. Success means the reader understands the entity-confusion risk, sees the demand already present, and can picture a credible first 90 days.

## Positioning

This is an entity-recovery and local-demand plan, not a generic traffic checklist: it distinguishes the Wall Township business from similarly named California businesses, consolidates TuxedoKing and Tuxedo Den signals, and turns three decades of service proof into sources AI systems can trust.

## Operating Context

Pre-engagement audit prepared after Momentum's proposal and follow-up thread. Evidence is drawn from the live site, current public search, Semrush UI, Ahrefs' public authority checker, a representative technical crawl, and a local mobile Lighthouse test.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The artifact must separate verified facts, third-party estimates, and recommendations. It cannot claim access to Google Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile, booking data, CRM outcomes, or full authenticated Ahrefs Site Explorer. No retrievable note-taker record was found for the Tuxedo Den call; the proposal thread is the recoverable source of pre-sale context.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Tuxedo Den's black, white, oxblood red, anniversary gold, direct service voice, appointment model, and New Jersey identity. Avoid discount-first positioning or claims not verified from the site or source thread.

## Evidence on Hand

Live tuxden.com site and schema; Semrush US desktop snapshot dated August 20, 2026; Ahrefs public authority snapshot dated August 21, 2026; current search-result samples; representative six-page crawl; local Lighthouse mobile run; Momentum proposal and follow-up thread.

## Product Principles

- Fix identity before amplifying demand.
- Make geography, ownership, aliases, and service area machine-readable.
- Search and AI visibility are one connected system.
- Each action has an owner, timing, validation gate, and measurable signal.
- Owned analytics replace third-party estimates after onboarding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable in browser and print, with semantic headings, accessible contrast, keyboard-safe controls, reduced-motion support, and plain language for mixed technical and nontechnical readers.
