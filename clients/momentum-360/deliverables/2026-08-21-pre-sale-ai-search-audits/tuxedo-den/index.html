Why this matters

An AI answer that confidently recommends a California business for a New Jersey search is obviously wrong. A subtler version is more dangerous: a user asks for Tuxedo Den and receives a mixed summary, reviews, or location context. That erodes trust before the appointment journey begins.

What fixes it

Complete local schema, consistent business aliases, exact NAP, external citation cleanup, location-specific source coverage, and clear first-party language such as “Tuxedo Den in Wall Township, New Jersey, operated by TuxedoKing Formalwear, Inc.”

Authority & links

The link indexes disagree by two orders of magnitude. That is a quality warning.

Semrush reports 185.8K backlinks from 188 referring domains; Ahrefs’ public checker reports 1.5K backlinks from 502 linking websites. Providers differ, but this gap is too large to treat the raw total as a win. It may reflect sitewide links, redirected or historical URLs, duplicate counting, low-quality clusters, or index differences.
