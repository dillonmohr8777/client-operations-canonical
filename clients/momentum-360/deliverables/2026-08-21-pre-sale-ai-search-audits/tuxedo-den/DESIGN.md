# Design System: Tuxedo Den AI Search Audit

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Master Fitter's Working Card"**

The report behaves like an expert alteration plan: direct, measured, and decisive. Formal black establishes authority, fitting paper carries the evidence, oxblood marks corrections, and anniversary gold preserves the business's 30-year proof.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Condensed display type with uncompromising hierarchy
- A signature Wall Township versus California identity-collision proof
- The exact live-site 30-year Tuxedo Den logo on the front and back covers
- Ruled measurement rows and alteration rails instead of generic cards

## Colors

Formal black and fitting paper form the base. Oxblood identifies corrections and anniversary gold carries proof, emphasis, and the established-history signal.

**The Alteration Mark Rule.** Red identifies what must be corrected; gold identifies value worth preserving.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Oswald with Arial Narrow fallback  
**Body Font:** Source Sans 3 with Arial fallback

Oswald delivers a tailored, poster-like voice. Source Sans 3 keeps dense evidence legible and practical.

**The Cut and Copy Rule.** Oswald makes the claim; Source Sans 3 shows the measurement behind it.

## Layout

The desktop container is capped at 1180px. Section heads carry a vertical alteration rule, metrics use ruled four-column bands, and the roadmap follows a fitting rail. At 850px the report collapses to one column, metric bands become two by two, collision panels stack, and data tables become keyboard-focusable horizontal regions. Print uses compact two-column roadmap lists and intentional full-page covers.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is mostly flat. A single firm shadow lifts the identity-collision proof; contrast, rules, and alternating black and fitting-paper fields create the remaining depth.

## Shapes

The form language is square, ruled, and tailored. Three-pixel badge corners soften status labels without making the report playful. Circles belong only to the exact anniversary logo.

## Components

### Identity-collision proof

A white New Jersey panel and black California panel are divided by a red inequality rail. Both sides remain fully legible in HTML, mobile, and print.

### Evidence badges

Compact uppercase labels mark verified evidence, estimates, access gates, and risks.

### Roadmap rail

Square red notches connect the 90 day alteration sequence. Desktop and print use two-column action lists; mobile uses one column.

### AI learning loop

Five equal black panels express the recurring optimization cycle and use resilient wrapping to prevent clipping.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use the exact local logo asset at `assets/tuxedo-den-logo.png`.
- **Do** keep New Jersey and California evidence visually distinct.
- **Do** preserve reduced-motion behavior and accessible mobile tables.

### Don't:

- **Don't** replace the fitting-card grammar with rounded SaaS cards.
- **Don't** use red as generic decoration.
- **Don't** collapse conflicting third-party link counts into a false total.
