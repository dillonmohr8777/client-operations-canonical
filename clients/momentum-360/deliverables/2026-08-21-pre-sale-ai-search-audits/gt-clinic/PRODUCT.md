# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS, selected from the existing client-operations report conventions for a portable browser and PDF deliverable.

## Users

Primary reader: Dr. Ghazala Farooqui and the GT Clinic decision team evaluating Momentum before a full engagement. Secondary readers: the Momentum team responsible for explaining and implementing the plan.

## Product Purpose

Turn a current public SEO, AI visibility, authority, and technical snapshot into an evidence-led decision document. Success means the reader can see the discoverability gap, understand why it matters, and picture a credible first 90 days without mistaking estimates for owned analytics.

## Positioning

This is a physician-authority growth plan, not a generic traffic checklist: every recommendation connects Dr. Farooqui's verified expertise, patient questions, local service intent, and machine-readable evidence to the sources AI systems cite.

## Operating Context

Pre-engagement audit prepared after Momentum's proposal and before the scheduled SEO conversation. Evidence is drawn from the live site, current public search, Semrush UI, Ahrefs' public authority checker, a representative technical crawl, and local mobile Lighthouse tests.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The artifact must separate verified facts, third-party estimates, and recommendations. It cannot claim access to Google Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile, booking data, CRM outcomes, or full authenticated Ahrefs Site Explorer. The GT note taker was not admitted, so no transcript exists; proposal and calendar context are the only recoverable call-adjacent evidence.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve GT Clinic's navy, clean white space, restrained gold, physician-led credibility, and patient-friendly voice. Avoid hype, diagnosis, outcome guarantees, or unsupported medical claims.

## Evidence on Hand

Live thegtclinic.com site and schema; Semrush US desktop snapshot dated August 20, 2026; Ahrefs public authority snapshot dated August 21, 2026; current search-result samples; representative six-page crawl; local Lighthouse mobile run; Momentum proposal and meeting records.

## Product Principles

- Evidence before recommendations.
- Physician authority must be explicit, sourceable, and medically reviewed.
- Search and AI visibility are one connected system.
- Each action has an owner, timing, validation gate, and measurable signal.
- Owned analytics replace third-party estimates after onboarding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable in browser and print, with semantic headings, accessible contrast, keyboard-safe controls, reduced-motion support, and language that does not overpromise medical outcomes.
