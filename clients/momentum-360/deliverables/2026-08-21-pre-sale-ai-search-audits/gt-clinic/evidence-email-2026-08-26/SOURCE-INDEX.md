## What is not completed

The evidence package confirms that no owned baselines were connected during the audit. Search Console, GA4, Google Business Profile, booking, call tracking, and CRM remained pending access. The 30-prompt baseline was designed but not frozen against owned data. The audit is diagnostic and planning evidence, not proof of website publication, tracking configuration, campaign launch, ad spend, dashboard configuration, or medical-content publication.

## Corrections and unsupported claims

The canonical evidence ledger says that no sampled image lacked an `alt` attribute. It does not support the statement that incomplete image alt text was identified.

The canonical audit package does not contain evidence of a missing Google Ads conversion tag. That claim should remain unverified until a dated tag inspection or owned GTM and Google Ads review supports it.
