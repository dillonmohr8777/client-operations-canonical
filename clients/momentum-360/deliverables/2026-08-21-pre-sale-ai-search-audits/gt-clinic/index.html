What this means

GT is currently a supporting entity in a competitor-led answer, not the source whose evidence shapes the answer. The work is not “write for ChatGPT.” It is to create fact-dense pages, strong entity links, and independent local citations that answer engines can retrieve, verify, and quote.

Where citations come from now

Semrush surfaced Yelp and other clinic sites among the top sources. That makes off-site corroboration as important as on-site optimization: professional profiles, respected local media, relevant associations, patient-safe evidence, and consistent business data must agree.

Authority strategy

Earn fewer, better links: physician credential profiles, professional associations, device or treatment partner profiles, local health reporting, chamber and community sources, and original medically reviewed explainers that another publisher can cite. Audit questionable link clusters before considering any disavow action.

Proof assets

Create citable physician explainers, methodology pages, patient-safe case frameworks, treatment comparisons, and local health commentary.

Earned distribution

Pitch specific expertise to South Jersey media, community partners, professional groups, and complementary healthcare publishers.

90-day treatment plan

Repair the signal. Publish the proof. Earn the citation. Iterate.

The sequence matters. Access and measurement come first; technical and entity corrections come before scale; new content ships in evidence-backed clusters; AI monitoring informs the next sprint rather than becoming a vanity dashboard.

1
Days 0–14

Instrument and stabilize

  • Connect Search Console, GA4, Business Profile, booking, call tracking, and CRM
  • Define qualified consultation and service-level conversion events
  • Export indexation, queries, landing pages, and local actions
  • Fix homepage and blog H1 structure
  • Profile mobile script and hero-media cost
  • Validate sitemap, canonical, robots, and schema behavior in owned tools
  • Create the Dr. Farooqui entity ledger
  • Freeze a 30-prompt AI baseline
2
Days 15–30

Correct high-intent pages

  • Protect and expand dermal fillers Marlton
  • Clarify GT Restore Lift versus surgical facelift intent
  • Resolve Botox and injectable page ownership
  • Rewrite weak location metadata and duplicated sections
  • Add physician review, dates, sources, and treatment decision FAQs
  • Build Breadcrumb, Service, Article, and Person relationships where valid
  • Improve internal links by patient decision stage
  • Implement priority mobile performance fixes
3
Days 31–60

Build citable authority clusters

  • Publish physician-led answers for safety, candidacy, downtime, cost drivers, and alternatives
  • Create aesthetic and functional medicine hub pages
  • Author comparison pages without competitor disparagement
  • Add a clear medical editorial and review policy
  • Develop original local commentary and data-led proof assets
  • Normalize directory and professional profile data
  • Launch targeted local and professional outreach
  • Re-test priority prompts and citation sources
4
Days 61–90

Compound what earns trust

  • Expand only clusters gaining impressions, citations, or qualified engagement
  • Refresh answers where AI sources remain competitor-led
  • Strengthen pages sitting in positions 6–20
  • Close local citation and knowledge-panel inconsistencies
  • Review backlink quality and reclaim unlinked mentions
  • Connect paid search queries to organic content gaps
  • Publish the first outcome and learning report
  • Set the next-quarter experiment portfolio
Iterative AI optimization

This is a learning system, not a one-time “AI SEO” project.

Every two-week sprint follows the same discipline. The goal is not simply to appear in an answer; it is to become a correct, well-sourced, useful recommendation for the right local patient question.

ValidateIndexing, render, structured data, analytics, answer correctness
LearnKeep, improve, merge, retire, or scale in the next sprint
