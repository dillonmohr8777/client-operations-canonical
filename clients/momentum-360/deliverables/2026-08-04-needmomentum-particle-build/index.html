The founders

Built by people who run small businesses.

Mac Frederick

Founder & CEO

Mac Frederick started this agency after working at Google.

That is the whole origin story, and it still shapes how the work gets done: measure what the channel actually rewards, then build for it instead of guessing.

A Penn State graduate, he has built the company into one of the fastest-growing in the city — No. 2 on the Philly 100 and No. 651 on the Inc. 5000 — while also founding Phone Repair Philly, now four locations, and Philly IV, now two. Running local businesses of his own is why he talks to owners like an owner.

Outside the accounts he hosts an annual breast-cancer-awareness gala that raises about $100,000, and runs a Small Business Saturday push that has backed more than 100 local businesses. Modern Luxury named him one of its Men of Influence.

Sean Boyle

Partner

Sean Boyle runs the side of the business that makes a space sell itself.

Commercial real estate and tours: when a building, a restaurant, a suite or a job site has to earn a visit before anyone shows up, that is his work.

He leads Momentum 360, the spatial-media arm — virtual tours, photography, video and 360 capture built so a place feels close from anywhere. It is the reason a listing can go from search result to walkthrough without a second vendor.

Sean works from the Center City office at 1635 Market Street with Mac Frederick and partner Luke McCallion. If you want to see the standard before you commit, ask for a walkthrough of a space like yours — that request goes to him.
