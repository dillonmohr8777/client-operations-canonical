The assignment, read straight through.

Momo takes one brief — phones ringing while the crew is out, a website three years old, no clear picture of where jobs came from — and routes it through five stations. Nothing here is published or sent; every step waits for your approval.

01 · Discover

Find how they ask.

Proposed step: read the real questions before writing a word.

02 · Shape

Make it recognizably yours.

Proposed step: one look, built from your own materials.

03 · Respond

Answer the first call.

Proposed step: the missed call gets a reply and a real next step.

04 · Make

Say it in your voice.

Proposed step: drafts from your words and photographs, for your sign-off.

05 · Connect

Keep the work connected.

Proposed step: one thread from the brief to the report.
