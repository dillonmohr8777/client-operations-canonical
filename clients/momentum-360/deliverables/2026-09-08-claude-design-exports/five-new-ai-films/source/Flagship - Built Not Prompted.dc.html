Real WebGL throughout: the headline is genuine extruded geometry (each letter a stacked slice solid, so it occludes and catches a gold edge light as the camera passes between the letters), the light shaft is additive cone geometry with drifting dust, the cards bend per-vertex on landing, and depth of field is a true second pass sampling the depth buffer — not a blur filter over a flat image.
