Change the ground.

Every agency is shouting the same four words, so the ad does not compete on them — it strikes them out. AI-POWERED, AI-DRIVEN, AI-FIRST, AI-ENABLED stamp in as a stack of muted, interchangeable claims and each gets a blue rule drawn through it. Then the stack wipes, the screen goes quiet, and the film concedes the obvious: everyone has access to AI. What follows is the only line left standing.

The other four films in this project are mechanism pieces, full of motion. This one is the opposite and deliberately so: a near-black ground, type that arrives once on the 0.8-second Momentum curve and then holds still, and two full seconds of nothing between the noise and the line. Momo appears once, late, contained on midnight — warmth after the cold type, not the emotional lead.

Implementation and export receipt

Rendered in canvas 2D rather than WebGL, because this film is type: the drawn frame is the captured frame, with no shader and no separate compositing path that could drift from what you see. Every beat opacity, strike extent and type position is a pure function of t. stage.timingContract reports 20.00s, 30fps, 601 frames. One live decoder, retained rather than detached: the source attaches once and is paused outside its window. The Momo beat draws the frame baked from that same master until the decoder reports a delivered frame, so the beat is never empty. A decode failure falls back once and says so here in plain words. The 9:16 master is authored, not cropped: the hero line breaks to four lines instead of three, the launch stamp breaks to two, and type sizes are set against a 1080-wide unit rather than scaled down from the wide cut. Sound is off until you switch it on: two synthesized sine partials through a 260Hz lowpass, a short band-passed noise burst on each strike, and three marker tones. No purchased track, no generated music, nothing licensed. Reduced motion holds the hero line at 12.00s and does not autoplay. Record master is a real-time capture at on-screen size, not a frame-exact 1920×1080 render. Proposed concept work. The struck claims are generic category language, not quotations from any named competitor. No client, result, metric or launch date appears in the film.
