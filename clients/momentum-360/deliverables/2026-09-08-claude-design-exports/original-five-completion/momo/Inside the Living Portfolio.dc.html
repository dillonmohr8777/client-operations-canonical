The camera walks.

Nothing in this gallery moves. Three bays are hung on one navy wall — matte board, gold hairline, a wall plaque beneath each — and the only motion is the camera: an approach onto Momo's bay, a lateral traverse past the second and third at a fixed eyeline, then a pull back to twelve metres where all three read at once and a gold datum draws across the wall to join them. Each traverse ends in a held frame of more than a second so the scene can actually be looked at. No orbiting sheets, no tumbling paper, no desk.

Only the existing
footage.

The three bays carry the three supplied masters and nothing else. Momo's bay is the single live decoder, playing the Reveal master between 1.30s and 6.40s of the film; the other two hang the frames already baked from Watches the Money and Great Ones. Every bay shows its baked still first and swaps to video only once the decoder has delivered a frame, so a bay is never empty. No character was regenerated, no face redrawn, no scene invented.

Implementation and export receipt

Deterministic: the three bays are fixed geometry, and camera position, look target, wall datum and dim are each sample(track, t) — a pure function of t. await stage.renderFrameAt(t) seeks the decoder under a hard three-second ceiling, so a stalled decode cannot hang an export run. stage.timingContract reports 20.00s, 30fps, 601 frames. One live decoder, and it is retained rather than detached. The source attaches once; outside its window the element is paused, never stripped of its src — stripping it aborts the pending load and is what raises a native media error. A decode failure resolves to the baked still once and reports here in plain words. The 9:16 master is authored, not cropped: the same three bays are re-hung up the wall and the camera walks vertically instead of laterally, with the datum redrawn as a vertical rule and the lens opened from 36° to 46°. Sound is off until you switch it on — three synthesized sine partials through a lowpass and four marker tones at the walk's beats, generated in the page. No purchased track, no generated music, nothing licensed. Reduced motion holds the pull-back frame at 15.80s, where all three scenes are readable, and does not autoplay. Record master is a real-time capture at on-screen size, not a frame-exact 1920×1080 render. Proposed concept work. The wall plaques name the scenes only — no client, no result, no metric, no deployment claim anywhere in the film.
