Every pathway is a real tube rebuilt from its own control points each frame, so a tangle straightening is geometry changing rather than one shape cross-fading into another. The handoffs are causal: a bead reaching its node is what starts the next pathway coordinating. Engraved ticks are individual bevelled solids, not a texture.
