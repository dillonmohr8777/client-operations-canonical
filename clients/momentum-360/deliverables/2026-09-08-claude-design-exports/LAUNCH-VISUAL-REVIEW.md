# Launch films visual review

Reviewed Film A in the native Claude Design preview on September 8, 2026. This is a visual review, not a completed redesign.

Observed at the final frame: the blue Momentum logo has weak separation from the navy field. The CTA is legible, while the book cover is relatively dark. At approximately 5.3 seconds, the open spread has convincing dimensional curvature and ceramic imagery, but its oblique angle and small body copy make reading difficult at the displayed size.

Recommended next pass: improve illumination and logo contrast; enlarge essential page text; use a closer, less oblique camera for reading holds; allow a brief settled hold per spread and more space around the final CTA. Preserve the real sheet geometry, approved art, exact branding and restrained composition.

Export verification is required before delivery. The archived HyperFrames files are alternate CSS/GSAP compositions, not the native WebGL book.
