37 failing pairs across 10 sites and 120 measured pairs; 10 of 10 sites affected. Two failures are structural rather than per-site: the circular seal prints --accent on --brand (1.01 to 2.72:1 on six sites), and --muted is derived against --paper but rendered on --panel, where it lands at 4.28 to 4.49:1 on nine of ten. Nine near-misses in a row is a formula, not an accident.

Not folklore. Rules.

Guidelines banned outright

  • 01No AI purple No indigo-to-violet gradients, no #6366f1 family. The single loudest tell that nobody made a decision. Note that sangillo-tire-center currently ships --brand:#583E7D, the closest any live build comes to the line.
  • 02No neon glow No glowing borders, no saturated box-shadows. Elevation in this system is two steps of neutral shadow and nothing else.
  • 03No glassmorphism No frosted translucent panels over blurred backdrops. Surfaces here are opaque, which is also the only way contrast stays measurable.
  • 04No floating gradient orbs No soft blurred colour blobs drifting behind content. If a section needs depth, it gets a real crop, a real rule, or a real photograph.
  • 05No three-equal-card feature row Three identical icon-heading-blurb cards is the reflexive layout that variance 9 exists to prevent. .m-cards is 7fr / 5fr for exactly this reason and cannot be made equal without editing the component.

Dials: visual variance 9/10 · motion 8/10 · information density 4/10. This page runs density higher than 4 on purpose, because it is a reference sheet that gets read, not a page that gets presented.

Why this row is not three equal cards

.m-cards is unequal by construction, and a third child spans the row. The banned layout is not available without editing the component.

Accordion

Branding
80px rows, uppercase, all closed on load, no chrome but the rule. Papa's measured 82px pitch. Native <details>, so it works with no JS and is keyboard-operable for free.
Marketing
The + rotates 45 degrees to an x on open, 250ms on the shared ease. Under reduced motion the duration collapses to 0.01ms and the state still changes.
Websites
Hover nudges the label 0.75rem right and recolours it to --m-accent-ink at 6.18:1. Displacement, not a tint.
