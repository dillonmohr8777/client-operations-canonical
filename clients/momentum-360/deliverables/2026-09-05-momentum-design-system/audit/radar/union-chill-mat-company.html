Union Chill Mat Company has built temperature-control equipment since 1946, starting with cooling mats for steel mills before expanding into the Red Rocket radiant heater, the Clayton Fan, and the Ultramatic indirect-fired heater for construction and aviation work. Today the line covers vented and ducted heaters, portable air conditioning from 1 to 50 tons, generators, dehumidifiers, and industrial fans, available to purchase, rent, lease, or refurbish out of the Zelienople Industrial Park.

Ultramatic Indirect-Fired Heaters

Vented, ducted heaters rated from 300,000 to 2,000,000 BTUH that provide safe, 100% uncontaminated heat for construction and industrial sites. Available for purchase, rental, lease, or refurbishment.

Red Rocket Radiant Heaters

Industrial radiant heating equipment developed from the company's original steel-mill cooling-mat work. Built for job-site and plant-floor spot heating.

Portable Air Conditioning Systems

Customizable A/C systems from 1 to 50 tons of capacity, manufactured in-house. Sized to the job, not sold off a fixed shelf.

Industrial Fans

Industrial-duty fans, including the Clayton Fan, for ventilation and air movement on construction and manufacturing sites.

Generators

Portable generator equipment, including Whisperwatt models, offered alongside the heating and cooling line for purchase or rental.

Dehumidifiers

Industrial dehumidification equipment, including Orion brand units, offered as part of the full temperature-control lineup.

About Union Chill Mat Company

Union Chill Mat Company started in 1946 producing cooling mats for steel mills.

Union Chill Mat Company started in 1946 producing cooling mats for steel mills. That work led to the Red Rocket radiant heater and the Clayton Fan for industrial use, then the Ultramatic indirect-fired heater built for construction and aviation sectors, rated from 300,000 to 2,000,000 BTUH. In the 1980s the company added portable air conditioning systems sized from 1 to 50 tons. The equipment ships from the Zelienople Industrial Park in Zelienople, Pennsylvania, and serves steel mills, aircraft manufacturers, power plants, paint companies, special events, and the military. Every product line is available to purchase, rent, lease, or have refurbished rather than only sold new, with a representative reachable by phone for pricing and availability.

Questions

Can equipment be rented instead of purchased?

Yes. Union Chill Mat Company offers its heaters, air conditioners, generators, and dehumidifiers for purchase, rent, lease, or refurbishment.

What size air conditioning systems are available?

Portable A/C systems are available from 1 to 50 tons of capacity, manufactured in-house and sized to the job.

What kind of heat does the Ultramatic provide?

The Ultramatic is a vented, indirect-fired, ducted heater rated from 300,000 to 2,000,000 BTUH that provides safe, 100% uncontaminated heat.

What industries does Union Chill Mat Company serve?

Steel mills, construction sites, paint companies, special events, aircraft manufacturers, power plants, and the military.
