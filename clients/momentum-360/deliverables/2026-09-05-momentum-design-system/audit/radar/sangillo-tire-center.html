Tire Sales

Sangillo's sells most major brands of tires for automobiles and light trucks, including Michelin, Bridgestone, Firestone, Goodyear, Cooper, Continental, Pirelli, General, Kelly, and Dunlop. If a size isn't in stock, they can normally get it within 24 hours.

Tire Rotation

Rotating tires on a regular basis saves money through more mileage and fewer replacements, and helps fuel economy and treadwear. Staff check for damage and proper inflation at every rotation.

Tire Repair

In many cases Sangillo's is able to repair a tire instead of replacing it. The shop inspects for punctures, cracks, and uneven wear first.

Wheel Balancing

Every tire purchase includes balancing on state-of-the-art computer balancing machines. Improperly balanced tires cause vibration that increases with speed.

Tire Alignment Checks

The shop watches for signs that a vehicle's front and rear suspension is out of alignment, since misalignment wears tires unevenly and can affect handling.

Free Tire Disposal

Every tire purchase comes with free disposal of the old tire. Sangillo's recycles its scrap tires rather than sending them to landfill.
