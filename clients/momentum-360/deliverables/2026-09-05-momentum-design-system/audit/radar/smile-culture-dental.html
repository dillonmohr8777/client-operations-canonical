What we do

Smile Culture Dental in Huntingdon Valley is built on three words: kindness, comfort, and transparency. The team, led by Dr. Harshil Boghara and Dr. Kushal Parikh alongside general dentists Dr. Tony Tharakan and Dr. Miraj Dudhat and orthodontist Dr. Adam Taee, covers routine exams and cleanings through implants, veneers, Invisalign, and emergency care. All PPO insurance plans are accepted, and virtual consultations are available for patients who want to start the conversation before coming in. The office is one of five Smile Culture locations across the Philadelphia area and was recognized among Top Dentists 2025.

Preventative & General Care

Dental exams, professional teeth cleanings, and tooth-colored fillings catch small problems before they grow. Preventative care visits keep the whole mouth on track between appointments.

Dental Implants

Implant treatment comes from doctors with advanced implantology training, including continuing education at the Misch Implant Institute and Implant Pathways. Dr. Boghara is completing a fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantology.

Veneers & Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers and cosmetic treatments are handled by doctors with advanced cosmetic dentistry training and membership in the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. Dr. Boghara also trained with the International Association of Facial Aesthetics.

Orthodontics & Invisalign

Orthodontic care and Invisalign clear aligners come from Dr. Adam Taee, a top Invisalign provider since 2017 with an orthodontics degree from Boston University. Dr. Taee also treats TMJ disorder and dental sleep medicine.

Emergency Dental Care

Emergency dental care covers unexpected pain or damage without a weeks-long wait for an opening. The team fits urgent visits in alongside the regular schedule.

Facial Aesthetics

Facial rejuvenation and injectables, Morpheus8 microneedling, Lumecca IPL, and Diolaze laser hair removal are offered alongside dental care at the same office. These treatments are handled by doctors with training in facial aesthetics, not referred out to a separate spa.
