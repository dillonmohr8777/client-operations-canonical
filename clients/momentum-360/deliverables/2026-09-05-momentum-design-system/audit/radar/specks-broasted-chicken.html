Speck's Broasted Chicken has served Collegeville from the same spot on Ridge Pike since 1953, when Stanley B. Landis, known as Speck, opened Speck's Drive-In on the idea of Pipin' Hot Sandwiches and honest, affordable food. His son Randy still owns the restaurant and runs it day to day. The menu covers award-winning Broasted chicken, hand-dressed hoagies, and homemade coleslaw, plus soups, salads, and milkshakes in nine flavors. Order at the counter, call ahead, or pick up online for a fast, family dinner that has stayed a Collegeville landmark for over seventy years.

Broasted Chicken

Speck's award-winning Broasted chicken comes by the piece, in dinners, or in buckets from 4 to 100 pieces, in an equal assortment of breasts, legs, thighs, and wings. Dinners include french fries, a small side, and a roll. Large party orders are welcome with three days' notice.

Sandwiches & Hoagies

Burgers are made like homemade with fresh ground beef, alongside hot dogs, a chicken salad sandwich, and a fried fish filet or crab cake. Hoagies come dressed with provolone, onion, tomato, oil, and oregano, with lettuce and peppers on request.

Steak Sandwiches

Chicken or beef steak sandwiches come on a long roll, plain, as a cheese steak, or piled into a full steak hoagie. Every option comes in small, medium, and large sizes to match the appetite.

Sides & Soups

Coleslaw, macaroni salad, potato salad, pasta salad, and garden salad round out the counter, along with baked beans, mashed potatoes, and a hot vegetable of the day. Daily soups and chowders rotate through the week, with chili served every Wednesday.

Kids Meals & Milkshakes

Kids meals come with applesauce, a kiddie side, and a choice of juice or milk, with a hot dog, chicken nuggets, or a chicken leg as the main. Milkshakes come in nine flavors, plus fountain soda, coffee, and bottled drinks for the rest of the table.

About Speck's Broasted Chicken

In 1953, Stanley B.

In 1953, Stanley B. Landis, known as Speck, opened the original Speck's Drive-In on Ridge Pike in Limerick, built on the idea of Pipin' Hot Sandwiches and honest, affordable fast food, years before national fast-food chains reached the area. After a fire at the nearby Big Pixie location at Ridge and Germantown Pikes, Speck bought the property and ran both spots at once. The Limerick location changed hands over the years, but the Collegeville restaurant stayed with the family, with Speck's children, their spouses, and his grandchildren taking on roles over the decades. Today his son Randy still owns Speck's and runs daily operations. The restaurant remains known for its sandwiches, with the Broasted chicken and homemade coleslaw as the main draw, and mascot Speckle still appears at community events around town.
