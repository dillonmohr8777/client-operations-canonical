What we do

Parts Stocked For The Automotive Dealer

Nolt's Auto Parts #2 in Denver, PA is one of nine Nolt's Factory Warehouse locations that has been steadily growing to serve the automotive dealer since 1959. The stores stock ignition and electrical parts, brakes, filters, batteries, cooling and climate parts, and chassis and suspension parts from a long list of manufacturer lines, plus a hard-to-find parts service for less common requests. The network runs from the Ephrata warehouse and call center out to Denver, Manheim, New Holland, Myerstown, Quarryville, Lancaster, Elizabethtown, and Harrisburg, supplying shops and dealers rather than walk-in retail.

Ignition & Electrical

Spark plugs, wire sets, starters, and alternators from ACDelco, NGK, Champion, and Autolite for keeping a customer's vehicle running and starting reliably.

Brake Parts

Brake pads, shoes, rotors, drums, hydraulics, cables, and hardware from lines like Raybestos and Centric, stocked for common and less common jobs alike.

Filters & Fluids

Oil, air, and fuel filters from Fram and Wix, alongside motor oils, power steering fluid, and brake fluid.

Batteries

Automotive, truck, industrial, deep cycle, gel, and sealed lead acid batteries in a range of sizes.

Cooling & Climate

Radiators, heater cores, water pumps, fan clutches, and air conditioning parts including hoses and fittings.

Chassis & Suspension

Shocks, struts, CV boots and joints, bearings, seals, and coil springs from Monroe and Moog for suspension repair work.

About Nolt's Auto Parts #2

Nine Stores Stocked For The Trade

Nolt's Auto Parts #2 sits at 1016 Dry Tavern Road in Denver, PA, one of nine stores in the Nolt's Factory Warehouse network that has served the automotive dealer trade since 1959. General Manager Jim Miller and Operations Manager Jim Dugan run the company from the flagship warehouse and call center in Ephrata, with sister stores in Manheim, New Holland, Myerstown, Quarryville, Lancaster, Elizabethtown, and Harrisburg. The line card covers ignition and electrical parts from ACDelco and NGK, brakes from Raybestos and Centric, filters from Fram and Wix, shocks and chassis parts from Monroe and Moog, and re-manufactured components from Cardone, plus a hard-to-find parts line for requests outside the standard catalog. The business is built around supplying shops and dealers rather than walk-in retail, with a parts catalog available on request.
