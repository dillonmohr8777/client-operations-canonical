# Design System: Momentum 360 Growth Workshop

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Founder Command Stage"**

The experience combines a founder keynote, a live operating room, and premium optical glass. Mac and Sean are not decorative headshots; their presence is the human anchor that moves visitors from ambition to a concrete weekly system. Dense operational passages alternate with quiet cinematic portrait moments.

Liquid Glass is used as a lens for information, controls, and transitions. It refracts founder imagery or the active workshop stage; it is never a generic translucent card pasted onto empty space.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Founder imagery at editorial scale with deliberate close crops.
- Deep navy fields interrupted by cyan optics and controlled gold signals.
- Scroll-led chapters with one horizontal system sequence and one cinematic registration close.
- Command language for operations, restrained language for trust and consent.

## Colors

Midnight navy owns the environment. Momentum blue and horizon cyan define optical depth; founder gold is reserved for decisions and conversion.

### Primary

- **Momentum Blue:** Drives active states, progress, optical rails, and system transitions.

### Secondary

- **Founder Gold:** Marks dates, live signals, primary actions, and moments requiring commitment.

### Tertiary

- **Horizon Cyan:** Supports focus, motion, and high-contrast informational accents.

### Neutral

- **Midnight Stage:** Full-bleed background and cinematic negative space.
- **Paper White:** Primary text and critical labels.
- **Slate Blue:** Secondary text that remains legible on navy.

**The Gold Decision Rule.** Gold appears where the visitor commits, confirms, or reads an essential event fact.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Syne with system sans fallback  
**Body Font:** DM Sans with system sans fallback

**Character:** Syne's architectural display lettering creates a distinct founder-stage voice. The body face stays warm and efficient through long operational passages.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** Heavy, tightly tracked, and capped at 6rem for hero and chapter statements.
- **Headline:** Bold, compact, and balanced across two to four lines.
- **Title:** Semibold, direct, and used for steps or decisions.
- **Body:** Comfortable 1rem to 1.15rem copy with a 65–75ch maximum measure.
- **Label:** Small and decisive; uppercase is limited to genuine system state.

**The Outline Is Motion Rule.** Outline display lettering may appear only when scroll or state resolves it into solid information.

## Layout

Desktop uses a 1180px shell with full-bleed cinematic scenes. The page alternates asymmetrical founder stages, sticky editorial chapters, a horizontal workshop-system sequence, and focused conversion blocks. Mobile becomes a single narrative column with swipeable system stages and a persistent conversion dock. Section spacing is generous and intentionally varied; operational content is denser than portrait passages.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth combines offset ambient shadows, optical borders, image clipping, and backdrop-filter. Glass surfaces must sit over meaningful imagery or a visible environmental layer.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Founder Stage:** A deep downward ambient shadow that separates portrait frames from the midnight field.
- **Interactive Lift:** A smaller offset shadow used only during hover or active states.

**The Physical Glass Rule.** Every blurred surface must reveal, magnify, or separate something behind it.

## Shapes

Controls use compact 8px corners. Operational surfaces use 14px corners. Only cinematic image frames and conversion portals may use the larger 28px silhouette. Circular and pill shapes are reserved for status, avatars, and compact controls.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Compact confident corners.
- **Primary:** Founder gold on ink navy with clear lift and active compression.
- **Hover / Focus:** Offset lift, higher contrast, and a cyan keyboard focus ring.
- **Ghost:** Transparent command surface with a meaningful optical border.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** Operational surfaces stay at the 14px radius.
- **Background:** Tonal navy or real Liquid Glass over active scenery.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Flat at rest unless the surface occupies a different physical plane.
- **Border:** One optical edge or one shadow, never both as decoration.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** Dark inset fields with strong text contrast and persistent labels.
- **Focus:** Cyan optical edge and visible browser-native caret.
- **Error / Disabled:** Recovery copy explains what is missing and what the visitor should do.

### Navigation

The fixed header compresses into a glass command strip after leaving the hero. Mobile navigation uses the same environmental material and preserves direct registration access.

### Founder Stage

Close-cropped Mac and Sean imagery occupies at least half the first viewport on desktop and becomes the dominant visual before the first mobile CTA. Optical rails and glass captions respond to scroll but never obscure faces.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** let one portrait, statement, or system interaction dominate each scene.
- **Do** alternate dense operating detail with quiet founder presence.
- **Do** provide static and reduced-motion fallbacks for every authored motion.
- **Do** keep conversion, consent, calendar, and reminder actions explicit.

### Don't:

- **Don't** repeat equal-sized icon cards as page structure.
- **Don't** use Liquid Glass where nothing meaningful exists behind it.
- **Don't** invent testimonials, performance numbers, or approved event details.
- **Don't** let motion hide core content or block registration.
