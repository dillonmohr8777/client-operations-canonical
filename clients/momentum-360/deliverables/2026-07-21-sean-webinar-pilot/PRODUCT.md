# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are owners of local service businesses who want a repeatable growth system without becoming full-time marketers. Sean and Mac operate the workshop; Dillon prepares the funnel, experience, instrumentation, and launch package.

## Product Purpose

The workshop converts permissioned Momentum 360 audiences into qualified registrations, live attendance, and strategy-call opportunities. Success means visitors quickly understand the promise, trust the hosts, register intentionally, add the event to their own calendar, attend, and take one measured next step.

## Positioning

This is a working owner workshop, not a generic webinar funnel. Visitors bring one real growth constraint and leave with a weekly operating system their team can run.

## Operating Context

The funnel begins with opted-in subscribers, Skool members, referrals, and individually approved prospects. Registration captures consent and source attribution. Confirmation offers Google Calendar and ICS actions. Reminder, replay, attendance, and strategy-call events must remain separately measurable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Registration and operator views share one responsive web experience.
- Calendar insertion requires an explicit registrant action; silent third-party calendar placement is prohibited.
- Email reminders require explicit consent, an approved sender, unsubscribe support, and suppression handling.
- Production activation still needs the final event date, meeting URL, audience, privacy language, sender, form destination, and strategy-call route.
- Paid spend and live audience delivery remain separately reviewable actions.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the Momentum 360 name, corporate logo, Mac and Sean imagery, navy/gold/cyan identity, confident owner-focused voice, and the established M360 Orbit visual language. The page should feel premium, cinematic, technical, and useful rather than like a generic webinar template.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real Momentum 360 logo and recognition badges in `assets/`.
- Canonical individual founder portraits in `assets/sean-boyle-founder-bio-v1.webp` and `assets/mac-frederick-founder-bio-v1.webp`, plus the established paired founder imagery.
- Existing registration, confirmation, Google Calendar, ICS, operator, event-schema, and QA implementations.
- No approved testimonials, customer results, final webinar date, production meeting URL, or audience list; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate the operating system instead of repeating marketing claims.
- Make trust, consent, and the next action obvious.
- Let Mac and Sean feel present throughout the experience.
- Keep the attendee journey simple even when the visual presentation is ambitious.
- Build every production integration so it can be tested without sending or spending.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain keyboard accessible, responsive from 320px upward, readable with reduced motion, and usable without advanced animation support.
