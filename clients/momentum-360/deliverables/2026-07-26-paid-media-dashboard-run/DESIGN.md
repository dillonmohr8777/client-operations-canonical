# Momentum 360 Weekly Dashboard System

## Scene

These dashboards are read in Slack, often between meetings and under ordinary office lighting. The established near-black Momentum 360 canvas keeps dense paid-media data calm while blue and amber provide precise operational emphasis.

## Durable visual rules

- Base canvas: `#020617`; raised field: `#071225`; fine rule: `#1e2f4f`.
- Primary text: `#f8fafc`; secondary text: `#a9bad5`.
- Momentum blue: `#2563eb`; signal amber: `#f59e0b`; verified green: `#10b981`.
- Use a continuous metric ribbon with dividers instead of isolated metric cards.
- Keep platform sections visually separate with explicit Google Ads or Meta Ads labels.
- Use system sans for interface copy and tabular numerals for KPIs.
- Restrict pills to platform and source-status labels.
- Tables scroll horizontally on narrow screens; no KPI truncation.
- One entrance moment belongs to the signal ribbon. All content is visible by default and motion respects reduced-motion settings.

## Information order

1. Client, period, and verified-source status.
2. One-sentence weekly signal.
3. Primary KPI ribbon.
4. Channel-specific detail and campaign evidence.
5. What the data means and the next move.
6. Source and latency note.
