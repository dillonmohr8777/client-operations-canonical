# Weekly Paid Media Reporting

## Purpose

Give each paid-media client a fast, trustworthy weekly read of verified delivery, channel-specific performance, campaign context, and the next operational move.

## Audience

Client stakeholders reading a dashboard link from Slack on desktop or mobile.

## Reporting contract

- Reporting window: July 20 through July 26, 2026.
- Google Ads is verified through July 26.
- Meta Ads is verified through July 25; July 26 is labeled pending platform latency.
- Google and Meta remain visibly separated.
- Client brands are never blended.
- Lead and conversion-event counts are whole integers.
- Unvalidated conversion outcomes use neutral pending-validation language.
- Replenish keeps its existing 7-Eleven presentation system and excludes Get Directions.
- Shadow Heating and Cooling is excluded.

## Success

Within seconds, a reader can identify the client, reporting dates, spend, delivery, the strongest verified signal, and the next action without needing an analyst to explain the dashboard.
