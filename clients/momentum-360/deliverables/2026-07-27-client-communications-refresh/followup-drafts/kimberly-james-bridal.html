I should have included the full advertising dashboard with my last update. I attached a PDF copy here, and the live version is also available at Kimberly James Bridal advertising dashboard.

The new Philadelphia Google Search campaign is live at a $20 daily budget and is still learning. The partial July 27 observation shows $1.86 in spend, 33 impressions, 1 click, a 3.03% click through rate, and a $1.86 average cost per click. That early click cost is 58% lower than the $4.44 Apparel, Fashion, and Jewelry search benchmark, but the sample is still too early for a broader conclusion.

For Meta, the July 20 through July 26 reporting window produced $57.75 in spend, 5,999 impressions, and 4,462 people reached. The main improvement since then has been further qualifying the lead form before a bride reaches the boutique.

The form now uses Higher Intent review, SMS phone verification, and 4 questions covering wedding timing, gown budget, boutique visit timing, and whether the bride is ready for a stylist to contact her. Those extra steps naturally reduce the number of completed forms. The encouraging part is that the brides who finish are giving the team more useful information and showing a stronger level of interest.

Our goal is not to generate the largest possible list of names. It is to create more serious conversations for the stylists. We will keep monitoring Search terms, reviewing each newly qualified Meta submission, and matching the strongest leads with appointment outcomes.

The most useful feedback from the boutique will be which brides answer, which conversations show real gown and timing fit, and which ones become appointments. That information will guide the next form and campaign adjustments.
