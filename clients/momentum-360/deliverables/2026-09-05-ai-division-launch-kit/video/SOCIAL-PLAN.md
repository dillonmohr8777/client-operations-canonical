D19 (the name and lockup) and the prices on screen. Nothing posts from this
kit; Dillon posts.

films must read with the sound off, which is how social is watched.

first post: impressions, profile visits, Snapshot requests, quotes, signed,
collected — as separate counts (decision D10).

## Rules carried into every piece

- The mark is the verified PNG, composited after cropping; never generated,
  never re-lettered. Position, opacity, scale only.
- Every on-screen price carries **Proposed** until Mac agrees a number, at
  which point the cards rebuild from one JSON edit and the films are
  re-composited without regenerating a single plate.
- No founder likeness. No faces at all in this set.
- No claim of citations, rankings, leads or revenue. The films say what the
  division does; they do not promise outcomes.
- Reduced-motion still exists for every piece (the final composited frame).
- Nothing on the anti-slop list: no purple, no neon, no glass, no orbs.
