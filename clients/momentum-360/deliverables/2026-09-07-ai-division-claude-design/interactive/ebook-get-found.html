BUYER-QUESTION ARCHITECT · LABELED EXAMPLE

Chapter 04

Measure three numbers, not one

Do not add these together. Do not call the sum "AI ROI."

01 MENTION

Did a named engine mention or cite you for a frozen question. Date, engine, prompt, source.

02 REFERRAL

Did a verified visit arrive from that surface. Pending if analytics are missing.

03 OUTCOME

Did a lead or job attach to that visit. Pending until CRM or booking evidence exists.

Stochastic interfaces get three observations per question. Report the range. Never cherry-pick.
