Chapter 01

The reader changed

People still type questions. What changed is the page that answers them. Search, maps, and chat tools now write a short answer and point at a few sources. If those systems cannot fetch your site, you are not in the conversation. That is not a ranking. That is a door.

For a trade shop, the question is rarely "best marketing agency." It is "who can do this job near me, and can I trust the first screen." The first screen now has to work for a person and for a model.

Chapter 04

What you should not DIY

You can buy a model subscription this afternoon. That is not the fee. The fee is choosing a process, connecting the right systems, testing the failure cases, and leaving an operator who can run it.

DIY

Writing a clearer first paragraph. Uploading real photos. Answering the phone faster. Asking ChatGPT to draft a FAQ you then edit.

NOT DIY

Allowing AI crawlers without breaking the site. Changing entity types in Search Console. Wiring a form to a CRM with duplicates and retries. Putting a logo on generated film.

If the work can silently write to the wrong account, it is not a weekend project.

Chapter 05

How to read an AI answer about your business

When a tool writes about a trade shop, read three things. What it thinks you are. What it thinks you cover. What source it used. If any of those is wrong, the fix is on your site and your listings, not in a prompt you type once.
