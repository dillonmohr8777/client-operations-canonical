# What to actually build in Claude Design

The Friday hub is the meeting object. The conversion system is **interactive ebooks** plus a short list of tools that teach AI-in-marketing and end in a Snapshot or a 20-minute call.

Do not build another deck. Do not build a fake 100-point grade. Every piece below is a designed artifact a buyer can click.

Internal. Proposed. No send, post, or deploy from these files.

## The rule

A piece earns a slot if it does one of three jobs:

1. **Teach** enough that a service owner can see the problem in their own business.
2. **Convert** that recognition into a Snapshot or a booked discovery.
3. **Arm Jesse** so the next conversation is about one flagship, not "so, you do AI?"

If it does none of those, it is decoration. Kill it.

## Interactive ebooks — build these first

Five books. One teaching job each. Same design system. Each book is a chaptered, clickable studio with one interactive per chapter and a single CTA: run the Snapshot or book 20 minutes.

| Book | Audience | Teaching job | Interactive | Converts to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01 How AI changed marketing for local businesses** | Any service owner | The category story. Why this is not "buy ChatGPT." | Problem picker → one next step | Snapshot |
| **02 Get found by AI** | Owners who care about inbound | AEO / GEO without ranking promises | Buyer-question architect + crawler check | Readiness sprint |
| **03 Prototype in a week** | Owners with a site or dashboard they cannot stand | What a spec build is and is not | Five-day timeline you can scrub | Spec build |
| **04 Content that moves** | Owners tired of stills that die in the feed | Motion system, rights, logo rule | Brief builder (audience, offer, placement) | Motion system |
| **05 Every lead has a receipt** | Owners who lose leads between form and follow-up | Lead-ops without "more leads" claims | Walk one lead through intake → exception | Lead Ops agent |

**01 is the public magnet.** That is the book you flush first in Claude Design. Seed: `interactive/ebook-ai-marketing.html`.

**02 is the Snapshot companion.** Leave it after the audit. Seed: `interactive/ebook-get-found.html`.

03–05 are mid-funnel. Build them after 01 and 02 exist as finished Claude Design artifacts, not before.

### What "interactive ebook" means here

Not a PDF with a progress bar. Each chapter has:

- A cover line and one idea
- 400–700 words, designed, not a wall
- One control the reader uses (picker, checklist, calculator, timeline, before/after)
- A "what this is not" line so we do not overclaim
- A persist-on-the-page next chapter, plus a Snapshot CTA that never leaves the artifact in this draft

Print is a later export. Screen is the product.

## Other conversion collateral worth building

After the ebooks, these are the only other Claude Design types that earn time. They all teach AI-in-marketing and ask for one action.

### Diagnostics and calculators

| Piece | What the reader does | Why it converts | Honesty rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Search Snapshot** | Pastes a URL, gets three observations | Already produced quotes | No 100-point score. No live scrape in the artifact. |
| **Readiness diagnostic** | Answers six factual questions about their site and follow-up | Qualifies without a sales call | Observations only. Output is "run a Snapshot" or "talk lead-ops," never a grade. |
| **Slow-follow-up calculator** | Enters weekly leads, hours to first reply, close rate they already know | Makes Lead Ops concrete | Uses their numbers. We do not invent a close-rate lift. |
| **DIY vs division** | Toggles "I have ChatGPT" vs "I need an operator" | Kills the "can't I just subscribe" objection | Fee is for mapping, tests, handoff, support. |

### Do not build in Claude Design

- Another 26-slide deck (exists)
- A second press release
- A public marketing site pretending to be live
- A robot mascot
- A tools-of-2026 list we did not verify this week
- A revenue-forecast toy
- Cold-email sequences (D10 is pause)
- Anything that needs a live engine scrape

## Funnel, one line each

1. Book 01 or a lunch-and-learn creates the category.
2. Snapshot proves it on their URL.
3. Book 02 explains the first-order blockers.
4. Diagnostic or calculator picks the lane.
5. One flagship one-pager. Proposal only through approved routes.

241 cold emails produced zero replies. Two audits produced two quotes. Teach, then show their site, then sell one machine.
