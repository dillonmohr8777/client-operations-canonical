# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary reader: Bill Gallipo, evaluating a technical SEO and AI visibility audit for Meza Tile & Stone.

Secondary reader: Jesse DiLaura and Momentum 360 collaborators who need a credible, client-ready prospect deliverable.

## Product Purpose

Turn a detailed live-site audit into a clear decision document: what is working, what needs attention, what to do first, and how to verify the work.

Success means the reader can understand the commercial priorities quickly, trust the evidence, and use the 90-day sequence without needing the underlying research files.

## Positioning

The report connects measured technical findings, page ownership, local entity consistency, conversion friction, and AI citation readiness into one implementation sequence. It does not present generic SEO advice or invented traffic, rankings, leads, or revenue.

## Operating Context

This is a letter-size PDF shared in a Slack thread as a prospective Momentum 360 deliverable. It is read on desktop and mobile PDF viewers and may be printed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the complete, verified content and limitations of the source audit.
- Keep Meza Tile & Stone, the verified official logo, and first-party project photography recognizable.
- Use the official live-site logo asset without an awkward rectangular background.
- Do not place display text over busy photography.
- Do not invent business outcomes, access, credentials, rankings, traffic, leads, or AI citation presence.
- Preserve human-readable source URLs and the audit date.

## Brand Commitments

- Exact Meza Tile & Stone name and stair/pyramid mark.
- Near-black, white, and the live-site orange as recognizable brand anchors.
- Real project photography from the existing verified asset bundle.
- The redesign may replace the prior report's layout and typography completely.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing 15-page PDF and its ReportLab source.
- Live crawl, Lighthouse, content, indexation, and local-review research files under `research/`.
- Verified project photos under `assets/`.
- Official transparent logo image from the Meza website.
- Desktop and mobile homepage captures under `research/`.

No owner-level Search Console, GA4, CRM, Google Business Profile, call-tracking, hosting, or server-log access is available; the report must continue to state those limits.

## Product Principles

- Decision clarity before methodology.
- Evidence before recommendation.
- One owner and one next action for each priority.
- Visible limitations instead of false precision.
- Craft should reinforce trust without obscuring dense content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, comfortable body size, clear table hierarchy, descriptive text around visuals, and a structure that remains legible in print and on small PDF viewers.
