# Public proof rules

Cold email may only use proof that is either Momentum-owned or freshly observed on the prospect's own public site.

## Allowed in a prospect email

- That prospect's official URL
- That prospect's exact concept URL
- Need Momentum and Momentum 360 public pages
- The Signal homepage, Map homepage, workshop, and scroll story
- A dated public observation: title, H1, HTTPS failure, missing phone, missing first-screen answer
- A dated lab page-speed result for that exact URL, labeled as lab data

## Not allowed

- GT Clinic Semrush, Ahrefs, or Lighthouse screenshots
- Meza Tile audit PDF
- Kimberly James ads numbers
- Bridge or VA Claims product links
- Immortal Marketing pages or the Immortal signature
- Invented keyword volumes or "we ranked you for X"
- The Mac and Sean 4K Drive files until they have an anyone-with-the-link view permission

## How to make a keyword or AI proof card

For each approved preview, capture four public facts on the send date:

1. Official homepage title and H1
2. Whether HTTPS works
3. Whether the first 300 words answer "who is this and what do they do in this city"
4. One current AI-search question, with the platform, query, date, and whether the brand was named

Save those four facts next to the draft. If a tool is unavailable, write `pending validation` and still send only the verified site observation.
