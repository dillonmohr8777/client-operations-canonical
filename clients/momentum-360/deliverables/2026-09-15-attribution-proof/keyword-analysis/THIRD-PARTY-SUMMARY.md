This is third party index data. Every position below is an estimate from a commercial keyword index, not a measured average position from the client's own Google Search Console property. Do not present these figures as measured. Search volume is the index's monthly estimate for the United States. CPC is the value the index returned, blank where the platform returned null. Counts use position 10 or better for "top 10" and position 3 or better for "top 3".

Coverage: 12 domains pulled (10 with rows, 2 with zero rows), 11 domains blocked by the platform's monthly quota, 2 clients with no confirmable domain, 1 registry entry excluded. Details in the last sections.
