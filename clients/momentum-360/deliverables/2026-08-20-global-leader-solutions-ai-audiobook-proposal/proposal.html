02 / Production system
The lean route

One proof. One voice.
One controlled batch.

01

Inventory and count

Collect the course files, map titles and sections, count narrative words, and flag tables, worksheets, citations, or image-only content.

02

Approve a 60-second proof

Test three licensed stock voices on the same passage. Approve one voice, tone, pace, and pronunciation direction before bulk generation.

03

Normalize the scripts

Remove non-spoken page furniture, expand important acronyms, mark pauses, standardize headings, and build one pronunciation sheet.

04

Batch-render by section

Generate short, recoverable sections rather than one giant file. This contains errors and makes targeted fixes inexpensive.

05

Quality control

Run duration and file checks, review every opening and ending, listen at accelerated speed, and repair only the flagged sections.

06

Package and hand off

Deliver one master MP3 per course, consistently named section files where headings exist, and a manifest showing title, duration, and status.
