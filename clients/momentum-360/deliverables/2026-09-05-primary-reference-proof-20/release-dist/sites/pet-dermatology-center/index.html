Allergy testing & more

Allergy testing

The practice offers allergy testing and immunotherapy services for pets. Explore the practice’s information about this service, then contact the team with your pet’s details and your questions. Confirm the appropriate appointment rather than assuming every service is available at every visit.

Skin biopsy

Skin biopsy services help the team investigate skin conditions. Tell the veterinary team what prompted your enquiry and ask how this service is arranged. The practice can confirm the information it needs and which appointment route is appropriate.

Electrocautery

The practice offers electrocautery for selected skin concerns. A short description of your question gives the team a useful starting point. Ask about the service, any records it needs to review and how to confirm the next step for your pet.

Light therapy

Fluorescent light energy therapy is among the practice’s dermatology services. Use the practice’s own contact or request route to enquire about this service. Availability, appointment requirements and individual care decisions should be confirmed directly with the veterinary team.
