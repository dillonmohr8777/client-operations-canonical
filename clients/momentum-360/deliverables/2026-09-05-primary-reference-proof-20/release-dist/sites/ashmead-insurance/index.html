Commercial insurance

Explore coverage for contractors, manufacturers, distributors, nonprofits and small businesses. Tell the agency about the work you do, the people involved and the questions behind your search. Ask about the scope of the options you are comparing, including the terms and limitations that matter to your business.

Personal insurance

Review home, auto, umbrella, flood and other personal coverage options. Talk through the home, vehicles and everyday responsibilities you would like to discuss. Ask the agency to explain the options in context, so your questions stay connected to your own situation.

Life insurance

Discuss life insurance options with the agency and review what fits your needs. Start with the people and responsibilities you have in mind. The agency can be your point of contact for questions about the options it offers, the application process and the information needed for a discussion.

Certificate requests

Use the agency’s commercial certificate request service for insurance documentation. Have the purpose of your request and the relevant account details ready when you contact the agency. Ask which information is needed and how the completed documentation will be returned to you.

What belongs in the conversation?

Some questions are about the bigger picture. Others are about the practical details. Give both a little room before getting in touch with Ashmead Insurance. A clear starting point leaves more space for the conversation you actually want to have.

Your priorities

Start with what prompted the enquiry: a purchase, a renewal, a new venture or a question about an existing policy. That context gives the conversation a clear starting point.

The details

Ask about the limits, exclusions, deductibles and terms of any option you are reviewing. Keep questions tied to the actual documents and your individual circumstances.

Your next step

Before finishing the conversation, confirm what information is still needed, who will follow up and where to send any documents. Keep your next action clear.

Make space for a proper conversation.

You do not need to arrive with all the answers. A useful first enquiry can explain what you are looking for, whether you already have a policy and how you would like to be contacted. For an existing account, include the type of request so it can reach the appropriate team.

If your enquiry brings several questions together, write down which one matters most today. Ask the agency how it would like to receive supporting information, and keep personal documents within its own approved contact channels.
