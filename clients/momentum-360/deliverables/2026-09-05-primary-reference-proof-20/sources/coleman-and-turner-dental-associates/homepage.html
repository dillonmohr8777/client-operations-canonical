About Us

If you're looking for a dentist in Philadelphia you can count on, Center City Dental is here. Conveniently located at 1601 Walnut Street in Rittenhouse Square, our team of experienced Philadelphia dentists — Dr. Derrick Turner, Dr. Wendy Song, and Dr. Shefali Mehta — provides comprehensive dental care for patients throughout Philadelphia, including Rittenhouse Square, Center City, Northern Liberties, Queen Village, Society Hill, Old City, Logan Circle, Fishtown, and Port Richmond.

We welcome patients of all ages and personalize every treatment to fit your needs, your goals, and your schedule. With 4.8 stars across 665+ Google reviews, we're one of Philadelphia's most trusted dental teams.
