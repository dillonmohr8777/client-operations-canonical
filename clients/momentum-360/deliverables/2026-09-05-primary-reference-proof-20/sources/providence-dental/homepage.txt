Guiding Principles
We treat people, not teeth. It is our goal to co-develop a plan with you which will help you to maintain your oral health for a lifetime. Prevention is critical-therefore, we attempt to be proactive in every phase of therapy. We encourage regular cleanings and examinations in order to identify changes in your individual situation.
But it isn’t just our skillful, artistic doctors that makes us unique; you’ll also find friendly, smiling faces – and possibly the best customer service you have ever experienced. In fact, you might be surprised to discover that visits with us feel more like time spent with friends than a visit to the dentist!
We pledge and guarantee that we will always give you our best….because you deserve it!
