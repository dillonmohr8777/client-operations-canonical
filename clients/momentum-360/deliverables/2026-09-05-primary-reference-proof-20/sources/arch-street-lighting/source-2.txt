About Us
3rd-Generation Local Lighting Professionals
Now in its third generation, Arch Street Lighting got its start in 1914 when Harry Horn inherited his uncle’s gas mantle store on South Street in Philadelphia.
The switch was soon made from gas to electric and the store became Harry Horn Electric Supply.
After decades of being the best-known electrical supply store in the city, owner Michael Kurland (Horn’s grandson) remade his business and in 1996 opened Arch Street Lighting, an upscale lighting showroom in the Old City section of Philadelphia.
We hope that you’ll stop in to meet us, give us a call to learn more, or send us a message using our
