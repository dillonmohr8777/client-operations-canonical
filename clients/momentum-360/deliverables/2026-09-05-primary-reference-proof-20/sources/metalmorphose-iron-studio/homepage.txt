Metalmorphose is your local source for custom wrought iron…with a french twist.
Eric Zandotti, artist and master blacksmith, started
Metalmorphose Iron Works
nearly twenty years ago to bring steel to life in the classical french style.  Wrought Iron is literally in Eric’s blood, his grandfather was a traditional blacksmith in Southern France, and Eric’s talent transcends many styles from classical European to whimiscal and even modern.  Creations from Metalmorphose Iron Works can be found locally throughout Pennsylvania, overseas as far as the French Alps, and online in our
Etsy Store
.
Come visit the Chester County studio near the Brandywine River to discuss your project and see Metalmorphose Iron Works in person.
