Dental Services
Our office offers a full range of dental services designed to support oral health at every stage of life. Each treatment plan is customized to address both functional needs and long-term comfort.
General Dentistry
Comprehensive general dentistry services include preventive care, routine exams, professional cleanings, digital imaging, and early intervention to help maintain oral health and prevent dental disease. Ongoing monitoring and individualized care plans support lasting results.
Cosmetic Dentistry
Cosmetic dentistry services are designed to improve the appearance of the smile while preserving natural tooth structure and function. Treatments focus on balance, aesthetics, and long-term stability rather than temporary cosmetic solutions.
Dentures & Bridges
Dr. Damevski has extensive experience designing and restoring dentures and bridges that are comfortable, functional, and natural in appearance. Careful attention is given to fit, bite alignment, and long-term oral health to ensure reliable results.
Dental Services

INVISALIGN
Invisalign
straightens your teeth using a custom-made series of aligners created just for you. These aligner trays are made of smooth, comfortable and virtually invisible plastic that you wear over your teeth which will gradually and gently shift your teeth into place
AESTHETICS
From carefully crafted veneers to professional teeth whitening, Dr. Damevski offers aesthetic dental treatments designed to enhance the natural beauty of your smile.
Using precise, conservative techniques, Botox® and Juvéderm® treatments are also available to soften facial lines and support overall facial harmony. When combined thoughtfully, these services can brighten your smile, refresh your appearance, and help you feel more confident.
PERIODONTAL TREATMENT
If you are experiencing gum disease, our experienced team offers personalized periodontal care designed to restore and maintain healthy gums. Depending on your needs, we may recommend three-, four-, or six-month cleaning intervals to support healing and long-term oral health.
Our approach focuses on gentle care, careful monitoring, and preventive support tailored to each patient.
iTERO SCANNER
The iTero® digital scanner allows us to capture precise images of your teeth and bite without the discomfort of traditional impression materials. Instead of using messy molds, the scanner quickly creates a detailed 3D digital model of your mouth in seconds.
This technology improves comfort, efficiency, and accuracy — and allows you to view your images in real time on a chairside screen. Seeing a digital model of your smile helps you better understand your treatment options and visualize potential outcomes before care begins.
DENTAL IMPLANTS & CROWNS
Dental implants and crowns can restore both the function and appearance of damaged or missing teeth. Whether your concerns are structural, cosmetic, or both, these treatments are designed to improve strength, comfort, and long-term stability.
Thoughtfully planned restorative care can enhance your smile while also supporting how you speak, chew, and move through your day with confidence.
ROOT CANAL TREATMENT
Root canal treatment can relieve tooth pain by removing infection and preserving the natural tooth. Dr. Damevski performs this procedure using modern techniques designed to be comfortable and efficient, often completing treatment in a single visit.
Our focus is on relieving discomfort while restoring function, helping patients return to everyday life with confidence.

MEET DR. DAMEVSKI
Dr. Aleksandar Damevski, DMD, is a trusted general and cosmetic dentist known for helping patients restore their smiles, comfort, and confidence — especially those who have had negative dental experiences in the past.
With extensive experience in general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, dentures, bridges, bite correction, and full-mouth reconstruction, Dr. Damevski creates highly personalized treatment plans designed to restore both function and aesthetics. He is particularly skilled at treating complex cases and correcting problems caused by failing or poorly done dental work.
Dr. Damevski understands that oral health is deeply connected to overall health and quality of life. His approach focuses on dentistry that is not only precise and restorative but also calm, patient-centered, and compassionate. Patients who experience dental anxiety or dental phobia will find his manner reassuring and his office environment welcoming and stress-reducing.
From your first visit, Dr. Damevski prioritizes clear communication, comfort, and trust. Whether you need routine preventive care, cosmetic improvements, dentures or bridges, or comprehensive full-mouth rehabilitation, his goal is to help you feel at ease while achieving long-lasting, natural-looking results.
Dr. Damevski earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Syracuse University and his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University School of Dentistry. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the Pennsylvania Dental Association, and the Montgomery and Bucks Dental Society.
