Welcome to Tom's Automotive in Hatboro.

Here at Tom's Automotive we have expertly served Bucks and Montgomery Counties for 35 years.  Are you looking for a reliable and trustworthy auto repair shop in the Bux-Mont area?  Then look no further!  We offer quality expert repairs at honest and reasonable rates!  Same day service on most jobs.

  • Exhaust system & muffler repairs
  • Air conditioning system services
