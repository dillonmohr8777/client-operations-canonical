The goal of Harleysville Veterinary Hospital is to provide the best possible medical and surgical care for our patients and their owners. Our compassionate and well-trained staff will work to promote a friendly, organized, clean and safe hospital environment.

We Care for Small Animal, Avian, and Exotic Pets

Our team provides Comprehensive Veterinary Care
for all of your pet's medical needs:
  • Wellness Exams and Vaccinations
  • Parasite Prevention
  • Soft Tissue and Orthopedic Surgery
  • Dentistry
  • X-ray and Ultrasound
  • In-house Lab Testing
  • Pharmacy & Prescription Food
  • Microchip Implantation
  • Emergency Care
  • Full In House Pharmacy
  • Laser Therapy
