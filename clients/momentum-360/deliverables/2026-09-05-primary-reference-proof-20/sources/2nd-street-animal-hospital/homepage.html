Before you come in

Common Questions

Do you see exotic pets?

Yes — snakes, parrots, rabbits, chameleons, and more. Our board-certified avian specialist Dr. Meade leads our hospital. Visit Our Exotic Hub

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we’re actively welcoming new dogs, cats, and exotic patients. Book online or Call (215) 516-7000.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 8am–7pm · Saturday 9am–4pm · Sunday closed. For life-threatening after-hours emergencies, see our Urgent care page for Partner ERs.

Do you offer same-day appointments?
We triage every call and book same-day when we can. For life-threatening emergencies we triage and refer to overnight ER partners.
Do you accept referrals from other veterinarians?

Yes — especially for CT imaging, orthopedic surgery (TPLO), avian, and exotic cases.
