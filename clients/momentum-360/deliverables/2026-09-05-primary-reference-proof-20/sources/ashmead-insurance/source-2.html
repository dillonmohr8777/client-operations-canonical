About Ashmead Insurance Associates

Proudly serving Colmar and the Lansdale area with guidance shaped by decades of local relationships and a commitment to helping clients make informed insurance decisions.

Built on Local History and Long-Term Service

Ashmead Insurance Associates traces its roots back to January 1, 1957, when William Ashmead Sr. and Raymond H. Miller merged their agencies to better serve clients through stronger service and broader insurance solutions. Their approach focused on personalized support, practical advice, and customer service standards that stood out in the industry at the time. Today, the agency continues that tradition from its Colmar office while supporting clients throughout Lansdale, North Wales, Hatfield, and surrounding communities. With leadership grounded in continuity and a commitment to helping clients make informed decisions, the agency remains focused on building trust that lasts over time.

Meet the Team

What You Can Expect From Us Every Day

Our mission is to provide clear, reliable insurance guidance so individuals and businesses can make decisions with confidence.﻿

Independent Choice

Working with an independent broker means you’re not limited to one carrier’s options; you get access to multiple solutions that can be matched to your situation. This makes it easier to compare coverage and feel confident in the direction you choose.

Local Accountability

Having a physical office in Colmar means you’re working with a team that’s part of the same community and easy to reach when you need help. That local presence brings a level of consistency and responsiveness you can rely on.

Clear Communication

Insurance can feel complex when details aren’t explained clearly, especially with business policies or bundled coverage. Straightforward guidance helps you understand tradeoffs and make decisions without second-guessing.

Long-Term Support

Coverage needs change over time, whether you’re growing a business or updating your home and assets. Ongoing reviews and support help keep your policies aligned as your situation evolves.

Serving Local Clients Since 1957 With Independent Guidance

Choosing insurance coverage often means making decisions that affect your business, family, property, or future for years to come. Many people want more than a quick quote—they want to understand their options and work with someone who will still be there when questions, claims, or changes come up later. Ashmead Insurance Associates was founded in 1957 and continues to operate as an independent broker serving businesses, individuals, and families with personalized coverage guidance. By combining risk-management planning, access to multiple carriers, and a client-first approach, the agency helps people make informed decisions without unnecessary pressure or confusion.
