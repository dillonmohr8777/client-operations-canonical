Periodontal disease
The word periodontal means “around the tooth.” Periodontal disease attacks the gums and the bone that support the teeth. Plaque is a sticky film of food debris, bacteria, and saliva. If plaque is not removed, it turns into calculus (tartar). When plaque and calculus are not removed, they begin to destroy the gums and bone. Periodontal disease is characterized by red, swollen, and bleeding gums.
Routine professional cleanings and periodontal treatments are a crucial part of preventive
dental care
. Left untreated, gum disease can lead to tooth loss and even affect your overall health, increasing risks for heart disease and diabetes. By including regular visits and good home hygiene practices, you protect both your smile and your total-body wellness.

Cleanings & Prevention
A preventive program is a cooperative effort by the patient, dentist, and dental staff to preserve the natural dentition and supporting structures by preventing the onset, progress, and recurrence of dental diseases and conditions. Preventive
dental services
such as sealants, fluoride treatments, and oral hygiene coaching give patients the tools they need for a lifetime of a
healthy smile
.
Regular
preventive visits
also allow us to detect early signs of decay or gum disease, so treatment can be simple and minimally invasive. Prevention remains the most affordable way to maintain excellent oral health.

Dental Implants: A Permanent Solution for Tooth Loss
Living with missing teeth can affect more than your smile. It can limit your diet, disrupt speech, and lower your self-esteem.
Dental implants
are a state-of-the-art solution that allows you to replace missing teeth with permanent, stable, and natural-looking alternatives.
Single dental implants for individual tooth replacement
Implant-supported bridges to replace several teeth in a row
All-on-X
full-arch implants for patients missing most or all teeth
﻿
Read More
Our implant process begins with a thorough evaluation, which includes a 3D CBCT scan to assess bone density and structure. If needed, we offer bone grafting or sinus lifts to prepare your mouth for implants. Once placed, your implants integrate with your jawbone, offering unparalleled strength and longevity. Implants are the gold standard in tooth replacement because they:
Preserve the jawbone and prevent facial collapse
Look and feel like natural teeth
Don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support
Offer a 90–95% long-term success rate
If you’ve struggled with dentures that slip or if you avoid social gatherings because of missing teeth, it’s time to explore dental implants at 360 Dental PC. They are an investment in your oral health, your appearance, and your ability to enjoy life with a confident, healthy smile.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do you accept walk-ins?
Yes, especially for emergencies. For routine visits, we encourage you to schedule in advance; however, we will do our best to accommodate walk-ins.
When should I bring my child to the dentist for the first time?
The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a visit by their first birthday or when the first tooth erupts.
Is sedation dentistry safe?
Absolutely. We offer both nitrous oxide and oral sedation, administered by trained professionals, with careful monitoring throughout your visit.
How often should I get a dental cleaning?
Most patients benefit from cleanings every 6 months. If you have gum disease or buildup, you may need more frequent visits.
Are implants covered by insurance?
Sometimes, depending on your plan. We’ll provide a breakdown and help you understand your options before starting treatment.
