OUR SERVICES
Comprehensive Care for Every Pet
Art City Vets provides comprehensive veterinary care for dogs and cats in Philadelphia, from walk-in urgent care, wellness exams to surgery, dentistry, diagnostic testing, and specialized treatments. Our veterinary team provides personalized care for pets at every stage of life.
ER & Urgent Care
Walk-in urgent veterinary care for dogs
and cats experiencing illness, injury, or concerning symptoms. Open seven days a week in Philadelphia.
Learn About Urgent Care
Wellness Care
Routine wellness exams, vaccinations, preventive care, and personalized health plans for dogs and cats at every life stage.
Learn About Wellness
Surgery
Skilled veterinary surgical care with thoughtful treatment planning, attentive monitoring, and compassionate  support throughout recovery.
