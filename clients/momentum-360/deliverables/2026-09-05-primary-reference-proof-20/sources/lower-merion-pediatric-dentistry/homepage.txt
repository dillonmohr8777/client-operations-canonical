Meet Dr. John Horchos!
Our new owner of Lower Merion Pediatric Dentistry.
Dr. Horchos has been practicing pediatric dentistry for more than 20 years and is board certified by the
            American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. He is a graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental
            Medicine and completed his residency specializing in pediatric dentistry at St. Christopher's Hospital for
            Children. He was named chief resident in his second year. Prior to Lower Merion Pediatric Dentistry, he
            practiced in Center City Philadelphia and Elkins Park and has taught at both the University of Pennsylvania
            and Temple's Kornberg School of Dentistry, where he served as associate clinical professor of pediatric
            dentistry.
