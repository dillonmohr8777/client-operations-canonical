Welcome to Brandywine Veterinary Hospital - Your Veterinarians In Chadds Ford, PA & Greenville, DE
At Brandywine Veterinary Hospital, we treat your pets like part of our family -- and our practice has been welcoming families and pets just like yours for over 60 years. As part of the Brandywine Valley community, our animal hospital provides quality veterinary care to each and every pet we see. We provide ongoing lifetime wellness care to animals, and we welcome you to our family. We serve Chadds Ford, PA, and Greenville, DE.
Brandywine Veterinary Hospital: Our Services
We provide core wellness veterinary services to pets and their families. This includes wellness visits, checkups, senior pet visits, and regular shots and medication. We also encourage microchipping in case your pet gets lost, and are happy to handle the microchipping process for you.
Preventative Veterinary Care
Our preventative care includes exams for pets at every stage of life, including veterinary nutritional counseling, so you can ensure you're feeding your pet food that provides them with the nutrients they need as they go through life. We also believe in dental cleaning and care as part of wellness for pets and are able to provide these services as well. With clinics in Chadds Ford and Greenville, our veterinarians are ready to welcome your pet. Make an appointment today.
Veterinary Surgery
Our veterinarians are practiced, skilled professionals. Our veterinary staff is capable of performing the following common, routine surgeries:
Orthopedic Surgery
ACL Repairs
Fracture Repairs
Dental Surgery
Spay & Neuter
Soft Tissue Removal
When your pet is undergoing surgery or another procedure, our staff can provide you with updates and let you know when your pet is awake and ready to come home.
Pet Therapies
When a pet undergoes an injury or needs special treatment, we can accommodate this beloved member of your family. Our therapies and rehabilitative services include:
Platelet rich plasma (PRP)
Underwater treadmill
Laser
Stress and Anxiety Reduction for Your Pet
Just like humans, animals do get stressed and tense. This one reason why a veterinarian on our team can provide acupuncture services. Behavioral care can be a crucial part of pet care, and we're committed to supporting you in making healthy choices for your pet's wellbeing.

Meet Our Skilled Veterinarians
Bradley
S. Gray
VMD CCRP
Veterinarian
Dr. Gray is the owner of the practice and is a second-generation veterinarian. His father, Dr. Harry L. Gray, started this practice in 1957.
Dr. Gray graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Veterinary Medicine. While Dr. Gray has a diverse skillset, his primary interests are in soft tissue and orthopedic surgeries. He is also certified in Canine Rehabilitation. He currently shares his home with two Black Labs, a Great Pyrenees cross, and two cats.
Amanda
Vaile
DVM
Veterinarian
Dr. Amanda Vaile was born and raised in Delaware, and graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Pre-Veterinary Medicine and Animal Biosciences from the University of Delaware. She went on to receive her Doctor of Veterinary Medicine from Oklahoma State University - Go Pokes! She spent the first six years of her career practicing in Fort Worth, Texas and recently moved back to the area. Dr. Vaile’s professional interests include preventive medicine, dermatology, internal medicine, and behavior. Her top priority is partnering with clients to provide exceptional care for their pets. In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with her husband, son, and their two dogs and two cats. Outside of the clinic, you can typically find her hiking, exploring national parks, enjoying live music, or spending time with family and friends.
