We are proud to be your medical home.

At our office, we believe that raising a child is a rewarding as well as a challenging experience for a parent. Our goal is to help you enjoy this experience and to ensure that your child's full potential is reached. We believe that healthy children are the results of healthy families and communities.
Meet Our Providers

Dr. Marcie E. Macolino, MD

Dr. Marcie E. Macolino, MD, completed her Bachelor of Science in Biology at Wheaton College and earned her Medical Degree from Temple University School of Medicine. She is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics and has passed the National Board Exams. Dr. Macolino is a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics and has been a valued member of Mt. Airy Pediatrics since completing her residency at St. Christopher's Hospital for Children in 1996. She also served as an intern at the same institution. Throughout her career, she has been actively involved in volunteer work, including projects with Habitat for Humanity and medical internships in Belize and Honduras through Mercy Ships. Residing in Germantown with her husband Jerry, who works as a computer consultant, Dr. Macolino is a proud parent to four children, with Rachel currently attending high school. In her leisure time, she enjoys savoring tea, exploring Longwood Gardens, and delving into a good book.

Susan W. Nordlof, MSN, CRNP

Susan W. Nordlof, MSN, CRNP, received her Bachelor of Arts from Wheaton College and both her Bachelor of Science in Nursing and Master of Science in Nursing from the University of Pennsylvania. A dedicated member of the National Association of Pediatric Nurse Practitioners, Susan has been living in the neighborhood since August 2001, when she joined Mt. Airy Pediatrics as the first pediatric nurse practitioner. She has also enjoyed teaching pediatric nurse practitioner students at the University of Pennsylvania as a clinical faculty member and preceptor. Earlier in her career, Susan practiced as a pediatric nurse practitioner in Lancaster, PA, contributed to various children's health projects in West Philadelphia during her nursing studies, and worked as a home visitor and drop-in group coordinator for adolescent mothers in Chicago. Originally from Bucks County, PA, Susan has spent most of the past 20 years in Philadelphia. She is the mother of two and, outside of work, she can often be found at local ball fields, coffee shops, libraries, or community meetings. Susan lives in Mt. Airy with her husband and children.

Danica Mae Lagman, CRNP

I am a certified pediatric nurse practitioner specializing in primary care. My bachelor's and master's degrees are both from the University of Pennsylvania. I have previously worked on the inpatient Oncology and Bone Marrow Transplant units at the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia as a registered nurse. During this time, I also served as a nurse preceptor and harm prevention champion, fostering my interest for education and primary prevention. Working with the oncology population has continued to nurture my love and appreciation for the resiliency and curiosity of kids as well as the importance of family-centered care and community, and I strive to reflect these interests in my everyday clinical practice. In my free time, I love to read, craft, run, and spend time with loved ones. Originally from Massachusetts, I came to Philadelphia for my undergraduate education, have grown to love this city, and have stayed ever since.
