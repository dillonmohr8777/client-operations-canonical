About Glocker Realty & Insurance
Glocker Realty & Insurance is a locally owned, independent real estate and insurance company dedicated to providing our clients with unparalleled personal service.  We have been apart of the community for over 50 years and current owners
Martin Slater
and
Matthew Kennedy
strive to continue that tradition.
Our team of real estate professionals, administrative staff, and brokers are exceedingly knowledgeable and ready to assist you with selling your current property, or helping you find the home of your dreams.  We hope you take a few minutes to browse our site and learn why we provide
RESULTS THAT MOVE YOU
.
