Welcome to
The New Pennsburg Diner.
Our family owned business has been serving the local community and new faces alike since 1981, providing a warm family atmosphere. You will be pleased with our menu variety. From breakfast served all day, lunch specials, steaks & seafood to homemade comfort food. Always with a smile and a warm heart.
Come enjoy your family & friends with us. Schedule your events. We can accommodate almost every request.
