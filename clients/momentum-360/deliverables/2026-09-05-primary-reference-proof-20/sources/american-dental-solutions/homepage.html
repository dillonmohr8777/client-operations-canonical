Smiles are Made at American Dental Solutions

Are you looking for a Dentist, Periodontist, or Oral Surgeon? Well then you came to the right place! American Dental Solutions has been making families smile in Southeast PA since 1979.

We believe that quality dental care, orthodontic care and oral surgery should be convenient and affordable for every family. So we have a team of specialists on hand, ready to take care of all of your family’s dental needs. We provide a wide range of services including General Dentistry, Braces, Oral Surgery, Endodontics, Periodontics, and Dentures under one roof to help make taking care of your smile easy and convenient. We also offer emergency services!

Visit Us Today at American Dental Solutions

Find a location near you – Our team serves communities local to Berks, Chester, Lancaster and Montgomery counties in Southeast Pennsylvania. We invite you to visit any one of our 15 convenient locations, to see how a beautifully restored smile from American Dental Solutions can change your life! Above all, our compassionate team strives to make every patient feel comfortable and to deliver great results with an even better experience.

We accept most insurances. Don’t have dental insurance? No problem! We offer an in-house dental plan and easy payment options to help keep costs affordable for you and your family.

Schedule your dentist appointment online, or by calling us at 1-844-9SMILES. We look forward to seeing you!
