Frequently Asked Questions – Wayne's Garage Inc

Have some questions? We're here to help!

Who is the best auto repair shop near me in Philadelphia?

We may be a little biased, but many local drivers trust Wayne’s Garage Inc as their go to auto repair shop. As a long standing, family owned business, we have built our reputation on dependable service, honest communication, and quality workmanship. From routine maintenance to more complex repairs, our team is committed to keeping your vehicle safe and reliable.

Do you work on all makes and models?

Yes. Wayne’s Garage Inc services most cars, trucks, and SUVs, including both domestic and import vehicles. No matter what you drive, our technicians have the knowledge and equipment to properly diagnose and repair your vehicle.

Why is routine maintenance important for my vehicle?

Routine maintenance helps prevent unexpected breakdowns and costly repairs. Services like oil changes, brake inspections, fluid checks, and alignment evaluations keep your vehicle running efficiently and safely. We follow manufacturer recommendations and help you create a maintenance plan that fits your driving habits.

Why is my check engine light on?

A check engine light can indicate anything from a minor issue to a more serious engine or emissions problem. Ignoring it can lead to bigger and more expensive repairs. Our technicians use advanced diagnostic tools to identify the cause, explain the issue clearly, and recommend the correct repair.
