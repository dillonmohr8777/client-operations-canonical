We Make Custom Parts to Fuel Innovations

We form parts from metal sheets destined for applications where high precision and durability are needed. Our typical end-use applications are chassis, enclosures, fixtures, frames, arms, and brackets.

 

Our services provide a fast and effective solution for any part. Davidson Farbicating, Inc offers a broad range of sheet metal materials, including aluminum, steel, stainless steel, copper, and assembly services, including certified TIG-MIG welding and painting services.

Get Your Custom Sheet Metal Parts Now

As a leading sheet metal fabricator, we convert in-house your CAD and technical drawings into manufactured parts. Our waterjet or plasma cutting operation will remove precisely sheet metal materials. Then, we will form the material, weld, add inserts, and finish the part. Finally, our dedicated and courteous customer service specialists will coordinate your on-time delivery.

 

Over decades, we have developed a continuous improvement and Agile approach that allows us to make parts for many different applications and industries, including aerospace and aviation, military and defense, automotive, oil and energy, industrial, transports, consumer goods, medical, and dental products.
