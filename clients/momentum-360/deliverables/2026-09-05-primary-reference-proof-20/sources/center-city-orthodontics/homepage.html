Philadelphia Orthodontist

Dr. Tejjy Thomas is a Philadelphia Orthodontist who provides innovative and efficient care right in Philadelphia. One of the most advanced Philadelphia orthodontists, he utilizes the latest technology and modern best practices for his patients.

Orthodontics is no longer a specialty that is just creating perfect smiles and aligning teeth, but has now advanced to creating a balance between facial structures, airways, temporomandibular joint (TMJ) and the teeth.

We are a specialist Philadelphia orthodontist practice that provides customized, comprehensive treatment to patients of all ages, located on South Street in downtown. We are a forward thinking Philadelphia orthodontist office, with the latest in clinical techniques and warm, friendly professionally trained staff.
