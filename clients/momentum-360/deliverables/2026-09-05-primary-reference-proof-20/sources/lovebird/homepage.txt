Lovebird is a high quality chicken restaurant changing the way fried chicken is done. Created by the owners of Jules Thin Crust, Lovebird is now open for business in Blue Bell, PA, Bryn Mawr, PA, Doylestown, PA, and Newtown, PA. Serving up delicious, made-to-order sandwiches, salads, quinoa bowls and gluten free options, Lovebird is redefining the way we eat fried chicken.
