Our Purpose

Located in the heart of Old City, Philadelphia, Center City Veterinary Hospital provides compassionate, personalized care for dogs, cats, and exotic pets and the people who love them.

Our commitment to preventive wellness helps pets enjoy healthier, longer lives, while our advanced veterinary medicine allows us to diagnose and treat illnesses and injuries at every stage of life. From routine wellness exams to complex medical care and end of life support, we're here for your pet through every milestone.

We believe veterinary visits should be as comfortable and positive as possible. As a practice certified in low-stress handling, we prioritize your pet's emotional well being alongside their physical health, creating a calmer, more positive experience for every visit.

New patients are always welcome!
