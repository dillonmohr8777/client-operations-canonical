Why Choose Concord Pike Veterinary Hospital

Our Commitment to You

Our hospital opened its doors in 1972 and offers a wide range of medical services and surgeries for dogs and cats. Our team strives to provide a comfortable environment for our patients and clients and we want everyone, either two legged or four, to feel welcome as soon as they enter our veterinary clinic.

Reminder: Summer is a fun time for your pets but be aware of heat exhaustion, sun burn, bites & stings. Provide plenty of fresh water & limit outdoor ativities. Don’t forget to pack any medications when traveling.

Appointments

Our appointment book is computerized which allows us to efficiently make appointments for you and your pet. Our receptionists and team will attempt to accommodate all requests to the best of our ability. If you feel you have an emergency with your pet, please contact us immediately. It is best to call before coming in so that a staff member can advise you on your particular emergency.
