Skippack’s Italian Market is as much like a country store as your favorite first-class deli . You’re sure to be charmed and enticed by the atmosphere and mouth-watering aromas that greet you inside this hidden little gem in the heart of Skippack Village, Pennsylvania.

 

Besides boasting some of the best hoagies, sandwiches in Montgomery County, The market has a variety of assorted meats, cheeses, salads, home made soups and grab-n-go take out dinners.

Wooden shelves fill the front room, packed full of the authentic Italian groceries, and packaged sweets that keep hungry visitors coming back.
