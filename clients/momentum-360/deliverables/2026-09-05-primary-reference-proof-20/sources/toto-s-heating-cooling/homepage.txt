About Toto’s Heating & Cooling
Toto’s Heating, Cooling & Commercial Refrigeration has been servicing the local area since 1992.  After completing his education in Air Conditioning and Refrigeration, Charles Toto started his business to strictly repair commercial refrigeration.  Because of his knowledge and prompt, reliable customer service, his commercial customers quickly put their trust in him and he included walk in box installations and glycol beer systems to his list of services.
Soon after, customers started to request his services on their heating and air conditioning systems at their businesses as well as their homes.  So, Charles expanded his services to include both commercial and residential HVAC as well as water heaters, beer systems and whole house generators.
As a result, Toto’s now has technicians who specialize in commercial refrigeration as well as cooling and heating systems for residential as well as commercial applications.  Toto’s has established many career long relationships with our customers because we truly do care about our customers.  Today, our customer base ranges from homes and restaurants to school districts and geriatric hospitals.
Our technicians are experienced and trained.  They undergo background checks as well as drug testing and continual training.  We design, service, and install commercial refrigeration, air conditioning and heating systems of all makes and models.
Our company prides itself on our commitment to customers and our prompt, quality services. With our superior service, let us help you with all your HVAC and commercial refrigeration needs.
Count on Toto’s to deliver nothing but the best customer experience. We know how important the comfort of your home and business is for your family, employees and customers.  As a small business, we know it is critical to keep your equipment running smoothly, whether it is your HVAC or commercial refrigeration equipment.  We pride ourselves on our dedication and timeliness.  See what our customers have to say about us below.
