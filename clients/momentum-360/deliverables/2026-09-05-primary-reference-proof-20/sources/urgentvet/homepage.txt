At Your Service
Heather Marchant
Practice Manager
Heather brings 20 years of veterinary experience to the team. Growing up in West Virginia, Heather’s connection to animals has been a lifelong passion. She feels as though the veterinary profession was made for her, a sentiment she embraces every day. From a young age, Heather knew she was destined to work with animals, and it has shaped her career and life.
One of Heather’s most cherished memories in the veterinary field involves fostering medical cases with her wife, including a remarkable set of “swimmer pups” who were rescued from neglectful conditions. Their inspiring journey was featured in a viral
The Dodo
video, and Heather will forever treasure the memory of watching these three puppies overcome the odds and thrive.
With 20 years of experience, Heather has worked in a variety of veterinary settings, including general practice, emergency care, specialty medicine, and rescue work. She’s also fostered over 70 animals, demonstrating her dedication to animal welfare beyond her work at UrgentVet.
Outside of work, Heather enjoys spending time with her wife and their “menagerie” of animals, including dogs, horses, and goats. She also has a love for classic Dodge trucks, adding a bit of vintage charm to her already eclectic lifestyle.
Heather’s expertise, combined with her passion for helping animals and their families, makes her a vital part of the UrgentVet Pottstown team, where she continues to deliver compassionate care to all pets in need.

Meet Van Der Wall! This adorable boy recently visited us at UrgentVet due to some noticeable limping that had his family concerned. After a thorough examination by our veterinarian, we were able to identify the issue and provide the necessary care.

Bella
Pottstown, PA
Say hello to Bella! This sweet girl was brought to UrgentVet by her concerned pet parents after they noticed she had been experiencing vomiting, diarrhea, and a decreased appetite that started earlier in the day.
Our dedicated team quickly attended to Bella, and after a thorough examination with our veterinarian, we performed some diagnostics to get to the root of the issue. To help Bella feel better, she was given medication to ease her nausea and an antibiotic to address any underlying infection. We’re hopeful that Bella will be back to her happy, healthy self in no time!
