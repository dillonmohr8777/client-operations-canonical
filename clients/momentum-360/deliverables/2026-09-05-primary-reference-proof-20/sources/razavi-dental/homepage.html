Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Leily Razavi

Dr. Razavi received her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the  University of Toronto (#1 dental school in Canada). She completed her residency at Albert Einstein Medical Center in Philadelphia before joining the University of Pennsylvania Faculty Practice.

A believer in ongoing education, Dr. Razavi has invested deeply in continuing education. Her love of art and eye for esthetics has led to a passion for minimally invasive cosmetic, general and implant dentistry. She is an avid student of Spear and a member of AACD.

Dr. Razavi lives in Center City with her husband and daughter. She is recognized as the best cosmetic dentist in Philadelphia by Philadelphia Magazine six years running.
