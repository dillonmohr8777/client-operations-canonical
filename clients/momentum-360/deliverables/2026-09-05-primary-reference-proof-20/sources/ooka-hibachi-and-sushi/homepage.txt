WILLOW GROVE
This intimate restaurant, open since 2000 and conveniently located close to major transport routes, offers both a Hibachi and dining solution to satisfy your appetite. Feel free to bring along a special bottle of wine or sake from your cellar and enjoy it at this B.Y.O.B. sushi jewel. OOKA is the proud recipient of a Best of Philly award (sushi). The main reason for earning such award is the fact that OOKA strives to offer guests only the freshest and most pristine dishes, including fish which is flown in daily from around the world.
