## Best-practice checks

Google states that AI Overviews and AI Mode use existing SEO requirements; special AI files or special schema are not required. Eligible pages need indexing and snippet eligibility, but eligibility does not guarantee inclusion. Maintain crawl access, useful internal links, textual facts, matching structured data and current business details. Search Console includes AI-feature traffic within Web reporting; do not present it as an isolated AI-attribution dataset. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

For video search, distinguish an article containing a supporting clip from a page whose main purpose is watching one video. Use stable media/thumbnail URLs and accurate video metadata; indexing the page alone does not establish that the video is indexed. Select the verified final render and provide accessible captions/transcript. [Google video guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video)

Before publication: verify one clear H1, canonical target, indexability, factual business/entity consistency, useful alt text, internal links, valid sitemap entries, intended 404 behavior, mobile reading/CTA, and schema matching visible facts. This brief does not claim those site checks were performed.
