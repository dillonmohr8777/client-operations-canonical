## Rule for the factory

For every UI surface and every 25-site batch, use a relevant DesignSpells
category as an optional source of interaction ideas only when it supports the
page goal. Record the selected spell URL or slug in the local brief or
evidence manifest. Do not copy the visual treatment as a default, and do not
let the reference override verified business facts, brand evidence, the
factory's distinct art direction, accessibility, or human review.

Promote an interaction into a local reusable token or component only when the
same intent is needed across at least three surfaces. Preserve the existing
site-factory grammar and the brand-specific interaction language already
established across the portfolio.

## CTA scroll acceptance

CTA motion must clarify hierarchy or state, begin on intentional visibility or
interaction, remain brief and interruptible, preserve touch and keyboard use,
and provide a reduced-motion-safe fallback. It must not make the CTA harder
to use, cause overflow or layout shift, remove focus visibility, or put
meaning behind motion. QA covers desktop, mobile, touch, keyboard, reduced
motion, overflow, layout shift, console output, and purposeful use of motion.
