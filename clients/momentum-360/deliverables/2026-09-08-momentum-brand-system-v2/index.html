Reusable scrapbook foundations

Use sb-paper for a sheet, sb-layer for an offset paper stack, sb-frame for an inset plate, sb-tab for navigation labels, sb-label for specimen labels, sb-tape for a restrained fastening detail, sb-cutout-caption for a caption strip, sb-index for structured notes and sb-source for evidence. The real logo.
Every single time.

Identity is a fixed asset. Expression happens around it. These source files are copied exactly, with their original aspect ratios and transparency.

Use the full lockup on calm, light fields. Keep at least half a mark-height of clear space. Use the mark only where brand context is already clear. No redraws, recoloring, stretching, animated logos, or invented AI lockups.

Verified commitments and proposed changes

Existing source commitments: Momentum Digital logo assets; brand blue and orange; Archivo Black and Nunito Sans. Proposed v2 choices: editorial composition, semantic SVG family, workflow motion, reading and end-card rules. Every v2 choice is a review draft, not approved brand policy.

Momentum Digital, AI division and Momentum 360

This board covers Momentum Digital and its AI division. Momentum 360 remains a separate subbrand with its own verified identity assets. Do not use the Digital lockup as a replacement for a 360 logo. No 360 logo is supplied in this draft.
