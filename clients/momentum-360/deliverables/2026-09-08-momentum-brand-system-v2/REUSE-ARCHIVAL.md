# Momentum fieldnotes v2.1

Import tokens.css, then scrapbook.css. Add class="scrapbook" to body. Copy the assets directory unchanged with the styles. The exact existing logos and original ten icons retain their names and bytes.

## Icons
assets/icons-agents.svg: research, writer, designer, analyst, planner, builder, reviewer, coordinator, support, automator, memory, handoff.
assets/icons-marketing.svg: search, paid-ads, email, social, content, video, landing-page, funnel, crm, campaign, audience, conversion, reporting, calendar, experiment, brand.
Use icon-agents-NAME.svg or icon-marketing-NAME.svg for standalone downloads. Both sets have -etched.svg sprites with matching IDs. Clean versions at 24/32px; etching at 44px and larger. Apply .icon to consuming SVG, label controls visibly. Never use a glyph as a logo.

## Paper and reading
sb-paper: ivory fiber sheet. sb-layer: paper stack. sb-frame: inset plate. sb-tab: collection tab. sb-label: specimen label. sb-tape: restrained fastening detail. sb-cutout-caption: caption strip. sb-index: structured field note. sb-source: evidence annotation. Use selectively but keep the underlying material consistent across ebook chapters, contents, scorecards and web components.

## Motion
.sb-title > span groups headline lines. Toggle .play to reveal, with stagger. .sb-settle.play gives one subtle sheet settle. .sb-trace activates fine etched detail on hover/focus. The body .paused class resolves all animations to readable static state. prefers-reduced-motion turns everything off. Body text never moves. No logo animation.

## Typography and identity
Archivo Black: retained display. Nunito Sans: retained reading. Cormorant Garamond Italic: archival chapter and specimen voice. Blue/orange remain original brand colors; ivory/patina is the user-directed material extension. Exact Digital lockup remains on a calm light field. 360 remains separate.
