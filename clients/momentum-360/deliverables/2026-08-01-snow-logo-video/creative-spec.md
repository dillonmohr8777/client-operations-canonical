# Momentum 360 Snow Logo Film

## Outcome

Create a 21-second, 16:9 Momentum 360 brand film that closely follows the supplied X reference: a lone winter athlete crosses untouched snow, their paired tracks progressively reveal the Momentum 360 mark, and the film resolves to a precise branded end card with the slogan `need momentum?`.

## Locked production contract

- Client: Momentum 360 (`momentum-360`)
- Format: MP4, H.264 video with AAC audio
- Canvas: 1920 x 1080, landscape 16:9
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Target duration: 21 seconds
- Primary surface: web, LinkedIn, X, and landscape social placements
- Presenter: none
- Spoken narration: none
- Audio: cinematic ambient score, snow movement, low wind, restrained whooshes
- Exact on-screen slogan: `need momentum?`
- Exact brand name on end card: `Momentum 360`
- Authoritative corporate mark: `assets/momentum-360-logo-approved.png`
- Final display derivative: `assets/momentum-360-logo-approved-circle.png`, which only clips the approved square file to the circular display boundary required by the brand guide
- Generator tracing reference: `assets/momentum-360-logo-2048.png`
- Typography: Space Grotesk 700 where available; Segoe UI Bold fallback

## Reference structure

1. Opening: wide aerial shot over pristine snow. One small athlete moves away from camera.
2. Text build: `need` appears first, then completes to `need momentum?` in the upper left.
3. Track choreography: paired tracks curve and loop with increasingly high aerial angles.
4. Recognition beat: the full snow-traced Momentum 360 circle, stylized M path, and arrow become readable from directly overhead.
5. Transition: the snow outline dissolves into the approved corporate mark on Momentum Navy.
6. End card: exact mark, `Momentum 360`, and `need momentum?` with generous clear space.

## Brand constraints

- Do not redraw, recolor, distort, crop, morph, or place text over the corporate mark.
- Preserve the watercolor blues, white M, circular ring, and gold arrow.
- The snow track sequence may interpret the mark as a physical path, but the final digital reveal must use the approved artwork. The official square source may be clipped to a circle for display, without changing pixels inside that circle.
- Keep the visual system grounded and cinematic. Avoid generic blue-purple AI gradients, neon overload, sci-fi dashboards, fake metrics, robots, or cross-client assets.
- Use natural winter blue, white, Momentum Navy `#071632`, Orbit Blue `#2F82FF`, and Signal Gold `#F2B63D` only as restrained accents.

## Quality gates

- First three seconds establish motion and show the slogan beginning to build.
- No gibberish or misspelled text.
- No warped or substituted logo.
- No extra athletes, duplicated bodies, broken limbs, melted skis, or discontinuous tracks.
- Paired tracks remain coherent across shots and clearly resolve into the Momentum 360 silhouette.
- End card holds long enough to read on mobile.
- Full playback, representative frames, audio balance, codec, dimensions, frame rate, duration, and final frame are inspected before delivery.
