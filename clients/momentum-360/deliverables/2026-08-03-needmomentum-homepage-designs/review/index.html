On the SEO question

Jenny asked whether we can build up SEO on needmomentum.com without interfering with what momentumvirtualtours.com already ranks for. The answer is yes, and this homepage is the part that carries no risk at all.

  • Shipping this page changes no URLs and fires no redirects. The virtual-tours site keeps every ranking it has today.
  • Both domains are already on the same host, and needmomentum.com already owns /virtual-tours/ and /momentum-360/ — so a later consolidation is a configuration change, not a migration onto new infrastructure.
  • needmomentum.com is the broader property (roughly 85+ indexable pages against a deeper but narrower tour cluster), which makes it the correct destination if you do consolidate later.
  • The tour library stays linked from this page, so its equity keeps flowing while the brand moves.

Full recommendation, redirect table and the client-side steps are in docs/SEO-CONSOLIDATION.md.

Before this goes live

  1. Three brand names are in play. The live needmomentum.com is branded “Momentum Digital”, the logo wordmark reads “MOMENTUM”, and the tours brand is “Momentum 360”. These builds standardise on Momentum with 360 as a service — confirm that is the call.
  2. Two phone numbers, likely two Google Business Profiles (215-876-2954 and 215-607-6482). This is the highest-risk item for local rankings and needs GBP access to resolve.
  3. Address conflict: the site says 1635 Market Street, the Modern Luxury spread prints 1633. One is wrong, and NAP inconsistency hurts local SEO.
  4. Two award badges are too small to ship well (Best of Pennsylvania at 89px, Google Partner at 89×50). Re-source at 400px or larger; Google Partner is currently rendered as a text credential instead.
  5. No owned 360 panorama exists in our library. Both builds argue the tour service with instrumentation rather than a tour still. Pull three frames from the Matterport account and both pages get stronger.
  6. Client logos are held out (Berkshire Hathaway, Penn State, Wells Fargo) until written permission is on file.
  7. A draft page is leaking into Google: needmomentum.com/about-us-new-draft/ is indexed and competing with /about-us/. Worth fixing regardless of which design wins.
