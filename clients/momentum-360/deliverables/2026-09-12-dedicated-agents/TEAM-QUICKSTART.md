# Momentum Answers: team quick start

## Current status

Momentum Answers is paused during its provider migration. The FAQ and OpenRouter
provider are disabled, so do not expect a reply or tag the app for work. The
September 15 FAQ and draft checks are historical acceptance evidence; the
current migration has not been activated. Staff will be notified when it is
ready again.

Open [#momentum-help](https://app.slack.com/client/T066HGS7N/C0C2VSTBQ9W),
choose **Momentum Answers** from Slack's mention picker, then ask your question.
For example: `What should go in our Monday client update?`
Answers point to reviewed company guidance and training resources.

## Five useful draft prompts

Replace the bracketed fields with your facts before sending. Mention the app first.

- **Jason:** `draft jason: Review this lead and draft a follow-up. Source: [source]. Offer: [offer]. Latest interaction: [facts]. Separate known facts, qualification gaps, proposed next step and actual follow-up copy.`
- **Sean:** `draft sean: Turn this work into an operations plan: [scope, known owners, dependencies and deadlines]. Identify missing decisions and acceptance checks. Label proposed owners as proposed.`
- **Mac:** `draft mac: Review this reporting period: [dates, source systems and verified metrics]. Separate supplied numbers from missing ones. Give a reconciliation checklist and draft executive summary without invented revenue or attribution.`
- **Melissa Silber:** `draft melissa-silber: Prepare exactly [number] assets for [audience and goal]. Brand inputs: [facts]. Give each asset its own concept, suggested copy, production direction and missing inputs.`
- **Melissa Rigby:** `draft melissa-rigby: Build a delivery plan for [approved scope]. Known dates and dependencies: [facts]. Include milestones, review steps, acceptance criteria and unresolved scope decisions.`

## Continue or change direction

Mention the app again **in the same thread** to add facts to your draft.
Mention it with `ask: [question]` to return to process questions, `modes` for
the command list, or `stop` to suppress your pending replies in that thread.

Drafts require review. They do not execute CRM changes, send client messages,
publish campaigns or render creative assets. The source catalog is a reviewed
snapshot; linked recordings are resources, not ingested transcripts.

After approval and reactivation, the service will use the configured OpenRouter
provider and still needs its Windows host on, signed in and connected to Slack.
The old local Ollama requirement no longer applies. Do not send company corpus
content or team prompts to the new provider until its data handling is approved.
