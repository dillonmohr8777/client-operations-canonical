# AI Tech News Authored 100 Review

## Product

A private, noindex review collection containing one hundred prospect homepage concepts. The collection preserves the strongest authored work from Philadelphia Batches 2 through 4 and adds the best twenty five pages from the August unslop cohort.

## Audience

Dillon Mohr and the Momentum 360 team reviewing which concepts are ready for Monday outreach preparation.

## Jobs

1. Open any concept quickly from one clean list.
2. Judge the first screen, business identity, imagery, copy, spacing, and mobile composition.
3. See the exact business logo at the top and again at the bottom.
4. Keep every original historical deployment unchanged.

## Nonnegotiable quality contract

- Batch 1 is outside scope.
- Exactly 100 routes: 75 from Batches 2 through 4 and 25 selected unslop routes.
- Every page remains noindex and is presented as a private concept.
- No mail or QR workflow is included.
- No invented facts, redrawn logos, exposed batch counters, encoded visible text, dash heavy copy, broken media, or missing local assets.
- Logos use the first party source artwork and a transparent presentation. They appear only at the top and bottom of the page.
- The opening screen is readable immediately and uses relevant business imagery.
- Existing business specific visual directions are preserved. The review layer fixes quality drift without collapsing the pages into one shared template.

## Evidence boundary

Business facts and source URLs come from the existing concept packages and their embedded structured data. A visual asset is not treated as independently verified merely because it exists in a historical package. The build manifest records the source and transparency checks separately.

## Assumptions inferred from the explicit brief

- “Better intros” means a stronger opening scene and immediately readable business specific hero, not a new generic preloader.
- “No dashes” means removing em dash and en dash punctuation from visitor facing copy while preserving required hyphens in phone numbers, URLs, and business names.
- The new list is staged locally first because creating a new public Netlify site is a separate publication action.
