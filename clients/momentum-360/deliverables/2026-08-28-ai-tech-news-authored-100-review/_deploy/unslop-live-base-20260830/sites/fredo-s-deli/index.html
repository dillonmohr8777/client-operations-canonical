Story

A kitchen with a name on the door.

Fredo's Deli is famous for serving the finest in hearty sandwiches with a back to the basics mentality. You can create your own sandwich using the finest prosciutto, roast beef, corn beef, pastrami, sopressata or turkey breast then top it off with an assortment of different cheeses between fresh Italian, marble rye, wheat, French, baguette, ciabata or pita bread from fresh Bread Works Bread. You can dine-in, take out, or have your meal delivered. Ask us about our uncomparable catering services. Book your next party or watch the next big game in our back party room. You may bring your own beverage in the back room. Fredo's Deli is sure to please both meat lovers and vegetarians. Our famous Godfather sandwich is.

We're just off West Liberty Ave., in picturesque Pittsburgh's Dormont ( map ). Turn on Potomac Avenue and look left. Plenty of parking in front or across the street.
