The three dials
Variance 9. Consecutive sections never share a skeleton. If two can be described with one sentence, the second is wrong.
Motion 8. Staggered entrances, wipes from the bleeding edge, hover that displaces. Nothing shimmers for no reason.
Density 4. Few words, large type, one idea per section. If a section has three points it is three sections.
Banned outright
No AI purple or indigo-to-violet gradients.
No neon glow. Lift a surface with the hard block shadow, signal at 28%.
No glassmorphism, no frosted panels.
No floating gradient orbs behind content.
No three-equal-card feature row. Pairs are 1.5fr to 1fr, a lead and a follow.
No emoji icons. Inline stroke SVG on a 16 to 24px grid.
No generated wordmarks in video. Footage by model, letterforms by hand.
