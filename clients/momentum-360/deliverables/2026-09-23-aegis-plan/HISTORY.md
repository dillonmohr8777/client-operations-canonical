# NeedMomentum.com website operations history

**Cutoff:** 2026-09-23 (America/New_York). **Historical window:** 2026-03-23 through 2026-09-23. **YTD comparison:** 2026-01-01 through 2026-09-23. **Identity:** NeedMomentum.com / Momentum Digital. Momentum Virtual Tours is kept separate; one comparison message is called out below and is not merged into this history.

## Method and limits

- The primary source was the paginated history of Slack channel `#momentumsites` (`C1CFQBC79`), whose stated purpose is NeedMomentum.com. I read the Mar 23–Sep 23 window and a separate Jan 1–Mar 22 pass, then ran 12 bounded lexical searches (`website`, `site`, `SEO`, `lead`, `form`, `email`, `ranking`, `backend`, `downtime`, `security`, `tracking`, and `needmomentum.com`) across accessible public/private Slack. The workspace-wide search was useful for discovery but noisy; the dated claims below use exact messages from the primary channel.
- Gmail was searched read-only with 2026 date bounds and exact NeedMomentum addresses/subject terms. The connector returned mostly client threads, CC matches, or out-of-office notices rather than a clean NeedMomentum website-operations thread. No Gmail result is used as primary evidence below.
- This is an evidence-ranked history, not an uptime, Search Console, GA4, WhatConverts, WordPress, SMTP, or hosting log export. Repeated Slack reports show operational symptoms and attempted fixes; they do not independently prove duration, root cause, or current live state.
- Slack permalinks below are exact message links in `momentum3d.slack.com` and preserve the source date/message identity.
