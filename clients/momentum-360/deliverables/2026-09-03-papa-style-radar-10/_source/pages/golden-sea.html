What we do

On The Menu

Golden Sea Chinese Restaurant has served Blue Bell, PA since 1992, when Jack and Amy Chung opened it after running Golden Light in Horsham. Chef Ka Yam Yip, trained in Hong Kong, spices up the regular menu with recurring specials like Soft-Shell Crabs and whole Chinese Style Sea Bass. The Reporter and The Times Herald voted Golden Sea Best Chinese Restaurant. Regulars can order the full printed menu or one of sixteen numbered combination plates, each paired with an egg roll and fried rice, simply by calling the numbers in.

Special Appetizers

Starters straight off their own menu: egg rolls, shrimp toast, spring rolls, chicken lettuce wraps, and B.B.Q. spare ribs. A short list built for sharing before the main course.

Chow Mein

Served with dry noodles and rice in chicken, roast pork, shrimp, beef, and vegetable versions, plus the house Golden Sea Special Chow Mein. A menu staple since the restaurant opened.

Lo Mein

Soft noodles in vegetable, roast pork, chicken, beef, shrimp, and a House Special version. Ordered by the pint or quart, their own menu's terms.

Sweet & Sour

Sweet & Sour Pork, Chicken, or Shrimp, served with rice. A dine-in and take-out standard on their printed menu.

Seafood

Shrimp and scallops prepared with lobster sauce, bean sprouts, Chinese vegetables, snow peas, or black bean sauce, plus a Walnut Shrimp listed on its own. Their menu marks several with an asterisk for hot and spicy.

Chef's Suggestions

Chef Ka Yam Yip's own specials, including Golden Sea Special Chicken, Seafood Special, Four Seasons, and Szechuan Seafood Deluxe. Their menu calls this section out by name.

About Golden Sea Chinese Restaurant

A Family Kitchen Since 1992

Golden Sea opened in 1992, run by Jack and Amy Chung, who first opened a restaurant called Golden Light in Horsham before moving to Blue Bell. Jack came from mainland China by way of Hong Kong and Newark, working as a seaman before settling down. Amy escaped from Canton by sea in 1974, a seven-hour crossing with her brother Joe and three friends. Their son Andrew grew up in the business and often serves as its spokesperson. Chef Ka Yam Yip, trained in Hong Kong, spices up the regular menu with recurring specials like Soft-Shell Crabs and whole Chinese Style Sea Bass. Jack Chung sums up the philosophy plainly: serve good, fresh food and treat people well, and the rest follows. Golden Sea has been voted Best Chinese Restaurant by The Reporter and The Times Herald.
