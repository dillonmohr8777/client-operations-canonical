What we do

Sales, Service, Parts & More

Weathers Motors has run as a family-owned dealership in Media, PA since 1922, over a century of buying, selling, and servicing cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs. The team stocks quality pre-owned vehicles across domestic and import brands, then backs every sale with a full-service shop: ASE-certified technicians, a parts center, tire sales, PA state inspection, and an in-house body shop. Financing runs through a finance center built for shoppers across the credit spectrum, with online pre-approval and a trade-in appraisal tool. Customers outside Media get a video walkaround, an at-home test drive, home delivery, or maintenance pick-up within 15 miles.

Pre-Owned Vehicle Sales

Quality pre-owned cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs across domestic and import brands. Every vehicle is inspected before it's offered for sale.

Service & Repair Center

ASE-certified technicians handle oil changes, brake work, tire rotations, wheel alignments, transmission service, and general auto and truck repair. The shop services all makes and models since the dealership isn't tied to one brand.

Parts Center

A parts department stocked to support the service shop and walk-in customers looking for the right part for their vehicle.

PA State Inspection & Tires

State inspection and a tire center with discount pricing, done on-site as part of keeping vehicles road-ready year-round.

Body Shop

Collision and body repair handled in-house rather than farmed out to a third party.

Financing & Trade-In

A finance center with online pre-approval for shoppers across the credit spectrum. Sellers can get a KBB instant cash offer and a trade-in appraisal, or sell outright through the we-buy-cars program.

About Weathers Motors

A Century of Family-Owned Service

Weathers Motors has been family-owned and operated in Media, PA since 1922, over a century of serving Delaware County. The dealership buys, sells, and services cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs across domestic brands like Ford, Chevy, and GMC alongside imports like Honda, Hyundai, and Kia. Because the shop isn't tied to one brand, its ASE-certified technicians work on all makes and models. The service department earned the Federated Dealer of the Year Award for its work. Every pre-owned vehicle passes inspection before it reaches the lot, and Carfax One Owner vehicles are available for buyers who want that history. Sellers get a KBB instant cash offer and a trade-in appraisal tool. The finance center works with shoppers across the credit spectrum, and remote services reach customers within 15 miles of Media.

Questions

What areas does Weathers Motors serve?

The dealership is based in Media, PA and serves Delaware County, with customers coming from as far as West Chester to Allentown.

Does Weathers Motors work on vehicles it didn't sell?

Yes. ASE-certified technicians work on all makes and models, not just the brands sold on the lot.

Can I sell or trade my car to Weathers Motors?

Yes. The dealership buys cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs, and offers a KBB instant cash offer, a trade-in appraisal tool, and a we-buy-vehicles program.

Do I have to visit the lot to test drive or buy a car?

No. Customers within 15 miles of Media can request a video walkaround, an at-home test drive, home delivery, or maintenance pick-up.
