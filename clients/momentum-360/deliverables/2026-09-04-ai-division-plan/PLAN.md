## The decision

Launch a three-client founding cohort around a single promise: **make a service business easier to discover, faster to respond, and simpler to operate, with evidence of what changed.** Start with one flagship lead-operations workflow. Offer AI visibility and campaign production as tightly scoped alternatives for accounts whose needs fit them. Keep Mac's four service categories for navigation; do not launch four separate delivery organizations.

The competitive advantage is Momentum's customer relationships, understanding of marketing, implementation, and accountable support. Model access is an input. A customer can buy a chatbot directly; the division must earn its fee by selecting a valuable process, connecting the right systems, verifying the result, and maintaining it.

The first-month target is three signed paid pilots, with at most one completed and referenceable implementation by day 30, and measured delivery economics by day 30. This is a target, not a sales forecast. Referenceability requires client permission, an accepted pilot, and measured outcome evidence. Do not promise three completed or referenceable implementations in week one. Schedule the remaining implementations against actual capacity. The plan does not depend on building a proprietary foundation model or purchasing hardware. [Correction applied 2026-09-07 per QWEN27-REVIEW.md defect 1.]
