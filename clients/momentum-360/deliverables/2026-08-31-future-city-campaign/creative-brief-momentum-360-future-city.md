# Momentum 360 — Make Every Space Move

Status: production-ready concept; paid generation not yet submitted
Primary format: 15 seconds, 9:16, 1080p high-bitrate identity master, followed by a 4K upscale after visual approval
Secondary formats: 1:1 and 16:9, generated or reframed only after the vertical master is approved

## North star

Momentum turns real places into stories people can step inside.

This is a two-film campaign in the visual grammar of the supplied scan-shell reference, not a beat-for-beat clone. A real place exists inside a suspended photogrammetry fragment. Mac Frederick and Sean Boyle move naturally inside that fragment. Momentum's cyan and gold signal maps the environment, traces a 360-degree orbit, and resolves the brand.

End line: **MAKE EVERY SPACE MOVE.**

## 1. Brand character

- Ambitious, precise, local, technically fluent, confident without tech-bro gloss.
- Momentum 360 is the spatial-media brand. Use the exact Momentum 360 mark, never generated text or a guessed logo.
- Mac and Sean are equal-weight founders, but they should not mirror each other like synchronized avatars.
- The brand promise is transformation of a real space into an immersive story, not a generic claim that AI is the future.

## 2. Visual vocabulary

- Clean midnight field (`#041027`) around one suspended photogrammetry paper-shell.
- The shell feels like torn capture data: fibrous edges, point-cloud gaps, folded depth, translucent LiDAR planes, and occasional metallic scan shimmer.
- Real Philadelphia geometry lives inside the shell. Avoid landmark collage, fantasy skyline mashups, and fake street signs.
- Momentum signal colors: cyan/blue dominant, sparse gold for meaningful beats, white only for highlight/specular detail.
- Particles behave semantically: sample surfaces, trace a route, form a 360 orbit, reconstruct architecture, then resolve the mark.
- Faces remain photographic and human. No beauty-filter skin, oversized eyes, face averaging, or age drift.
- No full-frame synthetic environment, random particle soup, dirty gray void, neon cyberpunk city, or illegible generated typography.

## 3. Spatial composition

- The scan-shell occupies roughly 78% of the vertical frame, floating in clean negative space.
- Mac and Sean stay inside the shell. Limbs may break the shell edge only at deliberate impact beats.
- Preserve a readable silhouette for each founder in every hero shot.
- Mac begins frame-left and Sean frame-right, matching the approved founder-film orientation.
- The camera travels through the space while the shell itself subtly rotates. The viewer should feel a real 360 capture, not a flat collage zoom.
- Reserve the final 2.0 seconds for a stable, post-composited brand lockup.

### 1.25–4.00 — The city unfolds

The fragment opens into a miniature, architecturally believable Philadelphia street world. The camera passes through one fold and reveals Mac and Sean walking toward us. Their likenesses, clothing, and left/right placement are anchored to the approved founder media.

### 10.50–13.00 — Build momentum

The capture line becomes a true 360-degree orbit. Cyan points reconstruct a contemporary Momentum 360 studio facade from the Philadelphia shell. Gold appears only at route nodes and on the final orbit completion.

### 13.00–15.00 — Founder lockup

Mac and Sean settle in front of the branded studio and cross their arms in their existing approved rhythm. Hold their real faces clearly. Composite the exact Momentum 360 mark and the line **MAKE EVERY SPACE MOVE.** in post. Do not ask the video model to draw the logo or typography.

## 5. Film B — The Corner Store Scan

This is the direct structural cousin of the supplied grocery/convenience-store reference, but it tells a Momentum 360 service story.

### 0.00–2.00 — Real place, isolated

### 12.75–15.00 — Service lockup

## Motion rules

- One primary action per founder per shot.
- Mac owns invitation, framing, and the lens-reach beat.
- Sean owns forward authority, relationship, and the handshake beat.
- Maintain normal joint speed and real weight transfer; no skating, float-walking, or rubber hands.
- Keep hands below the face except for Mac's single lens-reach beat.
- Never morph one founder into the other. Do not add a third founder look-alike.

## Audio direction

Create an original sound bed with the functional energy of the supplied reference rather than lifting its music:

- 110–118 BPM pulse with a clean sub hit at the shell ignition.
- Granular reverse breath into the city reveal.
- Small scan ticks and camera-mechanism transients synchronized to meaningful capture nodes.
- One widening stereo orbit from 10.5 to 13.0 seconds.
- A short, confident tonal resolve under the final two-second logo hold.
- No generic trailer braam, corporate ukulele, EDM drop, or constant glitch noise.

## Acceptance gates

- Both founders match their canonical portraits at opening, midpoint, and end.
- The city/store exists inside the shell for the entire film; no full-frame generated background takeover.
- No generated logo or text appears in the raw model output.
- The final mark is the exact Momentum 360 asset and remains stable for at least 1.5 seconds.
- The particle orbit reads as 360-degree capture, not decoration.
- Hands, eyes, teeth, and facial age are reviewed on a frame contact sheet before upscaling.
- Only an approved identity/motion master advances to the 4K pass.
