1. Why these ten and not ten more city pages

August built ten brand new Halloween city guides. They worked: all ten are indexed, the property is at a 92 out of 100 average readiness score, and organic clicks are up 160 percent on the prior window. But the Search Console data says the next $750 should not go to ten more net new pages.

Three findings drive the whole plan:

  • The brand term is broken. bar crawl usa pulled 138 clicks at average position 16.0. A business ranking sixteenth for its own name is losing traffic it has already earned.
  • The biggest impression pool converts at almost nothing. bar crawl returned 5,998 impressions and 35 clicks at position 6.3. That is a 0.58 percent click through rate on the single largest demand pool on the property.
  • Proven formats have no home page. lawrenceville brunch crawl ranks position 1.1 with 50 clicks, and brunch crawl sits at 7.0, yet there is no national brunch crawl hub. The demand is already proved; the page to catch it does not exist.
The shape of September: four pages that fix and consolidate what already ranks, four that claim proven queries with no dedicated page, and two that must be built this month to rank for November and December inventory. Halloween sells in September and October, so the seasonal work cannot wait for October.

3. The ten pages

Tier one: fix and consolidate what already ranks PAGES 1 TO 4

1. Bar Crawl USA brand entity page
bar crawl usa  ·  138 clicks  ·  position 16.0

Rebuild the page Google serves for the company name so it answers who Bar Crawl USA is, which cities it runs in, and where to buy. Organization and WebSite structured data, a clear city index, and internal links from every guide. This is the single highest value fix on the property because the traffic is already being earned and then handed to someone else.

2. National "bar crawl" category hub
bar crawl  ·  5,998 impressions  ·  35 clicks  ·  position 6.3

The generic term is the largest demand pool on the site and the worst performing one. Build a genuine national hub: what a bar crawl is, how Bar Crawl USA events work, what a ticket includes, and a live city and date index. Title and meta rewritten for the 0.58 percent click through rate, which is a snippet problem as much as a ranking problem.

3. Halloween hub rewrite, Boos and Booze
halloween bar crawl 702 impressions position 7.8  ·  halloween bar crawl 2026 position 3.3

The hub currently holds October impressions and does not hand them off. Rewrite the title, description and intro so it routes searchers into the ten city guides built in August, and add the dated 2026 intent that already ranks at 3.3. This is the page that monetises the August work during the weeks tickets actually sell.

4. Events hub rebuild
Navigation defect flagged by Andy and by Mac

Andy's note that a page looked unfinished and Mac's note about navigation are the same problem: the hub does not connect to the inventory. Rebuild it as a dated, filterable index of every upcoming event with correct ticket links, proper heading structure, and an obvious mobile ticket call to action.

Tier two: claim proven queries with no page PAGES 5 TO 8

5. Brunch Crawl national hub
lawrenceville brunch crawl 50 clicks position 1.1  ·  brunch crawl position 7.0

A single local brunch crawl page ranks first and pulls 50 clicks. The category term ranks seventh with nothing behind it. Build the national brunch crawl hub the local page is currently carrying alone.

6. Atlanta bar crawls city hub
bar crawl atlanta position 7.1  ·  beltline bar crawl position 6.0

Atlanta is the home market and two separate Atlanta queries rank on page one with no city hub tying them together. One page covering Beltline, Midtown and the rest of the Atlanta calendar, linking down to individual events.

7. Taco and Tequila Crawl theme page
taco and tequila crawl  ·  position 10.8

Sitting just off page one on a theme that repeats across cities. A proper theme page with the multi city calendar should clear the page one threshold, and the format is reusable for future themed crawls without new page spend.

8. Soulard and St. Louis bar crawl page
soulard bar crawl  ·  18 clicks  ·  position 10.6

Eighteen clicks from position 10.6 means real demand landing on a weak result. A dedicated Soulard page with event dates, venues and ticket links is a short move to page one on a query already producing clicks.
