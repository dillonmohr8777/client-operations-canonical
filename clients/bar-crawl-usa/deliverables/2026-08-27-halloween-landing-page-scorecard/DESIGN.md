# Design System: Bar Crawl USA Halloween Landing Page Launch Scorecard

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The After-Hours Producer Dossier"**

The scorecard extends the established producer-dossier identity into a six-sheet launch ledger: bone call sheets, near-black operational ledgers, ultraviolet score and measurement fields, cobalt proof cues, coral status marks, and condensed event-production typography. It reads like a disciplined handoff assembled after venue close, with the executive answer first and the exact evidence immediately behind it.

The shipped sequence moves from readiness to method to proof: the 92-point opening, the weighted rubric, ten live page scores and URLs, the shipped page standard, ticket and measurement architecture, then the recorded and access-dependent next actions. Hard rules, full-field reversals, compact tables, and written status language replace generic SEO-audit cards and ornamental dashboard styling.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Six fixed US Letter portrait sheets with a preserved single-column mobile reading order
- Barlow Condensed at 500 and 800 for scores, hierarchy, evidence, labels, and folios
- A narrow Arial exception for explanatory body copy and source-bound interpretation only
- Bone, near-black, ultraviolet, cobalt, and surface-specific coral roles
- Square ledgers, one-pixel rules, tabular values, inset proof bands, and flat print surfaces

## Colors

Bone and near-black establish the dossier material; ultraviolet owns the strongest score and measurement fields; cobalt organizes proof; coral marks status and high-attention evidence.

### Primary

- **Deep Ultraviolet** (`#30207d`): Owns the cover score field, rubric lead cell, ticket architecture sheet, numeric paper emphasis, and other bounded high-authority evidence.
- **Operations Cobalt** (`#244fce`): Creates the blue inset bands on proof fields and the light-blue stage in the measurement flow.

### Secondary

- **Status Coral** (`#b83a2f`): Carries paper-surface status, the verified stamp, date line, and high-attention values on light fields.
- **Status Coral Light** (`#ff9e8f`): Carries scores, timing cues, and the closing statement on near-black or ultraviolet fields.

### Neutral

- **Near-Black Ledger** (`#111317`): Primary ink, the live-URL ledger, readouts, dark signal box, and closing operational ledger.
- **Bone Paper** (`#f3eee4`): Dominant printable call-sheet surface.
- **Paper White** (`#fffdf8`): Inset proof and signal fields plus text on dark surfaces.
- **Quiet Ink** (`#5c5a58`): Supporting paper copy and sources.
- **Evidence Wash** (`#dce4ff`): Supporting notes, dark-field source copy, and the middle measurement stage.
- **Preview Canvas** (`#d7d3cb`): Screen-only surround that separates the physical sheets.

**The Coral Contrast Rule.** Dark coral belongs on bone or white; light coral belongs on near-black or ultraviolet.

**The Bounded Field Rule.** Ultraviolet and cobalt create structural evidence fields. Coral identifies status and decisive values. None becomes a decorative gradient.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed with Arial fallback  
**Body Font:** Arial with Helvetica fallback, limited to explanatory prose  
**Label and Numeric Font:** Barlow Condensed with Arial fallback

**Character:** Barlow Condensed compresses the 92 monument, uppercase arguments, narrow score rows, and operational labels without losing authority. Arial is a deliberate, narrow print-legibility exception for explanatory paragraphs, list copy, and sources; it is not an alternate display, label, score, or evidence face.

### Hierarchy

- **Score Monument** (800, 132pt, 0.68 line height): The cropped opening score only.
- **Display** (800, 50pt with a 58pt emphasized line, 0.84 line height): The cover title and ultraviolet launch statement.
- **Headline** (800, 30pt, 0.9 line height): Each interior sheet opening.
- **Title** (800, usually 13.5pt to 22pt, 1 line height): Rubric categories, cities, proof fields, flow stages, signal fields, and ledger actions.
- **Body** (400, usually 8.3pt to 10.5pt, 1.42 line height): Source-bound explanation, evidence interpretation, list copy, and constraints.
- **Numeric Evidence** (800, 18pt to 40pt): Weighted values, city scores, production signals, and snapshot counts.
- **Label** (500 or 800, 7.2pt to 13pt, uppercase): Brand bars, metric labels, folios, dates, statuses, and timing cues.

**The Condensed Authority Rule.** Barlow Condensed owns hierarchy, scores, status, structured evidence, and operational metadata.

**The Narrow Arial Rule.** Arial may carry small explanatory body copy because the shipped print artifact uses it for dense legibility; never promote it into headings, scores, labels, folios, or future identity expansion.

**The Comparable Values Rule.** Scores and weighted values use tabular alignment and condensed numerals; on mobile, their reading order stays intact even when right alignment becomes left alignment.

## Layout

The artifact is fixed to six US Letter portrait sheets at 8.5 by 11 inches. Standard interior sheets use 0.5-inch horizontal padding, 0.46 inches above content, and 0.58 inches below. A 0.68-inch brand bar anchors the cover, while a ruled folio sits 0.23 inches above the bottom edge on every sheet.

The cover uses a 3.05-inch ultraviolet score field beside the launch title and three shipment counts. The five interior sheets use purpose-built structures: a five-column metric band and weighted rubric, a three-column URL score ledger, a two-by-two proof grid, a three-stage measurement flow with two supporting signals, and a three-column status ledger. Repeated vertical gaps cluster around 0.22 to 0.25 inches, with tighter 0.10 to 0.18-inch internal rhythm.

Below 850px, each sheet becomes a full-width, auto-height reading surface. Cover, title, proof, and signal grids stack; metric bands become two columns; rubric, score, and ledger rows become one column; flow stages stack with their connectors; readouts stack label over explanation; folios retain 22px side insets. Print removes the preview canvas spacing and shadow while preserving exact Letter geometry.

**The Six-Sheet Proof Rule.** The sequence remains: score, rubric, ten live URLs, shipped standard, ticket and measurement architecture, and recorded or access-dependent next actions.

**The Evidence Retention Rule.** Responsive adaptation may reorder columns into a reading stack but never hide a score, URL, proof item, status, source, or pending-access explanation.

## Elevation & Depth

Print surfaces are flat. Depth comes from full-field color changes, ruled separators, inset cobalt or coral top bands, cropped scale, and the contrast between paper and dark ledgers. The only ambient shadow is the screen-preview sheet shadow (`0 18px 46px rgba(17,19,23,.18)`), and print removes it.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Screen Sheet Lift** (`0 18px 46px rgba(17,19,23,.18)`): Separates consecutive Letter sheets on the preview canvas only.
- **Cobalt Proof Inset** (`inset 0 6px 0 #244fce`): Identifies the first and fourth shipped-proof fields.
- **Coral Proof Inset** (`inset 0 6px 0 #b83a2f`): Identifies the second and third shipped-proof fields.

**The Flat Print Rule.** If an ambient shadow survives print, it is outside the system.

## Shapes

The form language is square, ruled, and ledger-like. Score fields, metric bands, rubric rows, URL rows, proof fields, readouts, flow stages, signals, and status rows use zero radius. The verified Momentum 360 partner mark alone retains an 8px corner radius. The status stamp rotates negative three degrees, providing the only intentional irregularity.

**The One Irregular Mark Rule.** Rotation belongs to the verified status stamp only; every evidence field, row, rule, and folio remains exact.

## Components

### Score Field

A full-height ultraviolet cover column uses the oversized light-coral 92, an explicit “out of 100,” the readiness label, and a ruled verification block. It leads with the answer while written language prevents the internal score from reading as a search ranking.

### Brand Bar and Folio

The cover brand bar pairs the verified Bar Crawl USA logo with the rounded Momentum 360 partner mark and report name. Every sheet ends in an uppercase Barlow Condensed folio with a flexible one-pixel rule and exact sheet fraction.

### Status Stamp

A two-pixel dark-coral outline, uppercase condensed label, square corners, and negative-three-degree rotation mark current live verification on paper.

### Metric Band and Rubric Row

The five-cell ruled band opens with an ultraviolet lead cell and 24pt condensed values. Weighted rubric rows align category, points, and evidence in three columns, using ultraviolet numeric emphasis on paper.

### URL Score Row

The near-black ledger aligns city, light-coral score, and an unshortened live URL in three columns. Links remain written and wrap anywhere when needed; mobile stacks all three values rather than truncating the evidence.

### Proof Field

White square fields use a six-pixel cobalt or coral inset top band, 20pt titles, compact body copy, and lists. They group search, local-usefulness, conversion, and QA evidence without introducing generic rounded cards.

### Readout

A near-black two-column strip pairs a light-coral condensed conclusion with explanatory body copy separated by a quiet rule. Mobile stacks the conclusion over the explanation and moves the divider to the top.

### Measurement Flow and Signal Field

Three square stages move from landing page through checkout initiation to order reconciliation. White, evidence-wash, and coral-light fields communicate progression; adjacent white and near-black signals distinguish one verified production modal from implementation across ten pages.

### Operational Ledger Row

The closing near-black ledger aligns a coral-light state, named action and explanation, and right-aligned outcome. Recorded, submitted, pending, and next are always written, so color never carries status alone.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve the six-sheet score-to-action proof sequence and every exact score, URL, status, and evidence statement.
- **Do** use Barlow Condensed at 500 and 800 for hierarchy and evidence, with the narrow Arial exception reserved for explanatory prose.
- **Do** preserve bone, near-black, ultraviolet, cobalt, and surface-specific coral roles.
- **Do** let full fields, hard rules, inset proof bands, cropped scores, and tabular alignment create hierarchy.
- **Do** keep written status labels and preserve the complete reading order below 850px.

### Don't:

- **Don't** turn the scorecard into a generic SEO dashboard, rounded card stack, or pill system.
- **Don't** use decorative gradients, ambient print shadows, soft floating layers, or ornamental motion.
- **Don't** use dark coral on dark fields or light coral as paper-surface body copy.
- **Don't** replace Barlow Condensed with Arial in headings, scores, labels, folios, or evidence typography.
- **Don't** collapse evidence into summaries that hide exact scores, live URLs, sources, or access dependencies.
