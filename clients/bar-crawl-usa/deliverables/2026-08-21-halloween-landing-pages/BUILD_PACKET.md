## Shared improved structure

Use the incumbent Elementor page-builder system. Do not add a custom theme file, standalone coded landing page, or hard-coded template.

1. Campaign hero
   - One clear H1 using market, “Halloween bar crawl,” and 2026.
   - Date, time, and neighborhood in a compact fact line.
   - One primary action: “View event details and tickets.”
   - Full-width verified attendee photo with a dark navy image veil for text contrast.
2. Fast decision strip
   - Date and operating hours.
   - Route area or neighborhood.
   - Number of participating venues only when the live source supports it.
   - Costume contest and official after-party signal when supported.
3. Event-specific answer
   - A concise two-paragraph local explanation, not repeated national copy.
   - For Atlanta and Cleveland, an edition selector leads to two equally complete sections.
4. What the ticket includes
   - Use only the inclusions published on that event’s live source.
   - Avoid carrying a discount, shot count, drink price, or venue count from another market.
5. Route and arrival plan
   - Registration location and window first.
   - Participating venues in chronological order when the source provides times.
   - A short “plan your night” note covering arrival, rideshare, parking uncertainty, and checking the official listing for updates.
6. Social proof gallery
   - Two or three verified media-library images, not one generic image repeated through every section.
   - Use the event source’s featured image plus the shared campaign crowd and partygoer images.
7. Costume and group section
   - Costume contest details supported by the source.
   - Group discount only where the source expressly states it.
8. Useful FAQs
   - Date and time.
   - Registration and route area.
   - What the ticket includes.
   - Costume expectations.
   - Where official updates and tickets live.
9. Related discovery
   - Link the relevant city hub.
   - Link the Boos & Booze theme hub.
   - Link a second local event only when it is a distinct current choice.
10. Final decision stage
   - Re-state market, date, and edition.
   - One official event/ticket action.
