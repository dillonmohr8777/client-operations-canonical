Your St. Pete night works better with one simple plan.

Start with the official event listing, confirm the registration point, and save the route before your group leaves home. Choose two places everybody wants to see, one place to reunite if the group splits, and the official after party when one is published. That leaves enough structure to keep the night moving without turning a bar crawl into a group project.

The published window is 4 PM to 10 PM in Downtown St. Petersburg. Arrive early enough to check in without rushing, eat before the route gets loud, and keep the official ticket page handy for any same day change.

Sunscreen can clock out. Your costume cannot.

St. Pete heat and late October humidity can still punish a heavy costume. Choose breathable layers, secure props, and comfortable shoes. Set one Central Avenue meetup point and one after party meetup so the night can flex without losing people.

Make the smart plan before the first round.

Bring a valid ID, keep the ticket confirmation easy to reach, and arrange a sober ride home before check in. Parking and transit information can change.
