Universal build standardWhat every page must include
One definition of doneAcross all eight pages

Build once. Apply consistently. Measure separately.

The city story changes. The quality bar, source discipline, and measurement path do not.

Search foundation

  • One verified canonical URL, title, description, and H1
  • Breadcrumbs, page hierarchy, sitemap inclusion, and indexing request
  • Valid structured data tied to facts visible on the page
  • No duplicate city or neighborhood targets

Answer quality

  • Direct summary near the top for human and AI retrieval
  • Local context a generic competitor cannot reuse
  • Useful FAQs based on real visitor questions
  • Event facts sourced from approved live records

Proof and experience

  • Approved real event photos or video with reuse rights
  • Clear expectations, inclusions, route-area cues, and organizer credibility
  • Accessible image text and readable mobile composition
  • No placeholder venue, date, price, or sold-out claims

Conversion and measurement

  • Current event modules with working ticket destinations
  • Internal links from hub to event and relevant guides
  • UTM governance for paid paths and separate ticket-click events
  • Search Console and GA4 baseline captured after indexing
Done means live and evidenced.

A page is complete only when the canonical target, copy, schema, media rights, event links, mobile layout, accessibility, analytics, and indexing request have all passed review. A draft in WordPress is progress, not completion.
