# Surface: Seasonal Performance Report

## Mode

Read

## Job

Give Andy Zirger an executive view of current traffic quality, prior seasonal scale, the strongest landing pages, organic-search strength, ranking opportunities, and the next seasonal priorities.

## Proof sequence

1. Establish the current traffic and organic-search snapshot.
2. Compare channel quality in the current GA4 window.
3. Show the scale and channel mix of the prior October-to-December season.
4. Identify the strongest seasonal landing pages and current pre-season demand.
5. Establish the Semrush domain and keyword-intent baseline.
6. Separate Halloween ranking strength from the Ugly Sweater architecture gap.
7. Close with a short, ordered action plan for August through December.

## Chosen direction

Extend the After-Hours Producer Dossier into a portrait seasonal performance book. Bone call sheets, ultraviolet data fields, near-black ledgers, coral decision marks, cropped numbers, and Barlow Condensed make the report feel built for an event operator rather than exported from an analytics tool.

## Constraints

- Prepared specifically for Andy Zirger.
- GA4 and Semrush windows remain explicit.
- First-party traffic and modeled organic-search estimates stay separate.
- Client-facing pages exclude internal operational caveats and restricted measurement language.
- Every published number remains exact to the supplied source PDF.
