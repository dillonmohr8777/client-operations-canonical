Use a premium after-hours event producer dossier built as an eight-page portrait operations call sheet. ## Constraints

- The production count is exactly 10 pages.
- Atlanta and Cleveland each remain one page with two clearly separated event editions.
- Birmingham remains outside the active count until its source year is corrected and reread.
- Status must be written and may not rely on color alone.
- Proposed page paths remain visually distinct from verified event-source records.
- The print format is US Letter portrait across eight pages.
