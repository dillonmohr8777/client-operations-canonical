# Surface: Worked Page Analytics

## Mode

Read

## Job

Show exactly 24 confirmed June and July worked-page rows with their current GA4 and Search Console values, explicit source windows, July paid-media context, and bounded next priorities.

## Proof sequence

1. Establish the 24-row worked-page scope and the three exact source windows.
2. Show all 10 June rows in one ledger.
3. Show all 12 July Halloween event rows across two ledger sheets.
4. Keep the two July new-listing rows separate from the Halloween optimization set.
5. Convert the exact evidence into bounded priorities without claiming unverified sales or paid-media impact.

## Chosen direction

Use a premium after-hours operations ledger built as a five-page landscape call sheet. Bone source panels meet a broad ultraviolet scope field; near-black ledger bands, cobalt cell emphasis, light coral values on dark fields, condensed type, and tabular alignment make every row fast to compare.

## Constraints

- The ledger contains exactly 24 worked-page rows.
- GA4 and Search Console windows differ by one day and remain labeled separately.
- GA4 landing-page metrics include all acquisition channels.
- Search Console is the organic-search source.
- Active-user and new-user values are per-page row sums, not cross-page unique people.
- Key events are configured GA4 events, not verified purchase outcomes.
- Pages planned only for August or October remain outside the historical worked-page count.
- The print format is US Letter landscape across five pages.
