Sep 21Verified work / decision

Traffic more than doubled and checkout starts fell

Sessions went from 2,914 to 6,179 week over week, almost all of it paid social, which rose from 1,301 to 3,780 sessions. Over the same two weeks the number of people who started a checkout fell from 10 to 2, and key events recorded from paid social fell from 48 to 15. Organic search moved the other way, from 45 to 71 key events on roughly flat traffic.

More than twice the traffic producing fewer checkout starts is the clearest signal available on where the value actually is. Organic is carrying this site and the paid social surge is not converting.

Analytics property read directly, Sep 21
Sep 21Verified work / decision

An event named as a purchase is firing on page load

An event named as an advertising purchase conversion fired 5,525 times this period and 2,637 last period. Its name says it fires on page load, and the numbers match page views rather than purchases. Genuine form submissions over the same period were 32, and form starts were 99.

Any purchase or conversion count taken from the advertising platform for this site is inflated by a large factor. It is the same class of defect found on another account this month and it needs the same correction.
