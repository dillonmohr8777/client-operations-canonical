Organic clicks are up 160 percent.

Earned. Not bought.

From August 10 to September 7 the site earned 2,356 clicks on 68,203 impressions. The 29 days before that, July 12 to August 9, did 905 clicks. That is the August SEO and AEO program showing up in search: city guides indexed, existing event pages carrying volume, and paid search dark the whole time. The older October hub still holds 10,040 impressions with 8 clicks, so the next rewrite is sitting on visibility already won.

Google Ads is connected. Last delivery was June, so none of the 2,356 clicks in this window were paid. August as a month did 2,300 clicks against 749 in July.

What moved forward

Specific actions, deliverables and decisions, with their evidence status.

Sep 9Verified work / decision

Search Console access granted and verified

The property sc-domain:barcrawlusa.com now reports Full User for the Momentum account. The permission was confirmed by querying the account directly rather than by reading the grant screenshot.

Page level search reporting is now possible for this site instead of site level estimates.

Search Console API verification, Sep 9
Sep 9Verified work / decision

All ten 2026 city guides confirmed indexed

URL inspection returned Submitted and indexed with a PASS verdict for every one of the ten 2026 city guides, each last crawled on August 28. The sitemaps report 225 web and 626 image URLs submitted through the index file and 51 through the feed, with zero errors and two non blocking warnings.

Indexing is now ruled out as an explanation for early traffic levels. Nothing needs resubmitting.

Search Console URL inspection, Sep 9
Sep 9Verified work / decision

Search performance reviewed page by page

Across August 10 to September 7 the site recorded 2,356 clicks on 68,203 impressions, read again on September 10 as later days finalized. The ten new guides account for 27 clicks on 732 impressions with average positions between 3.2 and 9.5, which is a normal start for pages first crawled on August 28. The existing event pages carried the volume with 407 clicks on 8,203 impressions. The older October hub page collected 10,040 impressions at an average position of 9.09 and returned 8 clicks.

The hub page is the largest single opportunity on the site: the visibility already exists and the listing is not earning the click.

Search Console page dimension, Sep 9
Sep 9Verified work / decision

Measurement tags and structured data audited on all ten guides

Google Tag Manager container GTM-WJW4DR99 and GA4 property G-2LNEWBT4XW are present on all ten guides. Structured data covers BreadcrumbList, Organization, WebPage, WebSite and FAQPage. Event structured data is not present on any of them.

The tracking foundation is sound. The missing Event markup is the specific gap between these pages and richer search results.

Rendered page audit, Sep 9
Sep 9Verified work / decision

Legacy city pages still ranking with no 2026 event

Pages for Gainesville GA, Asheville, Fort Worth and Knoxville drew 1,565 impressions and 20 clicks in the reviewed window. None of them has a live 2026 event behind it.

That traffic is either an unlisted opportunity or a redirect candidate. It needs a decision either way.

Sep 10Verified work / decision

Google Ads reporting connected

The advertising account now reads directly through our reporting connection, campaign by campaign. The 2026 record to date is 395,379 impressions and 10,562 clicks against $6,936.73 of spend across February to June. Paid search has been dark since June, which means every one of the 2,356 search clicks in this reporting window was earned rather than bought.

The account is built, warm and reportable. October can be measured end to end the moment it switches back on.
