# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Adults evaluating a city-specific Bar Crawl USA event from organic search, social, or a campaign link. Their immediate job is to understand which event edition applies to them, when and where it happens, what the ticket includes, how the route works, and how to reach the official ticket path.

## Product Purpose

Bar Crawl USA publishes local, themed group nightlife events and gives prospective attendees one official place to evaluate the event, review current operational details, and continue to tickets. For the August 2026 build, success is ten accurate, useful, indexable Halloween landing pages that support the confirmed October markets.

## Positioning

The pages connect national event credibility to detailed local routes, participating venues, schedules, ticket inclusions, and neighborhood-specific guidance. The local operational detail is the differentiator; it must remain tied to the current official event record.

## Operating Context

The production site is WordPress with Elementor-based campaign/event layouts and AIOSEO. Current `event_listing` records are the factual source for dates, times, inclusions, route areas, participating bars, and ticket actions. Pages must be built through the WordPress page-builder workflow rather than a hard-coded external implementation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Build exactly ten October 2026 Halloween landing pages: Atlanta, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Columbia, Greenville, Portland, St. Pete, Macon, Roswell, and Sarasota.
- Atlanta combines the BeltLine and Midtown editions in one market page; Cleveland combines West Park and Lakewood in one market page.
- Do not invent venues, times, inclusions, ticket destinations, or Midtown event facts that are not present in an approved source.
- Use the site's existing media library and verified Bar Crawl USA imagery.
- Preserve separate event facts and ticket paths for each edition inside combined-market pages.
- Pages require unique titles, descriptions, H1s, useful local copy, FAQs, internal links, meaningful image alternative text, supported schema, and mobile QA.
- Current public evidence does not expose a 2026 Midtown Halloween event record. This remains an explicit source check inside authenticated WordPress rather than a fact to infer.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Bar Crawl USA name, existing logo, energetic social-event voice, Halloween campaign identity, and recognizable navy-and-white site framework.
- Keep the experience lively and specific without generic assistant language, fabricated urgency, or copied city text.
- Use “St. Pete” and “St. Petersburg” naturally, and include state names where they resolve ambiguity.

## Product Principles

- Source integrity before promotional flourish.
- Local specificity on every page.
- One obvious path from event choice to official tickets.
- Equal clarity for each edition on combined-market pages.
- Build early enough for indexing and refinement before October.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic heading order, meaningful alternative text, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive links, reduced-motion-safe behavior, and layouts that remain usable on narrow mobile screens.
