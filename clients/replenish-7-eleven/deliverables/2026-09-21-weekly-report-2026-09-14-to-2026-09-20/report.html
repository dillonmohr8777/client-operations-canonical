Finding out why nothing is running, and ruling out the explanation everyone had settled on.

Read your report

The campaigns are not running, and the reason is probably not the one everybody assumed.

Checked, not assumed.

All nine campaigns recorded no impressions and no spend across the period examined. The working assumption had been that this traced back to the billing issue from July. A direct look at the account on September 14 does not support that. The more likely explanation is expired campaign flight dates, which stop delivery silently and look identical to a billing problem from the outside. No account changes were logged during the period, so nothing we did caused the stop. This is a diagnosis rather than a repair, and it is worth saying that nothing was fixed this week.

This is a work and delivery update. Current platform metrics are not established for this report, so they are not estimated.

This account shares a billing entity with another brand. Every figure in this report covers the named Replenish campaigns only and no account level total is taken.
