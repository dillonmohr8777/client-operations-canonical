# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Replenish leadership and client stakeholders reviewing weekly paid-media performance, plus Dillon Mohr as the account manager presenting and distributing the report.

## Product Purpose

Provide a branded, verifiable weekly view of Replenish advertising delivery and traffic performance, with downloadable presentation files and neutral measurement language.

## Operating Context

The report is produced from authenticated advertising-platform readbacks, delivered as PowerPoint and PDF, and published to the existing Replenish Netlify site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep Google Ads and Meta Ads evidence separate.
- Use only the exact reporting period and verified KPI math.
- Do not attribute Meta performance without a mapped Replenish account.
- Do not include modeled directions metrics.
- Treat conversion reporting as pending validation until definitions, attribution, latency, tracking, and downstream outcomes are verified.

## Brand Commitments

Use the exact 7-Eleven logo and the white, green, orange, and red presentation identity from Dillon's sent Replenish PowerPoint. Replenish does not use the generic blue advertising dashboard.

## Evidence on Hand

- Exact emailed Replenish PowerPoint template and embedded 7-Eleven logo.
- Google Ads account 627-501-4654 readback for July 20 through July 26, 2026.
- Canonical KPI ledger in the weekly presentation deliverable folder.

## Product Principles

- Evidence before claims.
- Client identity before agency template.
- Clear weekly decisions before exhaustive platform detail.
- Downloadable artifacts and live presentation must agree.
