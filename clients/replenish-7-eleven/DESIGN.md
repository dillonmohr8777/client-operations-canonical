# Replenish × 7-Eleven Reporting Design

## Visual World

The live report is a digital counterpart to the exact emailed 7-Eleven KPI deck: crisp white presentation stock, a strong 7-Eleven green field, orange and red signal accents, dark navy copy, and the authentic 7-Eleven mark as the primary identity.

## Color Roles

- `#008062` 7-Eleven green: headers, primary controls, metric emphasis.
- `#F4811F` orange: short signal rules and active accents.
- `#ED2525` red: secondary signal rules and small brand moments.
- `#FFFFFF` white: primary report surface.
- `#F3F7F6` pale green: quiet data bands.
- `#172033` dark navy: headings and body copy.
- `#667085` slate: supporting copy.

## Typography

Use Segoe UI and system UI workhorses to match the PowerPoint. Headlines are compact, bold, and sentence case. Data values use strong tabular figures. Small labels may be uppercase but never become the page's dominant texture.

## Composition

The first viewport reads like a client report cover merged with a live scorecard: the exact logo, reporting period, decisive weekly thesis, five verified KPIs, and download actions. The campaign table and PDF preview follow as one continuous report, not a generic grid of cards.

## Components

- White report sheet on a pale neutral canvas.
- Green top rail and three-color orange, red, green signal rule.
- Square or softly rounded panels with 12–16px radii.
- Green primary button and white outlined secondary button.
- Subtle gray borders and offset soft shadows; no blue theme, glass, glow, or decorative gradients.

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop preserves the wide presentation rhythm. Mobile stacks the scorecard, turns the campaign table into labeled rows, and keeps the download actions full-width and keyboard accessible.

## Motion

One short page-reveal sequence may move the signal rule and KPI band into place. All content is visible by default and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes transitions.
