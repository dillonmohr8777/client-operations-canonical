# Omega Google Ads course adapter

Status: active local operating guidance
Client: `omega-landscaping`
Shared source: `../../../docs/GOOGLE_ADS_BOOTCAMP_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`

## Business outcome

Apply the course to generate qualified landscaping and concrete opportunities in Colorado Springs and the approved service area. Optimize toward one verified primary revenue or qualified-lead outcome, not a blended count of forms, calls, estimate starts, and platform actions.

## Campaign structure

- Keep landscaping and concrete service intent visible in campaign, ad-group, search-term, landing-page, and reporting structure.
- Use high-intent local Search to capture direct service demand after the primary outcome and destination are verified.
- Treat Performance Max as an input-driven demand engine that must be judged by qualified opportunity quality, not platform conversion count alone.
- Keep any historically paused Search campaigns unchanged unless current live evidence and authority cover a restart or rebuild.

## Measurement contract

- Proposed primary outcome: `qualified_lead`.
- Secondary diagnostics: `form_submit`, `phone_call`, and `estimate_start`.
- Required campaign-source convention: `omega_google_{service}_{market}_{campaign_type}`.
- Reconcile Google Ads, calls, forms, GoHighLevel or the current CRM, and qualified-opportunity disposition in the same reporting window.
- Deduplicate repeat forms, calls, imports, and CRM records. Exclude tests, suppliers, wrong-company inquiries, spam, and unqualified contacts from the primary outcome.

## Course adaptations

- Business intake must confirm the exact service, service radius, seasonality, job value, margin, capacity, lead destination, and landing page before keyword or budget planning.
- Unit economics should use qualified opportunity and booked-work value when available.
- Search-term review should turn verified supplier, wrong-company, DIY, employment, and irrelevant-service demand into carefully scoped negatives.
- Match types, PMax signals, budgets, and bidding strategies are testable controls, not fixed defaults. Use current data quality and volume.
- Do not scale from traffic efficiency, CPC, form count, call count, or platform conversions until lead quality and attribution are reconciled.

## Approval boundary

Local research, audits, drafts, QA plans, and paused proposals are allowed. Live account, site, tag, CRM, budget, bidding, campaign-state, or conversion changes remain governed by `launch-authority.json` and current explicit approval.
