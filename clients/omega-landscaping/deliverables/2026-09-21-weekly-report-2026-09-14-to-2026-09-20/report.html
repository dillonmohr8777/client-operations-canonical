A correction week. The measurement was wrong, it is now right, and the numbers will read differently because of it.

Read your report

The conversion count was being inflated by bots. That is fixed, and the number will now go down.

Measured, not assumed.

The most important thing that happened this week is a correction rather than a gain. The advertising landing page was recording a conversion at the moment the submit button was pressed, before the page had accepted the submission and before the spam filter had run. Filtered bot submissions were therefore being reported to Google as conversions. That was corrected and verified live on September 14. Expect reported conversion volume to fall from here. That fall is the correction working, not performance declining. Alongside it, the search terms audit continued through the next hundred terms, and the nine competitor and supplier negatives written on September 9 are still waiting on your approval.

This is a work and delivery update. Current platform metrics are not established for this report, so they are not estimated.

Verified changes, dated reads and open decisions are separated below. Where a figure is a platform recorded event rather than a confirmed person, it says so.

What moved forward

Specific actions, deliverables and decisions, with their evidence status.

Sep 14Verified work / decision

Conversion counting defect corrected and verified on the live page

The landing page fired its conversion on button press rather than on accepted submission, so submissions the honeypot later filtered as automated were still reported as conversions. The firing point was moved behind acceptance and the spam check, then confirmed on the live public page.

Every conversion figure reported for this account before this date was inflated by an unknown amount. Correcting the count is worth more than any optimisation made on top of a wrong number.

Verified on the live landing page after deployment, Sep 14
Sep 14Verified work / decision

Reporting constraint written into your account record

The instruction not to report the earlier conversion figure as it stands was moved out of a working note and into the permanent account record, so it survives regardless of who prepares the next report.

The inflated figure had already been quoted once. Putting the constraint where reporting actually reads from is what stops it being quoted again.

Search terms audit continued through ranks 101 to 200

The audit covering August 17 to September 15 was worked through its next hundred terms. All one hundred recorded no clicks and no cost against 210 impressions. These terms are not costing money today, but they show what the campaign is still eligible to match against. That is the list the negatives are built from.
