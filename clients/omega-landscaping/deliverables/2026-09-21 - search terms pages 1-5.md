## Classification caveat

Competitor and supplier identity is read from name pattern, not from a verified
local-business registry. `araco`, `preferred concrete`, `mosaic outdoor living`,
`pikes peak`, `cutting edge`, `all do landscape`, `backyard paradiso`, `earthx`,
`monarch`, `vistacraft`, `crazy rooster`, `the sod guy`, `neighbor bright`,
`genesis group`, `jtb`, `flat and fancy` and the rest are read as real local
businesses. `srm concrete`, `transit mix`, `pavestone` and `precast concrete` are read
as materials suppliers. **Confirm each before any negative is applied.**

Two conversions is not a statistical sample. Conversion lag may still add late
conversions inside this window.
