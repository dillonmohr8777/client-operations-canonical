# Omega Landscaping & Concrete Ad Landing Page

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: dependency-light static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript published to the exact existing Netlify landing-page site recorded in `RELEASE.md`.

## Users

Colorado Springs area property owners arriving from a service-specific paid search ad who need to determine whether Omega handles their landscaping or concrete project and request an estimate.

## Product Purpose

Turn qualified local search intent into estimate conversations while keeping landscaping and concrete services separately inspectable. Success means a visitor can identify service fit, see real local project work, understand the next step, and start an estimate request or phone call.

## Positioning

Omega presents outdoor living and concrete as one coordinated design-build scope informed by grading, drainage, construction, and Colorado site conditions.

## Operating Context

The page is a focused Google Ads destination for Colorado Springs, Monument, Falcon, Peyton, Black Forest, and northern Colorado Springs. Campaign parameters and Google click identifiers must remain intact through the conversion path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Separate landscaping and concrete intent.
- Provide phone and estimate-request paths.
- Preserve `utm_*`, `gclid`, `wbraid`, and `gbraid` values in form fields and outbound links.
- Primary measurement goal: `qualified_lead`.
- Secondary interaction names: `estimate_start`, `form_submit`, and `phone_call`.
- Do not claim tracking is live until the deployed destination is validated.
- Do not invent prices, project timing, ratings, testimonials, warranties, awards, or outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

Use the verified Omega mountain seal and the existing cream, royal blue, red, and black identity. The voice is direct, capable, local, and specific to real exterior work without hype.

## Product Principles

- Make service fit obvious before asking for contact information.
- Earn trust with verified work and exact service-area language.
- Preserve a short, friction-aware path to phone or estimate request.
- Treat measurement readiness as an implementation detail, never a public claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must remain understandable without motion or JavaScript, support keyboard navigation, expose clear focus states, use descriptive image alternatives, and keep mobile actions reachable without obscuring content.
