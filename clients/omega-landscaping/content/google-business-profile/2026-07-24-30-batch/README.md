## Post 4: Colorado landscape design

**Image:** `omega-gbp-2026-07-30-colorado-landscape-design.png`

Put every landscape material where it works best.

A balanced Colorado yard can combine lawn, rock, plants, and paths without feeling pieced together. The key is giving every zone a clear purpose.

Use lawn where it adds comfortable green space. Choose rock and hardscape where durability and lower maintenance matter. Add plants that fit the light, exposure, and watering plan. Then repeat materials so the whole yard feels connected.
