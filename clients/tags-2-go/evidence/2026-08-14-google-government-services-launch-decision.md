## Policy conflict

Google's published rule says an authorized provider's domain must be linked from an official government website and explicitly identified there as authorized for the specific service. PennDOT's official directory establishes the current authorization and identity but cannot supply that hyperlink because PennDOT does not publish agent websites.

The submitted packet is the strongest truthful alternative evidence available. It supports a manual exception, but it does not satisfy Google's literal domain-link requirement. Approval remains uncertain until Google issues a decision.
