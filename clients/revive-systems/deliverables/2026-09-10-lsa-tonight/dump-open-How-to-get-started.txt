Required background checks
Google will check the standing of your business and its owner.
Who should participate
Anyone who owns or manages the business location you’ll be advertising should participate.
How to get started
You’ll need to enter a few business details on the Evident website and add contact information for each business owner. They will receive an email from Evident asking them to upload a copy of their driver’s license or ID.

When you’re done, return to this dashboard to complete the other business checks. It may take about a week for Google to get your background check results. To help move the process along, remind the others at your business to get started.

If you need any help with this step, contact Evident support.
Local Services Ads requires background checks to help your customers rest easy. Google partners with Evident, a company that securely runs these checks, so that your information is kept private.
This means that specific details aren’t shared with anyone, not even Google. We’re only informed of the final results to know if you passed or failed the check.
