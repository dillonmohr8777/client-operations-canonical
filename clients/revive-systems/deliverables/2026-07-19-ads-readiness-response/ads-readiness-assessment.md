## Required readiness gates

1. Choose one entry offer and one destination for paid traffic.
2. Define the primary conversion event and the qualified outcome it represents.
3. Verify the form or call path, notifications, ownership, deduplication, and analytics from click through disposition.
4. Confirm audience, geography, exclusions, offer, approved claims, and creative inputs.
5. Establish an approved test budget, stop conditions, decision window, and success threshold.
6. Add Revive to the exact paid-media roster only through an authorized portfolio decision, then map the exact platform account and client blueprint before any platform planning.
7. Complete current-page mobile, hierarchy, message-match, and lead-path QA before buying traffic.

## Recommendation

Finish the conversion path and measurement gates first. Then make a separate go or no-go decision for a small controlled test only after Revive is explicitly eligible for the selected platform and the exact account, budget, scope, and authority are recorded.
