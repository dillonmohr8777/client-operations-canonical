# Revive Systems marketing context

*Last updated: 2026-09-20. V1 internal draft for review; verified claims are
limited to `CLIENT.md`, `context/operating-context.md`, the 2026-07-16 organic
package, and the 2026-09-14 weekly-report draft.*

## Product overview

**One-liner:** Revive Systems provides health, nutrition, strength, and
accountability coaching designed for sustainable progress in real life.

**Category:** Health and weight-loss coaching; personal training and nutrition
support. **Type:** Service business. **Business model/pricing:** Not verified;
do not publish prices or offer terms without approval.

**Current offer path:** Organic discovery -> 3 in 30 entry offer -> application
-> qualified conversation -> VIP coaching when fit is confirmed. The 7-Day
asset, Inner Circle, Revive Reset, webinar, and community are supporting paths,
not equal primary calls to action.

## Target audience and jobs to be done

- Busy adults seeking sustainable weight loss, strength, energy, nutrition,
  recovery, and consistency rather than another short-term plan.
- Local prospects in Chambersburg and Franklin County, Pennsylvania.
- Adults 40+ are a supported content angle, but the full age range is not yet
  verified.
- Jobs: choose the right starting program; rebuild a routine during stressful
  weeks; understand coaching options; progress from an entry program to the
  appropriate level of support.

## Problems and switching dynamics

- **Push:** Repeated short-term plans, inconsistent routines, competing health
  advice, and unclear program choices.
- **Pull:** A structured starting point, personalized support, real-life fit,
  local credibility, and a clear next step.
- **Habit/anxiety:** Existing routines are familiar; prospects may worry about
  fit, pressure to buy, health claims, or what happens after applying.
- **Messaging requirement:** Explain who each offer is for, what happens next,
  and how the offers differ without guarantees or medical-treatment claims.

## Personas

| Persona | Role | Cares about | Value promise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Busy local adult | User/buyer | Sustainable progress that fits work and family | A structured, realistic starting path |
| Returning or past client | Warm prospect/referrer | A relevant reason to re-engage | A simple conversation and appropriate next step |
| Mike / Revive leadership | Decision maker | Brand credibility, qualified conversations, safe growth | Organic-first content tied to one conversion path |

## Differentiation and proof boundaries

- Coaching combines health, nutrition, strength, recovery, and accountability.
- The recommended funnel uses one entry path and escalates support when fit is
  established instead of presenting every offer equally.
- Public/local proof, Mike's story, reviews, media credibility, and verified
  success stories may support content only when the exact evidence is sourced.
- No unsupported superlatives, guaranteed weight loss, medical claims, invented
  testimonials, prices, statistics, or causal promises.

## Customer language and brand voice

**Words/themes to use:** built for real life; sustainable progress; structured
coaching; strength; nutrition; recovery; accountability; appropriate next
step; Chambersburg; Franklin County.

**Words/themes to avoid:** cure; treat; guaranteed; rapid transformation;
one-size-fits-all; automatic enrollment; unsupported "best" claims.

**Voice:** Direct, encouraging, practical, credible, and non-judgmental. Lead
with a useful answer, keep calls to action clear, and avoid hype or shame.

## Goals and conversion

**Primary goal:** Build an active organic presence that creates qualified
conversations and reconnects Revive with past clients/gym members.

**Primary conversion:** Start with or apply for 3 in 30, then move to a
qualified conversation. **Secondary conversions:** Engage with a useful post,
choose a free nurture resource, review/refer, or schedule the discovery call
requested in Slack.
