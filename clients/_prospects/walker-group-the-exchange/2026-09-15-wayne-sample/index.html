Section one

What Jesse put in front of you, in plain language

Jesse gave you two starting points and a recommendation. Both are real options. Here is what each one actually means once the shorthand comes off.

Option A  ·  Jesse's recommendation

Organic, meaning Google and AI search

Today a bank executive or a fintech founder who does not already know you cannot find The Exchange. They find listicles, LinkedIn profiles and university speaker pages. Organic work is the process of making The Exchange the answer to the questions those people are already typing, in Google and in the AI assistants that now sit on top of Google.

The output is a membership pipeline that runs when you are not in the room. Right now every member arrives through you or through Jonathan. That is a ceiling, and it is the ceiling standing between you and the doubling target you set for 2026.

Option B

Platform build

A working member platform for The Exchange. Cohorts, member profiles, event registration, the directory, the introductions you currently make by hand. Fifty percent deposit, scope agreed first, then a functional beta you can put in front of members.

Jesse's sequencing note matters here and he is right about it. Sending new demand to a site that cannot convert it is waste. The question is only whether your current site is good enough to receive traffic while the platform gets built. Section two answers that.

Those two are not either or forever. They are a question of which one comes first. Everything below exists so that you can answer that question with evidence rather than with a hunch.

Section two  ·  the sample

Three checks we ran on your properties today

This is the shape of the work. We look, we record exactly what came back, and we tell you what it costs you. Nothing here is an estimate.

01

Your apex domain does not resolve

Breaks silently

Anyone who types your address without the www, or repeats it from a business card, a podcast or a LinkedIn bio, gets nothing. No error page, no redirect, no site. The www version is healthy. The bare version simply fails to connect.

GET https://www.theexchangenyc.com 200 OK 2,748 bytes GET https://theexchangenyc.com no response connection failed

This is a single DNS record. It is the cheapest fix on this page and it is quietly costing you every referral that is spoken rather than clicked.

02

The Walker Group site is a 2019 redirect to a dead address

Dead end

walkergroupnyc.com answers, so every automated checker calls it healthy. It is not. The entire page is ninety five bytes of instruction telling the browser to go somewhere else, and the somewhere else refuses the connection. The file has not been touched since March 2019.

GET https://walkergroupnyc.com 200 OK 95 bytes Last-Modified: Fri, 29 Mar 2019 Body: meta refresh to http://waynebrown.nyc GET http://waynebrown.nyc 403 Forbidden

Your email address is on that domain. It sits in every proposal, every introduction and every signature you have sent for seven years. When a prospective member or a bank partner checks it, that is what they find.

03

Your positioning is good. Search engines are not being told where to look.

Foundation is sound

Credit where it is due. Your title and description are clear, specific and correctly aimed at senior leaders. That is more than most sites in your category manage, and it means this is a distribution problem rather than a message problem.

Title: The Exchange | Private Advisory Network for Senior Leaders Description: A private advisory network where senior leaders build trust, insight and long-term value... robots.txt: crawling allowed sitemap.xml: not found

No sitemap means Google is left to guess at your structure, and the page itself is small enough that there is very little for an AI assistant to quote. The words are right. There is just not enough of them, in enough places, for anything to cite.

Section four

What each path produces

If organic goes first

Discoverability

  • Apex domain and Walker Group redirect fixed in week one
  • Pages built for the questions your members actually ask, one per cohort and per program
  • Structured markup so assistants can quote The Exchange by name rather than paraphrase a listicle
  • Your existing material, the newsletter, the Virtual Lunch archive and the Revenue Accelerator, turned into pages that can rank instead of posts that expire
  • Monthly reporting on which queries you now appear for and which members arrived through them
If the platform goes first

Somewhere to send them

  • Scope agreed and fixed before anything is built
  • Functional beta covering member profiles, the seven cohorts, event registration and the directory
  • The introductions you make by hand become something members can initiate themselves
  • A real application flow, so membership interest stops living in your inbox
  • Built so organic work can be layered on top without a rebuild

Our read, and it is only a read until you tell us what matters most this quarter: the three fixes in section two are worth doing regardless of which path you choose, and they are small. They are not a phase. They are housekeeping that has been outstanding since 2019.
