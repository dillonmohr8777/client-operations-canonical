## Definitions (as used in this package)
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):** Structure content so AI/answer surfaces (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, voice) can extract clear answers and recommend the business.
- **GEO (two coordinated meanings for this program):**
  1. **Generative Engine Optimization** — earn citations/mentions inside generative answers.
  2. **Local geographic optimization** — city/service-area pages, GBP, NAP consistency, geotagged project proof for Pittsburgh suburbs Fagan already serves.

## Painting-specific best practices synthesized

### 1. Answer-first service pages (not one generic “Services” dump)
- Dedicated pages for high-intent services Fagan already offers: interior, exterior, commercial, HOA, residential, office, warehouse.
- Open with a **direct answer in the first ~40–200 words** (who, where, what project types, how to get an estimate).
- Self-contained sections AI can lift: process, prep, timeline expectations (only verified facts), common concerns, estimate path.
- BizIQ documents large organic-lead lifts when painters move to localized service pages vs. single services blobs.

### 2. Conversational FAQ system (AEO core)
- Homeowners ask full questions (“How long does exterior painting take in Pittsburgh summers?”), not 3-word keywords.
- Pair visible Q&A with FAQPage schema only where the same Q&A appears on-page.
- Group FAQs by service + decision stage (research → compare → hire).

### 3. Generative citation signals (GEO)
- Clear entity facts: legal name, service area list, certifications shown on site (Lead-Safe / EPA, BBB A+, licensed & insured), warranty language **exactly as published** (site states two-year warranty).
- Third-party corroboration: review freshness, directory consistency, project proof with real Pittsburgh neighborhoods.
- Prefer **citation-worthiness** (being the source AI quotes) over rank-chasing alone.
- HubSpot: AI-sourced traffic can convert at higher intent when the brand is already explained in the answer.

### 4. Local / geo layer (Pittsburgh)
- Keep unique content on suburb pages Fagan already lists (Fox Chapel, Squirrel Hill, Upper St. Clair, etc.) — no thin doorway swaps.
- Google Business Profile: primary category Painter / Painting Contractor; fresh reviews; original geotagged job photos; service areas aligned to site.
- Internal links: service ↔ area ↔ gallery/project proof ↔ free consultation form.

### 5. Technical extractability
- Crawlable HTML answers (not image-only or JS-only critical copy).
- LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness + Service + FAQ schema where justified.
- robots/AI crawler access check (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, etc.) as a baseline task — do not block answer engines unless legal requires it.
- Sitemap, canonicals, mobile CTA above the fold to free consultation.

### 6. Measurement contract (do not sell vanity rankings)
Weekly / monthly:
- Priority pages specified → implemented → indexed
- Target query set (homeowner language) impressions/clicks
- AI citation checks (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Copilot) for the same queries
- Qualified organic estimate requests / phone intent (with attribution rules already in Fagan operating context)
- Competitor pages winning citations
