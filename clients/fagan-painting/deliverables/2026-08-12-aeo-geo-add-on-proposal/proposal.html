01

AEO: answer-ready pages

Make the highest-intent pages easy for an answer engine to understand and quote.

  • Build and approve a 10-query set covering interior, exterior, multifamily/commercial, HOA, and Pittsburgh-area service intent.
  • Upgrade up to 3 priority service pages with a direct answer in the first 200 words, process/prep/estimate blocks, and stronger internal links.
  • Create up to 15 service-grouped homeowner Q&A pairs and QA visible FAQ, HowTo, Service, and Organization schema where justified.
02

GEO: entity + local trust

Give Google and generative systems one consistent, supportable picture of Fagan.

  • Create an approved entity and facts sheet for services, areas, credentials, warranty language, phone, website, and project proof.
  • Reinforce priority service-area signals across up to 3 local pages, Google Business Profile guidance, and core directory references.
  • Connect service, area, gallery/project proof, and estimate paths without fabricating a street address for this service-area business.
03

Measure: citations + demand

Track whether the work is becoming visible and useful, not just whether a page exists.

  • Baseline and monthly recheck the 10 approved queries in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews when present, and Copilot.
  • Log citations, cited pages, competitors, answer quality, and material changes in a simple scorecard.
  • Report pages specified, implemented, and indexed alongside organic visibility and qualified estimate signals, with pending fields labeled.
