# Fagan Painting AEO, GEO, and SEO transition plan

Status: Initiated July 27, 2026

## Bottom line

Fagan's ongoing Dillon-managed growth lane is now AEO, GEO, and SEO. Facebook management is transitioning to Benson and Legacy Paint Holdings. The organic program will be coordinated with Phil and Mac so answer-engine improvements support the existing SEO direction instead of competing with it.

This record interprets Dillon's “SCO” shorthand as SEO in client-facing and implementation language.

## Outcome

Build a search presence that:

- answers real homeowner and property-manager questions clearly;
- is easy for Google and AI answer systems to crawl, understand, extract, and cite;
- connects service pages, location content, FAQs, project proof, and the estimate path;
- preserves factual accuracy and does not invent pricing, service radius, guarantees, timing, credentials, or project outcomes;
- measures qualified organic demand, not rankings or traffic in isolation.

## First implementation lane

### 1. Organic and AI-citation baseline

Owner: Dillon / Marketing Chief  
Target: July 29, 2026

- Inventory live service, location, FAQ, and project pages.
- Record current indexed-page and organic-query baselines from authorized sources.
- Check robots.txt for GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, and other relevant AI crawlers.
- Check sitemap, canonicals, redirects, page rendering, and priority schema.
- Establish a ten-query monitoring set using verified customer language and approved service areas. Do not publish inferred targets as final until Phil and Mac review them.
- Test the approved query set in ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews when present, and Copilot; log citations and cited competitors.

### 2. Answer-ready page map

Owner: Dillon / Marketing Chief  
Coordination: Phil and Mac  
Target: August 2, 2026

- Select the first priority service and location pages from live demand, existing SEO work, and business fit.
- Give each priority page one clear core answer in the first 200 words.
- Add self-contained sections for project fit, process, preparation, common concerns, and estimate expectations using only verified business facts.
- Map internal links among related service, location, project, and estimate pages.
- Identify duplicate, thin, or overlapping pages for coordination before any structural change.

### 3. Homeowner FAQ system

Owner: Dillon / Marketing Chief  
Coordination: Phil and Mac  
Target: August 5, 2026

- Build question inventory from real lead feedback, estimate conversations, Search Console queries, and approved client knowledge.
- Draft concise direct answers that can stand alone when extracted.
- Group FAQs by service and decision stage instead of adding one generic sitewide block.
- Specify FAQPage schema only where the visible page contains the same questions and answers.
- Flag every answer that needs client confirmation before publication.

### 4. First content and schema sprint

Owner: Dillon / Marketing Chief  
SEO review: Phil and Mac  
Target: August 9, 2026

- Deliver the first optimized service-page specification with definition block, direct answers, step structure where useful, FAQ pairs, internal links, and schema requirements.
- Add named sources and years to any market statistics; omit unsupported statistics.
- Use approved project proof, authorship, and expert attribution where available.
- Validate JSON-LD and ensure important answer content is present in crawlable HTML.
- Keep publication behind verified WordPress access, exact-page review, and the applicable approval boundary.

## Measurement contract

Track weekly:

- priority pages audited, specified, implemented, and indexed;
- technical blockers affecting crawlability or AI-bot access;
- target queries with organic impressions and clicks;
- target queries with observed AI citations, including cited page and position when available;
- qualified organic estimate requests and verified downstream outcomes;
- competitor pages winning citations for the monitored questions.

Do not claim AEO or SEO success from a single ranking, citation, impression, or form. Report the baseline, implementation state, and verified movement over consistent windows.
