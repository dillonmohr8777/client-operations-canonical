# Product context

## Product

`Love, Philadelphia — The Podcast` sponsor landing page for Pritzker Law Group.

## Purpose

Create a launch-ready editorial home for a Philadelphia-focused podcast and make Pritzker Law Group's premium sponsorship unmistakable without turning the page into a law-firm advertisement.

## Primary audiences

- Philadelphia-area listeners discovering the show.
- Business, civic, development, and community audiences already familiar with Pritzker Law Group.
- Internal stakeholders reviewing the show identity and sponsor treatment before launch.

## Primary outcomes

1. Understand the show's Philadelphia-first premise.
2. Recognize Pritzker Law Group as premium sponsor.
3. Visit the sponsor's official site or Rachael Pritzker's official profile.
4. Leave with a clear sense that listening links and episode information are still coming.

## Confirmed truth

- Show name: `Love, Philadelphia — The Podcast`.
- Premium sponsor: Pritzker Law Group.
- Pritzker Law Group works in Philadelphia real estate, development, and government relations.
- Rachael J. Pritzker is identified by the firm as CEO and founder.

## Unknowns and launch gates

- Final show positioning and approved copy.
- Host and guest lineup.
- Release date and episode schedule.
- Podcast listening destinations.
- Production hostname and CMS.
- Final CTA and conversion destination.
- Analytics, consent, and form handling.

No unknown may be represented as confirmed.
