# Design direction

## Surface mode

Persuade.

## Visual world

The selected direction is `Civic Poster Wall`: a Philadelphia campaign-poster system with midnight navy fields, warm paper, civic red, Liberty Bell gold, compressed block lettering, and a five-panel opening composition. The supplied podcast identity is the central artifact. Bold crops, hard color fields, fine metallic rules, and torn-paper transitions evoke a real poster wall without generic skyline stock imagery.

### Tokens

- Navy: `#07162c`
- Navy light: `#102746`
- Civic red: `#d6302f`
- Red dark: `#a81f25`
- Poster paper: `#f1eadb`
- Paper ink: `#12213a`
- Bell gold: `#c89a3d`
- White: `#fffdf7`
- Display type: condensed system poster stack built from `Impact`, `Haettenschweiler`, and `Arial Narrow Bold`
- Body type: `Arial`, `Helvetica`, and system sans serif
- Corners: square poster panels; controls use no more than `4px`
- Depth: offset print-like shadow, never glow

## Interaction

- A compact sticky navigation anchors the show, premise, and sponsor story.
- Primary actions lead only to verified external destinations.
- Listening-platform controls remain visibly unavailable until real destinations are supplied.
- Motion is one orchestrated first-viewport poster assembly with subtle focus and hover responses.

## Content rules

- Sponsor prominence is clear but secondary to the show identity.
- No invented guests, episodes, launch dates, audience metrics, legal outcomes, or awards.
- Editorial copy may establish mood but cannot imply unverified programming.
- The legal disclaimer remains visible in the footer.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop uses a split editorial hero with the show identity opposite launch copy.
- Mobile collapses to a single reading column with the podcast mark first.
- Sponsor details and actions remain readable at 200% zoom and narrow widths.
