Our Story

We started Green Horizon Farms in 2018 with one simple idea: cannabis deserves the same craft and care as fine wine. No shortcuts. No bulk fillers. Just small batches grown with intention.

Every plant gets daily attention from seed to harvest. We obsess over terpene profiles, cure times, and consistency. Every batch is third-party tested. If it doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't leave our facility. 🌿
