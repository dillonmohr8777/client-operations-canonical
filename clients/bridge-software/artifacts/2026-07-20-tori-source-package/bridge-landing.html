Getting started

Simple. Verified. Safe.

Three steps to being on Bridge — whether you're a person or a business.

1

Verify your identity

Personal accounts confirm 21+ age. Business accounts verify their EIN against IRS records and provide a state license number. No anonymous profiles allowed.

2

Build your profile

Consumers build a personal page. Businesses get a public brand page and a private B2B view — one profile, two audiences, one place to manage everything.

3

Connect & promote

Consumers follow brands and discover deals. Businesses post product drops and promotions to their followers and the state-specific news feed. No spam. No noise.

The Bridge news feed

A promotional feed.
Not a comment section.

Bridge's feed is a curated, graphic-forward space for cannabis businesses to promote themselves — not a forum, not a gossip platform, not a popularity contest.

✅
New product drops
Businesses announce new strains, products, or lines with photos and details. Consumers and followers see it immediately.
✅
Deals & promotions
Weekly specials, discounts, limited-time offers — posted as graphics. State-compliant. Age-gated. Targeted to your market.
✅
Brand announcements
Grand openings, new locations, awards, harvest events, brand milestones — the things that matter to your community.
✅
Graphics only
Every post requires a visual. Bridge is a design-forward platform — your brand image is your voice.
🚫
No comments or likes
Bridge is not a popularity contest. No comment sections, no like counts, no follower tallies. Just clean, professional promotion.
🚫
No personal posts in the feed
The news feed is for verified businesses only. Consumer personal profiles are separate from the promotional feed.
🚫
No forums, questions, or debates
This is not a Reddit or Facebook Group. Bridge is an informational platform — not a gossip hub, not a rumor mill.
🚫
No targeting minors
All posts are subject to state cannabis advertising compliance. Businesses agree to platform content policies and confirm their posts comply with local law at time of posting.
Your safety is the product

Built with protection
at every layer.

Bridge does not sell data, does not serve ads to third parties, and does not allow anonymous accounts. Here's how we protect you.

🔒

End-to-end encryption

All business messages are end-to-end encrypted. No one — including Bridge — can read your conversations.

🪪

Full identity verification

21+ age gate for all users. EIN + state license verification for businesses. No anonymous accounts — ever.

🚫

Zero data sales

Bridge is subscription-funded. We will never sell, broker, or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties.

🛡️

State-compliant content

Business posts require a compliance acknowledgment confirming content meets state cannabis advertising laws. Posts are reviewed before going live.

🔐

Granular privacy controls

Personal users control who sees their profile — public, followers only, or fully private. Your data, your choice.

🏪

Storefront-only messaging

Only licensed storefront businesses can initiate conversations. Protects managers from unsolicited outreach and sales spam.

Audit-ready logs
