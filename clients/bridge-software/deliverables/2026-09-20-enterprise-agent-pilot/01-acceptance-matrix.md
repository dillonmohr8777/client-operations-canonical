## Status key and release position

- **Verified** — the cited evidence directly demonstrates the bounded claim in
  the named environment. It is not, by itself, client acceptance.
- **Partial** — one side of the contract or one environment is evidenced, but a
  required dependency, live bind, or acceptance proof is missing.
- **Blocked** — the next proof cannot safely run until the named dependency or
  approval exists.
- **Unverified** — the requirement is in scope, but no reliable evidence of its
  implementation or acceptance was found.

**Release position:** Milestone 3 remains **in progress**. Steps 2 and 4 are
explicitly **not client-approved**. No dated formal client acceptance was found;
formal acceptance is **pending**.
