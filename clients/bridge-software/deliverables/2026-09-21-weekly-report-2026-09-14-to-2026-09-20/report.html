Sep 20Verified work / decision

Typography fault found and fixed at the source

The font setup was causing the browser to synthesise fake bold text in roughly forty five places instead of using the real bold weight. It was confirmed by measuring rendered character widths rather than judged by eye, and fixed by changing how the font weight is declared.

Synthesised bold looks subtly wrong on every screen and no amount of design review catches it reliably, because it is a rendering fault rather than a styling choice.

Confirmed by rendered width measurement, fixed Sep 20
