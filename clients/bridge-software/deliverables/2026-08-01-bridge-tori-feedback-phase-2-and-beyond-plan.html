What I heard · 1 of 2

Tori’s product and design direction.

Brand and visual character

  • Keep purple and the dark foundation. It feels right and is easy to stay in.
  • Keep the Bridge and signal language, plus the strongest network positioning.
  • Add imagery, animation, hover behavior, motion, and more visual energy.
  • Reduce the hero typography’s dominance so supporting product value gets read.
  • Remove language that rejects metrics; Bridge should help users understand and improve marketing performance.

Navigation and information architecture

  • Make the primary destinations obvious: profile or home, Explore, Create, and Community News or News Feed.
  • Rename “Exchange” to “Explore.”
  • Consider “My Profile” or “Home Base” for the business home.
  • Simplify search and introduce clear category pathways.
  • Prototype a Pinterest or newspaper-style feed against a simpler vertical feed before locking the pattern.

Creation and audience controls

  • Support uploads of existing PNG, PDF, and image assets.
  • Add Promotion as a clear content type.
  • Allow one post to reach multiple approved audiences.
  • Separate public promotion from protected wholesale pricing and business-only information.
  • Preserve the strengths of the current Create experience while making it more practical.

Profiles and verification

  • Show current sales and accounting contacts directly on business profiles.
  • Prompt organizations to re-verify contact information regularly.
  • Define public, consumer, professional, retailer, and EIN-verified business access.
  • Decide how vendor-to-vendor visibility works without pretending competitive information can be perfectly hidden.
  • Broaden the verified directory to qualified service providers across the cannabis ecosystem.
Bridge · Tori’s product direction03
What I heard · 2 of 2

Network, commercial, and launch implications.

Nationwide discovery

Bridge should connect legal markets across the country. State, location, product, category, brand, strain, and favorites filters should help people see national movement without overwhelming the default feed.

Consumer utility

Dispensary profiles should connect people to live menus, ordering, directions, and relevant social or marketplace channels. The point is to make discovery useful and support real business outcomes.

Industry directory

The network can include qualified banks, transporters, testing labs, manufacturers, construction teams, HVAC providers, electricians, and other industry services, with appropriate credentials and references.

Revenue hypothesis

The conversation explored an early brand price near $349 to $350 per month, an introductory free period, annual commitment, and grandfathered pricing. These are testable hypotheses, not final commercial terms.

Two-sided launch

Brands and businesses must be recruited while consumer and industry awareness grows through events and street-level activation. The launch plan should avoid an empty-network problem on either side.

Later-stage HR

A future HR-support concept may create value, but employee handbooks, advice, privacy, role access, and legal exposure make it inappropriate for the MVP without a dedicated legal and operating model.

Sequencing principle: build the trusted network and its repeatable connection loops first. Test monetization and acquisition with evidence. Keep legally complex expansion concepts in a governed future backlog.

Product North Star after Tori’s feedback

Bridge becomes the verified, nationwide operating network where cannabis businesses, professionals, and consumers can discover the right people and products, publish to the right audiences, protect sensitive information, and move from signal to useful action.
