Why the larger feed is the right prototype move.

Tori asked for more pictures and less reading, a useful news feed, flexible post types, multiple audiences, favorites, and a nationwide network that stays manageable.

Directly answered

  • More pictures and a denser editorial rhythm.
  • Promotion as a distinct content type.
  • Product introductions and vendor announcements.
  • Events, education, industry news, and service availability.
  • Adult, retailer, verified-business, professional, and all-member audiences.
  • State, category, and favorites controls.

Deliberately not invented

  • Real operator activity or live market statistics.
  • Medical or treatment claims.
  • Paid placement policy.
  • Contributor and moderation authority.
  • Verification promises or protected-data rules.
  • Production ordering, menus, maps, or social integrations.
Bridge visual direction

The expanded image system uses one strong subject, asymmetric editorial cropping, restricted color, and visible print texture. Bridge retains its Connected purple and near-black identity, uses realistic cannabis-industry scenes, and applies a reusable static raster grain layer so the source photographs stay crisp.
