Contract Start, Investment & Phase Billing

complete. Once the agreement has started, Momentum Digital will begin the project according to the
phase-based structure outlined below.

each. Each billing cycle is a prepayment for the applicable phase of work.

prepayment has been received. Phases 2 through 5 do not begin until the previous phase has been

Client Ownership, Access & Rights

Upon payment for the approved project phases, the client will have full accessibility, ownership, and rights
to the completed software project, including the code, tools, software environment, and related project
assets developed specifically for the client.

Momentum Digital will provide the client with appropriate access to the project environment, including
relevant code repositories, software tools, hosting environment, admin accounts, documentation, and
related systems used to develop and manage the project.

The client will have the right to use, manage, modify, expand, and continue developing the software after
completion. Momentum Digital will not intentionally restrict the client from accessing, owning, or
transferring the project, provided all approved phase payments have been completed.

Software Confidentiality & Non-Resale

Momentum Digital agrees that this software project is being developed specifically for the client.
Momentum Digital will not share, sell, license, resell, publicly distribute, or commercially reproduce the
client's custom software product for another business or third party.

Momentum Digital may retain general knowledge, development experience, reusable internal processes,
and non-confidential technical skills learned during the project, but Momentum Digital will not resell the
client's proprietary software, confidential workflows, private business logic, or custom project assets.

support actually begin. The 24-month period does not begin when this agreement is signed.

Mutual NDA, Confidentiality & Non-Compete Protections

 Both parties agree to share and execute a mutual NDA and confidentiality agreement to protect private
 business information, software details, proprietary processes, technical documentation, client data,
 development strategy, and any sensitive materials exchanged during the project.
 Both parties also agree to mutual non-compete, non-solicitation, and non-circumvention protections where
 legally permissible and appropriate. These protections are intended to prevent either party from using
 confidential information, proprietary software, project strategy, client data, or private business
 relationships from this project to unfairly compete with or harm the other party.
 The final NDA, non-compete, non-solicitation, and non-circumvention terms should be reviewed and
 approved by both parties and their legal counsel before signing where appropriate.

       Purpose of mutual protections

       Momentum Digital does not share, resell, or repurpose the client's custom software.
