1. Platform Overview
The VACE platform is a purpose-built client management system designed specifically around VA Claims' advisory business. Its core purpose is to give David Fisher complete situational awareness over every active client relationship - who is progressing, who is stuck, whose court the ball is in, and who may owe money.
This is not a generic CRM. Every feature in this document was designed around the specific way VA Claims operates.

3. The 11-Step VA Claims Process
Every client moves through the following 11 steps. The platform tracks exactly which step each client is in, how long they have been there, and whether the hold-up is on the team's side or the client's side.

Step
Name
Description
1
Initial Intake
Client signs up and enters their name, email, and phone. Profile is created.
2
Document Collection
Client gathers and submits medical records and prior VA award letters.
3
Records Review
Team reviews submitted records to assess the client's case.
4
Nexus Letter
Team works with medical professionals to obtain a nexus letter linking condition to service.
5
DBQ Completion
Disability Benefits Questionnaire is completed by a qualified medical provider.
6
Claim Submission
The completed claim package is submitted to the VA.
7
VA Review
VA is reviewing the submitted claim - client and team await decision.
8
C&P Exam Prep
Team prepares client for the Compensation & Pension exam.
9
Exam Complete
Client has completed their C&P exam. Results timer may be started here.
10
Decision Review
VA decision has been received. Team reviews the rating and advises next steps.
11
Case Closed
Process complete. Client has received their rating increase and payments are settled.

4. Core Features
4.1 Dashboard
The main screen David sees when he logs in. Designed for immediate situational awareness without clicking through menus.
Stat cards showing: total active clients, clients at risk (30+ days), clients stuck (60+ days), completed YTD
Progress pipeline - visual bar showing how many clients are in each of the 11 steps
Client list table with columns: Name, Advisor, Step, Days in Step, Onus, Status
Color coding - red for stuck, amber for at risk, green for active
Click any step in the pipeline to filter the client list by that step
Filter tabs - All, Stuck, Waiting on Us, Waiting on Client
Add Client button to create new profiles instantly

4.2 Client Detail Panel
Slides in from the right when any client row is clicked. Shows everything about one client in one place.
Step progress bar - visual representation of completed vs remaining steps
Client details: days in step, onus, last contact, status
Advance Step button - moves client to next step, resets days counter
Toggle Onus button - switches between 'Waiting on Us' and 'Waiting on Client'
Notes section - add and view interaction logs and notes
Timeline - chronological history of all events and interactions

4.3 All Clients Page
A dedicated page for viewing, searching, and managing the full client list.
Search by client name
Filter by status (active, at-risk, stuck)
Filter by onus (waiting on us, waiting on client)
Filter by step (any of the 11 steps)
Sort by days in step, name, or step number
All filters can be combined and cleared

4.4 Alerts Page
A dedicated page showing every client that needs attention, sorted by urgency.
Critical alerts - clients 60+ days in current step
Warning alerts - clients 30-59 days in current step
Action needed alerts - onus on team for 14+ days
Filter by alert type
Click any alert to open the client detail panel and take action
Summary cards showing counts per alert type

4.5 Advisors Page
Manage the advisory team - add, view stats, and remove advisors.
Advisor cards showing: name, role, total clients, at-risk count, stuck count
Add new advisor with name, email, and role
Remove advisor with confirmation - clients remain in system pending reassignment

4.6 AI Assistant
Natural language interface for querying the entire client database without navigating filters.
Ask questions in plain English and receive intelligent, data-grounded answers
Pre-call briefings - ask for a summary of any client before a call
Advisor queries - 'Who is Donnie's most at-risk client?'
Gap detection - 'Which clients haven't been contacted in 30+ days?'
Suggestion chips for common queries
Full conversation history within each session

4.7 Payment Watch (Phase 3 Feature)
Tracks clients in the post-exam window when VA rating decisions are expected and payments may be owed to VA Claims.
'Start Results Timer' button on each client profile - begins counting from that date
Two client groups are monitored:
60-120 days post-exam - likely received rating, payment probably due
121+ days post-exam - outliers requiring special attention
'Mark as Paid' button removes client from the Payment Watch list
Clients re-enter the list if they go through the process again and the timer is restarted
Dedicated Payment Watch page separate from the main dashboard

4.8 Settings Page
Admin-configurable settings without requiring code changes.
Notification thresholds per step group - set how many days before an alert fires
Admin email address for receiving notifications
Future: additional notification channels

5. Onus Tracking Logic
One of the most unique features of the VACE platform. Every client is always in one of two states:

State
Meaning
Display Color
Waiting on Us
The team needs to take the next action to move this client forward
Blue
Waiting on Client
The client needs to take the next action before the team can proceed
Amber

The onus flag is manually toggled by the admin or advisor when the responsibility shifts. Alerts are generated when onus has been on the team for 14+ days without action.

6. Notification System
The automated notification engine runs on a daily schedule and proactively alerts David when clients breach time thresholds - so he doesn't have to check the dashboard manually every day.

6.1 Notification Types
Critical alert - client has been in current step for 60+ days
Warning alert - client has been in current step for 30+ days
Team action needed - onus has been on the team for 14+ days
Payment Watch entry - client enters the 60-day post-exam window
Payment Watch escalation - client crosses 121 days post-exam

6.2 Delivery
Daily email digest sent to the admin email address
In-app alerts page always reflects current status
Thresholds are configurable per step group in the Settings page
